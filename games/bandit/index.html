SORTIE 1

HOW TO FLY

  • Left thumb flies. Push it the way you want the nose to go. Push further to turn harder; hard turns cost speed.
  • Lock on. Get behind the bandit and keep him inside your ring until it turns red. A lock only takes from behind him.
  • Right thumb: FIRE. With a lock, FIRE sends a missile. The closer you are behind him, the less time he has to shake it. Up close, hold FIRE for the cannon.
  • He drops flares. A flare can pull your missile away. He needs a moment before he can drop another: a second missile straight after the first often gets through.
  • When he is behind you a mirror opens at the top. He can only lock on from behind you. Beeping means he is doing it: turn hard to slip out of his sights.
  • MISSILE! Turn hard, and tap FLARE as the ring closes on the button. Too early or too late and it stays on you. Flares come back slowly, so do not waste them.
  • Shoot down every bandit before your fuel runs out to unlock the next sortie. Take no hits for three stars.
