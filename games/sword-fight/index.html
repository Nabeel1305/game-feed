Sir Knight

HOW TO FIGHT

  1. Your sword follows your finger. Swipe in any direction and your blade cuts that way. Tap anywhere on him to poke him right there: the helm hurts most.
  2. Watch his blade. When he winds up, his sword is drawn back on the side the blow will come from, and it gleams. A thrust points at one spot on you.
  3. Block by striking. No shield: swipe across his blade's path on the side it's coming from. Against a thrust, swipe through the spot he's aiming at. Meet it at the last moment for a perfect parry and he's wide open for a riposte.
  4. Pull straight down toward yourself to step back out of reach. It costs stamina.
  5. Don't flail. Swinging nonstop doesn't block anything, and repeating the same move lets him read you and punish you.
