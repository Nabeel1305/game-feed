HOW TO FIGHT

  1. Watch his blade. When he winds up, his sword gleams on the side it's coming from: your left, your right, over the top, or a thrust at your chest.
  2. Block by striking. No shield: swing into his attack at the right moment. Swipe left or right toward the side it's coming from, up for an overhead chop, and tap to knock a thrust aside. A last-moment clash is a perfect parry and leaves him wide open.
  3. Attack. Swipe across him to slash. Tap him to poke, quick but light. Hit him while he's off balance for big damage.
  4. Swipe down to step back out of reach. It dodges anything but costs stamina.
  5. Watch your stamina. Swing wildly and you tire, and he'll read you, parry you and punish you.
