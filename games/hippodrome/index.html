HOW TO RACE

  • Left thumb: the reins. Slide left or right to steer your team.
  • Right thumb: the whip. Tap to crack it. Tap just as the ring closes, in time with the gallop, for a PERFECT crack: more speed and fresher horses.
  • Mind their wind. Whip too often, or off the beat, and the horses are blown and slow right down.
  • The turn. Hold your right thumb down to rein in before the post, steer round with your left, then let go and whip away. Too fast and you slide out to the wall. Too tight and you strike the post.
  • The inside wins. The closer you pass the post, the faster the turn, and the bigger the risk. Rivals swing a little wide on the way in: get close behind one, turn tighter, and you take the inside.
  • The start. Three trumpet notes, then GO. Crack the whip right on GO. Too early and your team rears.
  • Finish near the front to unlock the next race. First place wins the wreath.
