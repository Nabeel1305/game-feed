HOW TO RACE

  • Left thumb: the reins. Slide left or right to steer.
  • Right thumb: the whip. Tap just as the ring closes, in time with the gallop, for a PERFECT crack: more speed, fresher horses. Whip too often or off the beat and the horses are blown.
  • The turn. Hold your right thumb to rein in before the post, steer round with your left, then let go and whip away. Too fast and you slide out to the wall. Too tight and you strike the post.
  • The inside wins. The closer to the post, the faster the turn. Rivals swing wide on the way in: get close behind one and turn tighter to pass.
  • Ram. Swerve hard into a rival who is level with you. Harder hits break more, and more still against a wall. Break their chariot and they are out. Each blow costs yours a little.
  • Dodge. Rough rivals ram you too. The screen edge glows red on their side before they swing: steer away. Against the wall you cannot, so keep some room. If your chariot is destroyed, your race is over.
  • The start. Three trumpet notes, then GO: crack the whip right on GO.
  • Finish near the front to unlock the next race. First place wins the wreath.
