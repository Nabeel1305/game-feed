SAIL · RAID · BOARD
EXPEDITION 1

How to play

  • Sailing. Slide your left thumb to steer. When the lookout sights a ship: RAID, or SAIL ON to look for another (twice at most).
  • Merchants carry the most loot. Navy ships carry less, fight far harder, and their guns must be beaten before you can board.
  • Your guns fire out of your sides. Bring her into your gold arc and FIRE lights: press it then (a hard turn spoils the aim a little). Fired at nothing, a broadside is wasted, and every wasted one is slower to reload than the last.
  • ROW (above FIRE): hold it for a burst of speed. The crew tire in a few seconds and need a while to get their breath back.
  • Her guns only reach out of her sides: the red. It fills before she fires. Keep out of it: get close on her bow or her stern and turn faster than she can. Sail straight and she sits where your guns cannot reach.
  • Boarding. Block his blow: one swipe across his blade, out on the side it comes from. Or attack first: hammer his sword two or three times and his guard breaks (hit him, hit him). He may answer straight back, so be ready to block. Just after a broken guard he is set: strike him then and he cuts you, harder each time.
  • Then strike while he is open: swipe to slice, tap to stab. A late block, as his blade turns bright, throws him off balance: stab, stab, stab.
  • Dodge a shot, a red blow, or a blow your block missed: pull straight down, low on the screen. A dodge saves you; only a block opens him.
  • Pistol: hold it, drag the sights onto his head or heart, let go. One ball at a time: hold it again for four seconds to reload. Three balls.
  • The bar at the top is the fight. Fight well and your crew takes the ship. Fight badly and they break.
