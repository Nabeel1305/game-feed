How to play

  1. Set up. Each side gets 11 caps. Drag yours into any formation in your half, then tap Ready.
  2. Flick. On your turn, tap one of your caps, then pull back from anywhere on the screen and let go. The further you pull, the harder it flies.
  3. Knock them off. Hit the other side's caps off the long sides of the stool. A cap that falls off is out.
  4. Mind the line. If your cap stops past the line at the other end, they capture it. If they've lost caps, it comes back as one of theirs.
  5. The legs. Once a cap is completely past either line, it can't fall off: the players' legs are in the way.
  6. Win. Knock off all of the other side's caps.

Orange wins!
