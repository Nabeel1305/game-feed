HOW TO DUEL

  1. Hand on your holster. Put your thumb on the holster at the bottom right and keep it there through the count.
  2. Wait for "DRAW!" Move or let go before it and you've jumped the gun. Higher levels try to fake you out.
  3. Draw and aim. On "DRAW!", slide your thumb up. A crosshair appears just above your thumb so you can see what you're aiming at.
  4. Let go to shoot. A headshot wins the round. A body shot wounds him and slows him down, so you can shoot again.
  5. Win the duel. Take the rounds before he does. Each level is faster, sharper and harder to hit.
