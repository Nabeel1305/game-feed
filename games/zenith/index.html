HOW TO SAVE THE DAY

  • Calls for help come in one after another. Tap to fly there and steer through the gold rings on the way.
  • CATCH! People are falling. Slide your finger left and right to fly under them.
  • ZAP! Hold your finger on a threat to burn it with your laser eyes. Don't overheat, and don't hit the helicopters.
  • FIGHT! Tap bad guys before their glowing punch lands. Never tap the scared people.
  • BLOW! Hold on a burning window to blow the fire out. Watch your breath.
  • LIFT! Hold to lift, and slide left or right to keep it level.
  • DODGE & ZAP! Flick left or right to get out of the red zones. When the glowing core opens, hold your laser on it.
  • Fail a mission and the city loses a little trust. Lose all three hearts and the patrol ends. Finish a patrol to unlock the next.
