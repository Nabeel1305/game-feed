# 43. Ghost Lantern

## The idea
My pick for set 7, the spooky one (intensity 4): a haunted house at night, a lantern, ghosts that show only in its
light. 3D, because the beam sweeping a dark room (a real spotlight, furniture throwing shadows, rooms black until you
step in) is the whole thrill.

## How it plays (one thumb, one floating stick)
Drag a little: you turn on the spot and the lantern turns with you. Drag further: you walk. Ghosts are seen only in the
beam (and as a faint shimmer near you). Hold a ghost in the light, standing still, and a ring fills as the lantern pulls
it in (walking sways the lantern: a third of the pull). Let it slip out and it recovers. Ghosts are not fools: in front of
you they slide round to your side and back before coming in; frost creeps in at the edge of the screen toward one close
behind. A ghost that reaches you scares you (a heart). Some hide in the furniture until you walk into their room (it
rattles first). The lantern burns oil all the time; jars of oil stand in some rooms. Catch every ghost on the floor
before the oil runs out. After a while the whole house stirs and ghosts come through the walls.

## Levels
Two rooms at level 1 up to a 3 x 3 house; 4 ghosts rising to 14. Kinds join as you go: wisps, shy ghosts (level 3: they
move only when you look away), quick zigzagging ones (5), big ones that take long and pull hard (8), and from level 4 a
golden ghost now and then that runs from you (6 coins). Four houses (the old manor, the doll house, the flooded cellar,
the clock tower). Help after three losses: slower ghosts, less oil burned, an easier catch.

## Systems
The ghost book (every kind caught, with how many); the Lamp Shop (Brighter flame, Wider glass, Oil flask, Lucky charm);
ranks from Scaredy cat to Master of the manor; the map in the corner (rooms seen, jars found).

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind move `d`: the stick dragged some random way (a little or a lot), or let go.
- A thumb that does not look gets crept up on from behind and walks while the beam should be still: 576 blind-string
  games and 480 random ones, none won. Ordinary robots win the first levels every time, about 85% at 10-20.

## Lessons
1. LIGHT IT: turn on the spot and hold two wisps in the light. 2. FROST: two ghosts come from behind; turn to the frost.
3. SHY GHOSTS: catch two that move only when you look away. Skip tutorial on every lesson; Tutorial on home and pause.

## Engagement factors chosen
P3 honest danger (frost toward a ghost close behind, a heartbeat, the shimmer); P18 the catch ring filling and each catch a
note higher; P7 tension and release (dark rooms, rattling furniture, BOO!); P22 the ghost book; P14 the near goal
(ghosts caught of total, the oil bar).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: drag to turn, further to walk; light a ghost and hold it.
- L2: the beam sweeping, the whistle rising as the ring fills, the pop when it is pulled in.
- L3: every ghost is warned (shimmer, frost, heartbeat): a scare is a ghost you did not turn to.
- L4: ghosts caught of the total, the oil bar, the map.
- L5: your best oil to spare on each level; stars.
- L6: shy, quick, big and golden ghosts; four houses; hidden ghosts; bigger houses.
- L7: chase the golden ghost or keep your oil; walk to the jar now or later.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the ghost book; your lamp upgrades.
- L10: your best oil to spare on the home card.

## The card clip
A big ghost bursting out of a rattling wardrobe, the beam swinging onto it, the ring filling, the pop.
