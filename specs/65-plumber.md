# 65. Plumber

## The idea (set 12: his idea)
His words (19 Nov 2026): "you're a plumber. People come with... problems with pressure, problems with no water. Just make a
long list of all the things that plumbers have to come and fix. So you have to go to different people's houses and you have
to fix their water." 2D on purpose: a house cut open like a doll's house shows every pipe, tap, valve and boiler at once,
which is the whole puzzle (where is it really wrong?).

## How it plays (one thumb)
A level is a working day, 9 to 5. Calls come in on the phone: who, what they say is wrong, the pay, the drive, some urgent.
Tap one: the van drives there, and the house is cut open in front of you, the customer standing by the trouble.
- **Look:** tap a part of the house: the plumber walks over and looks (a moment); the card says what he finds. What the
  customer says is not always where it is wrong: a weak shower can be the stopcock half shut or the pressure valve set low;
  a smell in the bathroom is a bird's nest in the roof vent; no water at all is the stopcock, or a frozen pipe by the meter.
- **Fix:** pick the fix from the part's card. The wrong one costs time and the customer's patience (more if done twice).
- **Do it:** each fix is a small hands-on moment, made for what you are doing:
  HOLD a wrench (or the igniter, the torch, the filling loop, the bleed key) and let go in the green, too long cracks it or
  bursts it; PLUNGE by tapping as the ring closes on the circle (the beat changes every push, so a steady tap fails); FEED
  the snake or the auger by holding, and let go when the needle goes red, or it kinks; SET a float, thermostat or pressure
  dial by dragging the knob into the green; FIT a new pipe, hose or cartridge by dragging it into the gap.
- **Floods:** a burst pipe or a split washing machine hose floods the room until you shut the main off at the stopcock;
  a fix tried before the water is off sprays everywhere. A room that fills is a lost job.
- **Pay:** the call's pay, less for a customer who waited too long, a tip and a thank-you note for three stars.

## The jobs (21 kinds, more each day)
Dripping tap (washer), water under the sink (trap nut), blocked sink (plunge), blocked toilet, running toilet (float),
burst pipe (shut off, new section, solder two joints), no water (stopcock shut, or a frozen pipe), weak shower (scale in
the head, stopcock half shut, pressure valve too low), no hot water (pilot out, thermostat down), cold radiator (air, or a
shut valve), boiler low pressure, banging pipes (pressure too high), washing machine flood (new hose, tighten), smelly drains
(roof vent blocked), sewage backing up (roots: auger), slow shower drain (hair: snake), split outside tap (shut off, new
section), dripping shower (shut off, new cartridge), spluttering tap (aerator), washing machine won't drain (filter), frozen
outside tap (thaw).

## Levels
Day 1 starts with taps and sinks; each day adds a new kind of job (and shows it more often that day); targets, the pace of
calls, the patience of customers, the speed of floods and the width of every green rise with the days. Every tenth day is a
busy day: four calls at once, a longer day, a bigger target. Four towns. Help after three losses: a longer day, wider greens.

## Systems
The day's money against a target, the clock, calls that wait only so long (urgent ones less), drive times, customer patience
and stars, tips, three stars a day (happy customers, time to spare), coins, the van's upgrades (torque wrench: wider greens;
pipe camera: no waiting to look; faster van; wet-vac: slower floods), ranks from Apprentice to Legend of the pipes, and the
thank-you notes from three-star customers kept on the van wall.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `c` tap a call, `f` tap a part, `o` tap a fix, `h` press, `r` let go, `d` drag the knob somewhere, `x` drop
  a part somewhere.
- Blind play picks wrong fixes (each costs patience, more and more), lets go outside the green, plunges off the beat (the
  beat changes every push), holds the snake into kinks, and floods out. Measured: 77,504 blind strings and 480 random games,
  won 0 (blind earns about $10 of a $590 day).
- Lessons: any wrong fix, any slip in the hands-on part, or a flood starts the lesson job again.

## Lessons
1. A DRIPPING TAP: look at the kitchen tap, NEW WASHER, hold and let go in the green. Twice.
2. PLUNGE IT: the blocked toilet, PLUNGE, tap on the ring.
3. BURST PIPE!: shut off the stopcock first, fit a new section, solder both joints.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P6 every input answers (the wrench ratchets, the needle swings, the plunger shlups a note higher each push); P14 the near
goal (the day's money and its bar, the clock, the job's checklist); P4 fair, readable failure (the look says what is wrong;
a flood shows itself rising; patience is a bar); P15 nested goals (the job, the day, three stars, the van, ranks, notes);
P26 variety (21 kinds of job, more each day).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: tap a call, tap a part, pick the fix: one line tells you each, and the lessons do one of each.
- L2: the wrench, the plunger and the snake each feel and sound like the real thing; the till rings.
- L3: every fault shows itself (drips, puddles, spray, a frosted pipe, a cold radiator, a nest), and the look says what it is.
- L4: the job's checklist, the day's money bar, the clock.
- L5: best day's money per level on the top bar and the end card; patience stars per job.
- L6: a new kind of job each day; the same complaint with different causes.
- L7: which call: the urgent one far away for big pay, or the near small one; look first or guess the fix.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the van's upgrades, the thank-you notes on the van wall, your rank.
- L10: your best day on every level.

## Sound (S1-S11)
A soft two-tone phone ring, the van, footsteps, a found-it chime, the ratchet while holding, a plunger that steps up the
scale each good push, a snake rattle and a twang for a kink, a crack for over-tightening, a gentle "bonk" for a wrong fix,
a warble for a flood, and the till: brighter for three stars.

## The card clip
A burst pipe: the room filling, the stopcock turned off, a new section dropped in, two joints soldered, +$150 and three
stars.
