# 84. Roach Run

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His words (20 Nov 2026, idea 12, full text in `specs/his-list-2026-11-20.md`): "play as a cockroach searching for food inside a
house. Each round, leave your crack in the wall, find food and return safely while escaping falling slippers and bug spray."
With his directions for the view, controls, the round, food choices, slippers, spray, decisions, progression, sound and
success. Built from them point by point (below). 3D, as he asked: the world from a cockroach's height is the game.

## Research (20 Nov 2026; I cannot play these myself, so I studied reviews, store pages and guides)
- Cockroach Simulator (Steam, phones): free roaming in a kitchen; players like the scale of the room and dodging the human,
  and complain that there is little to do after a few minutes. (store pages, user reviews)
- Bugsnax / Grounded (backyard at bug scale): the giant-world feeling comes from a low camera, huge everyday objects and
  muffled human noise; Grounded's reviewers praise how the scale makes ordinary things into landmarks. (reviews)
- Stealth games on phones (Hitman GO, Sneaky Sasquatch): a clear "how much have they noticed me" meter, safe places that
  are obviously safe, and warnings before every hit, so a loss always feels fair. (reviews, guides)
- What I took: a low camera that makes table legs into towers; a visible notice meter (the eye) and obvious cover (under
  furniture you cannot be seen); every threat telegraphed (his shadow and swish, his raised can); and against the "nothing
  to do" complaint, a clear goal each round, choices of food, rooms and an upgrade path.

## His directions, point by point
- View and environment: 3D, an elevated camera just behind and above the roach; furniture at real size in centimetres
  (a table 75 cm high, chair legs, the gap under the cupboards and the fridge are tunnels). Kitchen first (levels 1-4), then
  the bathroom, bedroom and dining room in turn.
- Controls: a floating stick on the left; SPRINT on the right with a stamina ring that refills when you walk, stop or hide;
  food picked up by touching it; a HIDE button that appears only near a hiding place (under the fridge, in a shoe, behind
  the bin, under the bath mat...).
- Basic round: you start inside the crack and the camera looks out of it; the food glows; bring the night's amount home.
  What you carry sits on your back (crumbs heaped up, a biscuit on top).
- Food choices: crumbs are light; big pieces are worth three but slow you; DROP puts one down to escape (and you can pick it
  up again).
- Falling slippers: a shadow that darkens and grows, a rising swish, then the slipper. It is aimed where you were heading,
  so turning away dodges it. It lies there a moment, blocking the way.
- Bug spray: the can comes down from above and aims (the warning), then a cloud along its line that lingers about three
  seconds and blocks the way; there are ways round it. Staying in it makes you cough until you are caught.
- Interesting decisions: big pieces out in the open (and the light), crumbs near cover; the shortest way home is often the
  exposed one.
- Progression: slippers from level 2, big pieces from 3, spray from 4, light from 5, a person walking about from 6, two people
  on later busy nights; more to bring home, less warning, quicker to notice.
- Sound and music: playful sneaking pizzicato that goes quiet while a threat is coming; huge muffled footsteps; tiny
  skitters, crunches, the slap, the rattle and hiss of the can, a cough.
- Success and failure: win by bringing enough home (the stash grows in the mouth of the crack); caught, and TRY AGAIN starts
  the short round at once.

## His rules for every game (20 Nov 2026), kept
- Big controls away from the action (the stick wherever the left thumb lands; buttons in the bottom right).
- Fun within ten seconds: level 1 is three crumbs, about ten seconds.
- One mechanic at a time (above); mistakes said in words ("SPLAT! Watch the shadow...").
- Autosave, pause, separate Music, Sound effects and Vibration (Settings); original house and characters.

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
- Blind moves (BLIND in part2): `m` push the stick a random way (or let go), `s` sprint on or off, `h` the HIDE button.
- Food is spread across the room and has to come back to a 9 cm crack before dawn; people notice a roach running about.

## Lessons of the first three levels
1. RUN, GRAB, GET HOME: three crumbs.
2. DODGE THE SLIPPER: three slippers.
3. HIDE FROM THE SPRAY: live through two sprays.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK: 3 to 5 principles, the sound rules)
- P6 Every input answers back: skitters, crunches, the load on your back, the stamina ring.
- P3 Honest near-miss: CLOSE ONE! when a slipper lands near; the eye shows exactly how much you have been noticed.
- P12 Celebrations scaled: each delivery chimes a step higher; A FEAST for three stars.
- P13 Visible personal best: best coins per night.
- Sound: as above; the music ducks for threats.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1 The glow is food; the crack is home; the shadow is danger.
- L2 One stick, one button.
- L3 Every threat is warned; every loss says what got you.
- L4 The stash bar and the night clock.
- L5 Seconds left before dawn, dodges.
- L6 Four rooms, slippers, spray, light, walking people.
- L7 Big pieces or crumbs, the open or the long way round; upgrades (quick legs, big lungs, strong back, tough shell).
- L8 TRY AGAIN is one tap.
- L9 Eight shell colours, rank, coins.
- L10 Best coins per night.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A slipper's shadow growing over the roach as it sprints with a biscuit on its back, the slap just behind it.
