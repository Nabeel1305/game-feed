# 80. Paint It

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His words (20 Nov 2026): "Painting things with nice noises, make the controls good."

Mine: a calm 3D painting game. Each job is a real thing standing in a garden, a street or by the sea: a fence, a front door,
a beach hut, a shed wall with a heart on it, a birdhouse, a little car, a fishing boat, a mural. A faint pattern shows which
colour goes where. You drag your thumb over it and paint goes on under your thumb with a soft wet roll; you change colour
from the pots and switch between a wide ROLLER and a fine BRUSH. Blue tape round the windows is safe to paint over: at the
end it peels off and leaves crisp edges. Calm (intensity 1), one thumb.

## Research (20 Nov 2026; I cannot play these myself, so I studied reviews, guides and store pages)
- House Paint: painting the walls of houses, one of the most-downloaded calm painting games. (store page, reviews)
- Roller Splat: sells itself on smooth, satisfying paint. (store page)
- Color Maze and similar puzzles: ASMR sounds and haptics are the selling point. (store pages)
- Color Blocks: cubes filled by swiping a paint roller. (store page)
- What I took: painting under your thumb (no aiming, no puzzle in the way), a roller for big areas and a brush for edges,
  sounds that follow the stroke, a note for every finished part, and tape that peels off at the end for the "reveal".

## How it plays (one screen, one thumb)
- Drag over the thing: paint under your thumb (the roller or brush follows it in 3D). Tap a pot (bottom left) to change
  colour; ROLLER and BRUSH (bottom right) to change tool.
- The faint pattern shows each part's colour. Tape (blue) is safe; glass and the edges are not: paint there is a spill
  (the cell just past an edge takes a little paint without it counting, as when you cut in).
- DONE appears at 85% covered (or it finishes itself at 100%); the tape peels and the owner scores it: covered, minus spills.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever (LESSONS rule 0)
- The fence (one colour and its rails), the door (window, tape, a frame colour), the beach hut (stripes, a door, a roof), the
  shed wall with a taped heart, then the birdhouse, the car, the boat and the mural in turn. Stripes get thinner, tape goes
  from level 25 (unless bought), spills cost more and the owner wants more (72 to 97). Six places in turn.

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
- Blind moves (BLIND in part2): `d` a thumb put down anywhere (sometimes on DONE), `m` dragged anywhere, `u` lifted (sometimes
  then a pot). A blind thumb paints the wall, the glass and the wrong colours: 0 of 5,696 blind strings won.

## Lessons of the first three levels
1. DRAG TO PAINT: one plank of the fence, no grass.
2. TWO COLOURS: the beach hut's stripes in their colours.
3. TAPE IS SAFE: the door, with the brush near the window.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK: 3 to 5 principles, the sound rules)
- P21 Tidy-up satisfaction: every plank and stripe finished rings a note; the tape peels to show crisp edges.
- P6 Every input answers back: paint appears under the thumb, the roller turns, the sound follows the stroke's speed.
- P19 No clock: no timer; the owner only asks "All done?" after a long while.
- P13 Visible personal best: your best score on each job.
- P20 Gentle palettes: soft daylight, primer and pastel paints, colour sets to unlock.
- Sound: the roller's wet "shhhk" (louder and brighter as you roll faster), the brush's whisper, a note a step higher for
  each part finished (S5), the pots' clink, the tape's crackle, a gentle plucked tune.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1 Drag to paint: nothing to learn.
- L2 Paint goes on exactly under the thumb, with sound.
- L3 The verdict shows covered and clean, and spills are called as they happen.
- L4 The coverage bar; DONE at 85%; the next job.
- L5 Coverage, spills and the best score per job.
- L6 A new thing to paint every level, with its own shapes and colours.
- L7 Roller or brush; how close to go to the glass; DONE now or touch up.
- L8 PAINT AGAIN is one tap.
- L9 Colour sets (won with stars), rank, coins and upgrades (big roller, fine brush, more tape, a good name) stay.
- L10 Your best on every job.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
The last stripe rolled on and the tape peeling off the beach hut.
