# 51. Rex

## The idea (his, 19 Nov 2026, set 9)
"Play as a T-Rex roaming a fairly large, beautiful 3D world. You run around hunting other dinosaurs for food and fighting rival
T-Rexes." With his tips: prey that behave differently, a T-Rex that feels powerful, visible signals before attacks, places to
discover, tougher prey and rivals over time, hunts between quieter exploring, clear goals. 3D: the valley in front of you is
the game, with weather and light that change by level.

## How it plays (two thumbs)
Left thumb: a floating stick (push to stride, push far to charge, sideways to turn; you turn tightly when slow). Right thumb:
BITE (a short lunge, hits what is in front of the jaws) and ROAR (freezes small prey near you, makes a rival flinch out of its
lunge; it takes time to come back). Your footsteps shake the camera when you charge, and prey hear a charge from much further
than a creep. A ring and an arrow show the nearest thing your goal needs. Hunger falls; eating fills it and heals a little.
- Runners: quick, zigzag away; one bite. Duckbills: herds at the watering hole; bolt together; two bites.
- Spikebacks: armoured; turn their club tail to you as you come; bite only the head. Linger behind and the tail lifts (the
  warning), then swings; after a swing it is slow to turn, so that is when you run round to the head.
- Hornfaces: paw and lower their heads (the warning), then charge in a line. Step aside: if it missed, it is stuck for a
  moment and you can bite its side. If it hit you, it just wheels away.
- Rival rexes: circle you, rear and roar (the warning), then lunge. Step out of the line: only a lunge that missed leaves it
  open ("BITE!" over it). A rival bitten while it is fresh bites you back.

## Levels
Goals: eat two runners; a duckbill; beat the rival; then mixes of prey, a rival sometimes, and on every tenth level a big rival.
Spikebacks from level 4, hornfaces from 6. Warnings get shorter, rivals stronger, prey quicker to notice you. Weather by level.
Places: the watering hole, the fern brakes, the cave in the cliff (runners hide in it), the nesting ground (the rival's).

## Systems
Hunger and health, stamina for charging, roar's cool-down, the Den (Bone-crusher jaws, Long stride, Thick hide, Thunder lungs),
ranks from Hatchling to King of the Valley, stars for health kept and bites landed.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `s` (the stick pushed some way), `b` (bite), `r` (roar).
- Standing still and biting beat the rival at first (it lunged, hit, then stood "tired" next to you). Now only a lunge that
  misses leaves it open, and a hornface that hits you wheels away: blind 1 of 5,696, random 0 of 480.

## Lessons
1. CREEP AND BITE: walk up to a grazing runner and bite it, twice. 2. ROAR: freeze a bolting runner and bite it, twice.
3. STEP ASIDE: dodge the rival's lunge and land three bites while it recovers.

## Engagement factors chosen
P7 risk and reward (creep or charge, bite now or wait for the opening); P3 honest warnings (every attack shows first);
P14 the near goal (the list, the ring, the arrow); P1 skill that grows (reading each animal); P10 sound (footsteps, roar, crunch).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: walk, bite, roar: you are a T-Rex, nothing to learn.
- L2: heavy footsteps, the camera shakes on a charge, a crunch on every bite.
- L3: every attack is warned; dodge and you are never hit.
- L4: the goal list, the ring, the arrow, the hunger bar.
- L5: your fastest time on each level.
- L6: four kinds of prey, rivals, places, weather.
- L7: creep or charge; which prey to eat when hungry; when to roar.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the Den's upgrades, ranks.
- L10: your fastest time on the home card.

## The card clip
A rival rears and roars, the rex steps aside, the lunge misses, "BITE!", a crunch and the rival flees.
