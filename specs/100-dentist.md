# 100. Dentist

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His idea 30 (21 Nov 2026): "play as a dentist, examining patients, identifying their dental problems and treating them. Start
with simple cleaning and gradually introduce more complicated procedures", with his directions (specs/his-list-2026-11-21.md).
Mine: a calm one-thumb job, upright. Each level is a day at the clinic: a patient comes in with a complaint; you look in their
mouth one row of teeth at a time, find what is wrong (some of it only the mirror or the X-ray shows), fix it with the right
tool in the right order while keeping them comfortable, check it, and send them out smiling. From level 15 two patients a day.

**3D or not:** both, each where it reads best. The clinic is 3D (a bright room, the chair under its lamp, the patient with a
face that shows how they feel) for the arrival, the complaint and the smile at the end; the mouth close-up is 2D (one row of
teeth seen from above, a big U a thumb can work on): small targets on a phone read and touch better flat.

## How it plays (one thumb)
- **Tap a tool on the tray, then press and drag on the teeth.** The tool works just above your fingertip (a ring shows where),
  so your thumb never hides the tooth. The tool to use next glows on the tray (to level 10), and the first time you pick a tool
  a ghost finger shows how to use it.
- **Cleaning:** 🪥 the brush takes off food and plaque, 🪝 the scaler the yellow tartar at the gum line, ✨ the polisher the brown
  stains (only on clean teeth). Calm, steady strokes clean; scrubbing fast does nothing, says TOO ROUGH! and costs comfort.
  A tooth counts as clean at 92%, and the last specks vanish with a ding: no hunting for the last speck.
- **Finding:** 🔍 the mirror shows a cavity up close (a lens that magnifies); ☢️ the X-ray (from level 6) shows decay hidden
  between teeth and a bad tooth. The checklist shows a ❓ until it is found; the complaint is the clue.
- **Repairs**, each its own action: a cavity (drill only the dark decay, 💧 rinse, ⚪ fill, 🥄 smooth the extra, 💡 hold the blue
  light), a chipped tooth (fill the gap, smooth, light), a broken tooth (🥄 smooth the edge, 👑 drag the crown on, light),
  braces (🧴 hold glue on the loose bracket, 🔧 hold the wire key and let go in the green), a bad tooth out (🧊 numbing gel, wait,
  then 🗜️ rock the forceps side to side and lift).
- **Comfort:** their face and a bar. The drill held too long, rough strokes, the drill on healthy tooth, forceps without numbing,
  a wire too tight: all hurt. Stopping lets them recover; 💬 calms them (more for nervous ones and kids). Comfort at zero is a
  break; three breaks and they leave.
- **✓ Done** when it all shines: if anything is left, red rings show where (and it costs a star). ⇅ turns to the other row.
- **The clock** (the clinic closes): generous early, tighter later. **🪥 Relaxed clean** on the home card: no clock, cleaning only.

## Research (I cannot play other games; I read about them)
- **Tiny Dentist** (AOL/Joystiq review): cute, never graphic, but you are "left to figure out what each of the many tools does"
  and there is little reason to keep going. https://www.aol.com/2013-02-17-tiny-dentist-ios-review.html — so: the next tool
  glows, a ghost finger shows it the first time, and there are levels, stars, coins, upgrades and chairs to keep going for.
- **Dentist Bling** (Gamebrain summary of 900 reviews, App Store reviews): satisfying in short bursts, upgrades that let
  "customers last longer"; players tire of doing the same thing every time. https://gamebrain.co/game/dentist-bling ,
  https://apps.apple.com/be/app/dentist-bling/id1487968838 — so: every few levels a new kind of work with its own action, and
  patients with different personalities and combinations of problems.
- **PowerWash Simulator** (GameSpot, OpenCritic reviews): the joy of grime coming off, a "ding" when a part is finished, a
  before-and-after at the end; hunting for the last percent is the annoying part.
  https://gamespot.com/reviews/powerwash-simulator-review-filthy-rich/1900-6417923/ ,
  https://opencritic.com/binarymessiah/review/11395/powerwash-simulator — so: a ding per clean tooth at 92%, a before-and-after
  smile for every patient, no last-speck hunt.
- **Dentist Simulator 2** (Gaming.net): like playing Operation; mistakes are dramatic. https://www.gaming.net/reviews/dentist-simulator-2-review-pc-vr
  — so the "Operation" tension is kept small and fair: the drill warns and stops on healthy tooth, and costs a star, not the game.

## His directions, point by point
- *Colourful 3D clinic; patient in the chair, then a clear close-up of the mouth; friendly, expressive patients*: the 3D room
  and chair; a face that goes from fine to worried to hurting to a big smile; the 2D close-up when work starts.
- *Tap to select tools, drag to position, hold to use; zoom and rotate within limits; forgiving small targets*: the tray;
  drag or hold on the teeth; ➕ zooms in where you last worked; ⇅ the other row; the tool works above the fingertip with a
  generous radius, and a tooth counts clean at 92%.
- *A round: complaint, examine, identify, treat, check before they leave*: the speech bubble, the mirror and X-ray, the
  checklist, the tools, ✓ (which shows what is left).
- *Mirror, tap suspicious areas, a simplified X-ray later*: a faint shadow marks a suspicious spot; the mirror's lens finds it;
  the X-ray from level 6.
- *Early levels brushing, plaque, polishing; highlight the right tool and demonstrate it*: levels 1-3 (with lessons), the
  glowing tool and the ghost finger.
- *Fillings, chips, crowns, braces, extractions, each a distinct interaction*: drill-rinse-fill-smooth-light; fill-smooth-light;
  smooth-drag the crown-light; hold glue, hold-and-release the wire key; gel, wait, rock and lift. Introduced at levels 4, 5,
  7, 8 and 10, each alone first.
- *Satisfying cleaning, clear progress, no invisible last speck*: dirt fades under the tool; the checklist counts teeth;
  92% and a ding.
- *Precision for fillings, controlled movement not fast tapping*: the drill only on the dark decay and slowly; fast strokes do
  nothing; excess filling must be smoothed.
- *Comfort through faces and a meter; pressure and long use hurt; pausing helps*: exactly (and 💬).
- *Different combinations and personalities; nervous patients need reassurance; treatments in the right order*: calm, tough,
  kids, nervous (from level 9); combinations from level 11; the order is enforced and explained (rinse before fill, numb
  before forceps, glue before wire, smooth before the crown).
- *Fair mistakes: warn before an unsuitable tool, small mistakes correctable, explain why*: every wrong use says why and does
  nothing harmful except the drill on healthy tooth and forceps without numbing, which hurt (and say so).
- *Introduce each new tool separately before combining*: one new kind of work per level from 4 to 10, mixes after.
- *Gentle clinic music, water, brushing, polishing; drill softened and adjustable*: all; a **Drill sound** switch in Settings
  (soft, quieter, off).
- *Subtle vibration on cleaning and completing, with an option to disable*: a tick per tool, a light pulse when something is
  fixed, the Vibration switch.
- *Before-and-after smile and reaction; unlock decorations, tools and cases; a relaxed cleaning mode*: the before/after cards
  and "Thank you, doctor!"; chair colours by stars; the kit bag (electric brush, calm music, quiet drill); Relaxed clean.

## Levels, spam, lessons
Levels: 1 brushing (one row), 2 tartar (both rows), 3 stains, 4 cavity, 5 chip, 6 X-ray, 7 crown, 8 braces, 9 nervous patients,
10 extraction, 11 on combinations, 15 on two patients a day; the clock tightens with level. Robots (node, 8 days each): all win
levels 1-20; at level 40 ordinary 0.75, sloppy 0.25, sharp 1.0. Blind moves (any tool, a drag anywhere, a fast scrub, a hold
anywhere, flip, calm, done): 0 of 1120 strings and 0 of 120 random runs in node; scrubbing and holding the drill anywhere hurt,
and three breaks send the patient home. Lessons: 1 BRUSH, 2 SCRAPE, 3 POLISH (three teeth each; a rough stroke starts the count again).

## Engagement factors chosen
P5 teach by doing (the glowing tool, the ghost finger, three short lessons), P6 every input answers back (a sound per tool, dirt
fading, dings), P12 celebrations scaled (a ding per tooth, FILLED!, the smile and before/after), P21 tidy-up satisfaction
(clean enamel coming through). Sound rules S1-S11.
Longevity L1-L10: lessons; tap a tool, drag; mistakes explained on the spot; the checklist and clock; before/after and stats
after every patient; a new kind of work every level to 10, then combinations; which tool next, when to stop and calm them;
Next day at once; chair colours and the kit bag; best coins per level.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A cavity found in the mirror's lens, drilled, filled, set with the blue light: FILLED!, then the big smile.
