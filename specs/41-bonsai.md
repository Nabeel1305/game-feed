# 41. Bonsai

## The idea
My pick for set 7, the calmest one: grow a little tree in a pot through the seasons and shape it to win a festival ribbon.
3D, because the tree on its stand in a Japanese garden, the light and colour changing with the seasons (blossom, summer
green, autumn gold, snow) is the reward of the whole thing.

## How it plays (one thumb, taps only)
Behind the tree a faint dashed outline shows the shape the festival wants. Pink buds sit at the branch tips: **tap a bud**
and a branch grows that way with a pad of leaves (it costs one sap; each season gives some). **Tap a branch** to prune it
and everything beyond it. Buds you leave alone grow **wild** in summer: long, any way they like, so you cut what sticks
out. In summer **caterpillars** come: tap one before it turns red and eats a pad. Winter: nothing grows, a last tidy. At the
end the judges compare the tree to the outline: leaves inside count for you, leaves outside count against you. The match
shows live at the top against the score needed. **Next season ›** turns the season sooner.

## Levels
Six styles of outline (Formal upright, Broom, Slanting, Windswept, Clouds, Literati), each with its own difficulty.
From level 5 a tree grows for two years (buds come again each spring all over the tree). Later: less sap, wild shoots
more often, caterpillars more often and hungrier, a higher match needed. Every tree starts as nursery stock: bare
scaffold branches already heading for each part of the outline (they cannot be cut). Help after three losses: longer
seasons, a kinder judge.

## Systems
Six trees to grow, unlocked with stars (Juniper, Red maple, Black pine, Cherry, Wisteria, Ginkgo: each has its own
leaves, autumn colour and blossom). The Potting Shed: Rich soil, Gardener's eye (buds show where they would grow), a
Birdhouse (fewer caterpillars), Sharp shears (a cut gives back sap). Your garden: every tree you win with stays there.
Ranks from Seedling to Living treasure.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `t` (a tap anywhere on the tree's space), `n` (Next season).
- Random taps grow buds that point anywhere and cut good branches: blind play scores 0-18%. 960 blind-string games and
  480 random ones: none won. Ordinary robots win levels 1-3 every time, about 9 in 10 to level 20.

## Lessons
1. GROW: three buds that point into the outline. 2. PRUNE: cut the two wild branches. 3. CATERPILLARS: flick three.
A tap on nothing during a lesson starts it again. Skip tutorial on every lesson; Tutorial on home and pause.

## Engagement factors chosen
P3 honest feedback (the live match against the target, a line from each bud showing where it would grow on early
levels); P14 the near goal (the match bar with "need" and your best); P22 the trees to collect and your garden;
P10 sound (each grow a step higher in a row, the snip); P5 calm pacing (no clock, the seasons turn when you want).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: a dashed shape, pink dots: tap a dot to grow, tap a branch to cut.
- L2: leaves burst out, a clean snip, the caterpillar flicks away.
- L3: the outline is the judge and the match shows live; nothing is hidden.
- L4: the match bar against "need", the seasons bar with the festival flag.
- L5: your best match on each level, marked on the bar.
- L6: six styles, two-year trees, wild growth, caterpillars, trees with different colours.
- L7: which bud to grow with little sap; cut a wild branch or keep its leaves.
- L8: Try again is one tap and the tree is planted.
- L9: your garden of finished trees; the trees you have unlocked.
- L10: your best match here marked on the match bar.

## The card clip
A cherry in spring blossom, a bud tapped and a pink pad bursting out inside the outline, the match ticking past the target.
