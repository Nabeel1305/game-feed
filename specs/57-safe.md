# 57. Safecracker

## The idea (mine, set 10)
A quiet heist: crack a safe by feel. There was no lock or heist-puzzle game, and the set needed a calm one-thumb game that
is not cards. 2D on purpose: a dial reads best face on, and the whole game is the dial.

## How it plays (one thumb)
Drag your thumb round the dial; it turns with your thumb, one to one. Turn slowly: when the next number of the combination
passes the red pointer, the lock CLICKS (a deep thunk, a buzz, a gold spike on the listening strip) and a chalk mark appears
on the dial at that number. Turn back until the chalk mark sits under the pointer and tap SET: a bolt slides back. Open all
the bolts and the door swings open on the loot.
- Too fast (the rim glows orange, the strip says TOO FAST TO FEEL): you miss the click, and you make NOISE. The noise bar
  fills; full, the dog barks and the alarm rises.
- A wrong SET jams the lock and the ALARM rises. Full alarm: caught.
- From level 3 a GUARD does his rounds: footsteps and light under the door, then his torch sweeps the room. Lift your thumb
  and keep still until it has gone; moving or tapping in the torchlight and he sees you.
- Open the safe before dawn.

## Levels
The dial grows from 40 numbers to 100, the combination from 2 numbers to 5, you must turn more softly to feel the click, the
guard comes more often with less warning, and the night is shorter. From level 8 a wrong number RELOCKS a bolt. Four places
in turn (The Jeweller's, The Bank, The Museum, The Casino) and four kinds of safe (wall safe, office safe, strongbox, the
vault). Help after losses: the click is easier to feel, more time, a longer warning.

## Systems
Feel speed and noise, the chalk mark, wrong numbers and the alarm, relocking, the guard's rounds (warning, torch), the dawn
clock, kinds of safe and their loot, stars (no alarm, time to spare), the Fence (Stethoscope, Soft gloves, A lookout, Lucky
charm), ranks from Pickpocket to Legend, best haul per level.

## Controls
Drag round the dial; tap SET. Lifting your thumb is how you keep still.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `r`, `l` (nudge the dial one number either way), `s` (SET).
- Setting without having felt a click is a guess on a dial of 40 to 100: three or four guesses and the alarm goes. Turning
  steadily without setting runs into the guard's torch or dawn. Blind 0 of 5,696, random 0 of 480.

## Lessons
1. FEEL THE CLICK: turn slowly, find the click, turn back to the chalk mark, SET. 2. SOFTLY: open two bolts without the
noise bar filling. 3. KEEP STILL: let the guard's torch pass.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P6 every input answers (the dial turns under the thumb); P11 haptics (the click is felt as well as heard); P14 the near
goal (bolts left, time to dawn); P10 sound (the click is the game); P1 just hard enough (turn faster for time, or softly for
the click).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: turn the dial, listen for the click: everyone has seen it in a film.
- L2: the dial follows your thumb exactly; the click is instant, heard and felt.
- L3: the rim tells you when you are too fast; the chalk mark shows where it clicked. Nothing is luck.
- L4: bolts left, time until dawn, noise and alarm always on screen.
- L5: stars, and your best haul on each level.
- L6: dial size, number count, the guard, relocking, four places.
- L7: speed against care: go faster and risk missing it, or soft and risk dawn.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the Fence's tools and your rank.
- L10: best haul on the home card.

## The card clip
The dial turning, the gold spike and CLICK, the chalk mark sliding under the pointer, a bolt light going green, the vault
door swinging open on diamonds.
