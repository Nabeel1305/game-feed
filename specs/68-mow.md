# 68. Mow It

## The idea (set 12: mine, the calm one)
The set needed a calm game beside the kart and the knives. Mowing a lawn into stripes is one of the most quietly satisfying
things there is (the lawn-mowing games on phones are played for hours). 2D on purpose: stripes on a lawn are a thing seen
from above, and the whole garden fits on the screen.

## How it plays (one thumb)
Put your thumb down anywhere and slide: the mower drives that way (further for faster); lift it to stop. Every pass cuts a
stripe, light one way and dark the other. Cut the needed share of the lawn (85% at first) before the fuel runs out, then
tap DONE (or keep going for every blade).
- **Flower beds**: the owner's pride. Mow one and it is flattened; three and you are sent home.
- **The grass box** fills as you cut; full, the mower cuts nothing until you empty it at the compost bin in the corner.
- **Fuel**: plenty for a tidy job; going over grass twice wastes it, and ramming the fence or a tree at speed stalls the
  engine and costs some.
- **Stripes**: a long straight pass (6 m) that keeps cutting scores a STRIPE (bonus points; enough for the third star).
- In the long grass: coins (glints) and one treasure per garden (a lost ring, grandpa's watch...). Garden gnomes get
  nudged; from level 6 a dog trots about and gets in the way.

## Levels
Bigger lawns (9 by 13 m up to about 13 by 18), more beds and trees, gnomes, the dog, a slightly higher share to cut, a little
less spare fuel. Every tenth garden is a big one. Four gardens (Rose Cottage, the Vicarage, Sunny Villa, the Manor), a named
owner each time. Help after three losses: more fuel and a bigger bag.

## Systems
Share cut against the need, fuel, the bag and the bin, stripes, beds mown, coins, treasures found all-time, best score per
level, stars (no flowers; enough stripes), the Garden Shed (Wider deck, Big bag, Big tank), ranks from Weekend mower to Lawn
legend.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `s` slide some way (and keep it there), `r` lift.
- A thumb that does not look drives into fences and trees (each stall costs fuel), over beds, and over its own cut grass,
  and never empties the bag: it runs dry well short of the share. Measured: blind strings 0 of 1,984, random 0 of 480,
  beat patterns 0 of 1,440.
- Lessons have fuel too (running dry starts the lesson again) and ask for things blind driving does not do: two stripes, a
  lawn mown round a flower bed, a full bag emptied at the bin.

## Lessons
1. MOW IN STRIPES: two stripes and most of a small lawn.
2. MIND THE FLOWERS: mow round a bed without touching it.
3. EMPTY THE BAG: the bag starts full; empty it at the bin, then mow the rest.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P19 no clock, no pressure (only the fuel, generously); P21 tidy-up satisfaction (the stripes, the cut share filling); P20
gentle palettes (greens, flowers, a red mower); P14 the near goal (the bar with its mark, DONE); P17 rare harmless surprises
(the treasure in the grass, the dog).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: slide to drive; the grass changes colour behind you.
- L2: the hum rising with speed, the shhh of cutting, the clippings flying, stripes chiming up the scale.
- L3: beds, trees and the fence are always in plain sight; a lost lawn is wasted fuel or flowers you drove over.
- L4: the cut bar with the needed mark, the fuel and the bag.
- L5: best score per garden; "so close" when nearly there.
- L6: beds, trees, gnomes, the dog, the bag, bigger gardens.
- L7: empty the bag now or finish this lane; the long way round a bed or the risky gap.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the Garden Shed, treasures found, your rank.
- L10: your own best score on every garden.

## Sound (S1-S11)
A soft engine hum that rises with speed, a gentle shhh of cutting, a two-note chime per stripe climbing a step each time, a
clatter and a chord as the bag empties, a soft thup for a bump, a sad squash for flowers, a ping per coin, a twinkle for
treasure, a woof.

## The card clip
A half-mown lawn in bold light and dark stripes, the mower finishing a long straight pass: STRIPE!, then the last strip.
