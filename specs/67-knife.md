# 67. Knife Spin

## The idea (set 12: mine, like Knife Hit)
Simple and addictive in his set-11 sense: one tap, instantly understood, one more go. 2D on purpose: a target seen face-on
reads best flat, and the gap at the bottom is the whole game.

## How it plays (one thumb)
A wooden target spins. Tap anywhere: a knife flies up and sticks where it lands. Stick all your knives and the target
bursts into pieces; the next one drops in. Three targets make a level (a boss is a fourth).
- Hit a knife already in the target (or a steel stud) and yours clangs off: the round is over (a Spare knife, from the
  rack or the help after three losses, saves one).
- The spin changes: faster, slower, stops, turns back (every target turns back now and then).
- A throw rushed in straight after the last one wobbles and may land a little to either side: one knife at a time.
- Apples on the rim: slice them for points and apples to spend (gold ones are worth five). CLOSE! for a near shave.
- Every fifth level ends on a boss: The Cheese, The Tyre, The Shield, The Cake, The Melon, The Clock.

## Levels
More knives per target, more knives already in, steel studs from level 8, faster and wilder spins; each target in a level a
little harder than the last. Four places (the Barn, the Dojo, the Circus, the Docks).

## Systems
Targets in a level (dots, a skull for the boss), knives left (the column at the bottom left), apples, score with close shaves
and quick-throw combos, best score per level, stars (no spare used; slice the apples), coins, the Knife Rack (Spare knife,
Quick arm: knives fly faster, Steady target: slower spin), ranks from Beginner to Legend, apples sliced all-time.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind move: `t` tap. Tapping fast lands rushed, wobbling knives on each other; a steady beat lays a fan of knives that the
  turning-back target folds onto itself, and the knives already in are in the way. Measured: blind strings 0 of 448, random
  0 of 480; steady beats of every speed 0 of 1,920 on levels 1, 2, 3 and 6.
- Lessons: targets full of knives except for a few gaps, so only a throw timed for a gap gets in; any clang starts the
  lesson target again. Blind tapping for 90 s never leaves a lesson.

## Lessons
1. THROW INTO THE GAPS: one knife into each of three gaps.
2. APPLES: an apple in each of two gaps; slice both.
3. IT TURNS BACK: the target stops and turns back; fill three gaps.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P3 honest near-miss (CLOSE! when a knife lands just clear; "so close" on the end card); P7 hit-stop (the burst freezes a
heartbeat); P8 shake and permanence (every knife stays where it went until the target bursts); P12 celebrations scaled
(a target bursts; a boss bursts bigger); P30 signature (the bursting target, the boss shapes).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: one tap, one rule: don't hit a knife.
- L2: a thunk, chips, a jump of the target on every stick; a clang and a spinning knife on a miss.
- L3: every danger is in plain sight; a loss is always your timing.
- L4: knives left, targets left, the boss skull.
- L5: best score per level on screen; "so close" when you nearly made it.
- L6: spins that change, apples, studs, bosses.
- L7: go for the apple at the bottom now, or wait for a wider gap.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the Knife Rack, apples sliced all-time, your rank.
- L10: your own best score on every level.

## Sound (S1-S11)
A swish per throw; a wooden thunk a step higher with each knife in the same target, never quite the same twice; a metal
clang; a juicy splat (and a chime for gold); a crack and crash as a target bursts, deeper for a boss.

## The card clip
A boss target with one gap left at the bottom: the last knife goes in, the target bursts, BOSS DOWN!
