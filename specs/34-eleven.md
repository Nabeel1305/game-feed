# 34. Eleven

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His words (18 Nov 2026): "a full-on football game that just keeps on going levels, like 11 people, 3 buttons on the screen...
played sideways... One button to pass, one button to shoot, one button to like overhead pass or chip... You beat one team, you
go to another team, and each team is harder than the previous one."
Mine: 3D (a full stadium seen from the main stand, like the television), two thumbs, intensity 4.

## Played sideways
The phone app never turns, so when the screen is upright the whole game (pictures, menus, touches) is drawn a quarter turn round:
hold the phone turned to the left and it is the right way up. Inside the phone app it follows the way the phone is tilted (either
way round). In a browser that turns by itself, it is simply drawn as it is.

## How it plays
- You are gold, always attacking to the right. On the ball you play whoever has it. **Left thumb anywhere on the left:** a
  floating stick to run and dribble. **PASS** along the ground to the team-mate the stick points at (control moves to him).
  **SHOOT** at goal: point up or down to pick the corner (no aim and it goes at the keeper). **LOB** lifts it over the defence to
  a runner, or chips the keeper when you are close and he has come out.
- Without the ball you play the team-mate nearest it (team-mates only shadow: winning it is your job). The same buttons become
  **SWITCH** (to the man nearest the ball), **TACKLE** and **SLIDE** (longer, but miss and you are down).
- Two minutes a match (the clock shows 90). Level at the end: golden goal for a minute; still level: play it again.
- Throw-ins, corners and goal kicks happen by themselves, quickly. A little map of the pitch at the bottom.

## The systems that carry over
- **The ladder:** every level is the next team (generated club names, their own kits), each better than the last: quicker,
  sharper passing, braver pressing, harder tackling, better shooting and keeping. Divisions every five levels, Sunday League up
  to Champions.
- **The training ground:** sprint training, finishing, vision, a new keeper, hard tackling. **Rank:** Sunday league to Legend.
- Stars: win; by two; by three.

## Spamming cannot win
Blind thumbs: the stick held some way (or let go), PASS, SHOOT, LOB, on any beat. A shot from far is an easy catch; an
unaimed shot goes at the keeper; a pass or lob that runs to the goal is the keeper's; team-mates do not win the ball for you.
Blind strings 4 of 13,056, random 0 of 480.

## Lessons
1: three passes, then a goal. 2: win the ball back twice. 3: two lobs over the top that reach a team-mate.

## Engagement factors chosen
P6 every input answers back (the thump of a pass, the crack of a shot, the button flash), P12 celebrations scaled (GOAL with the
stands jumping, WHAT A SAVE, WON IT), P14 goal-gradient (the score and the clock), P15 nested goals (this match, the next team,
the divisions), P26 variety (a second intense two-thumb game, so the feed spreads them out).

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A run into the box, a shot into the far corner, the net bulging and the stands jumping.
