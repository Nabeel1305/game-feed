# 97. Hoops 3v3

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His idea 14 (20 Nov 2026): "play full basketball matches, including movement, passing, defending and shooting", with his
directions (specs/his-list-2026-11-20.md). Mine: three-a-side on a compact full court, sideways, built on Ice Hockey's frame
(the same sideways stage, stick and three buttons), with basketball's own rules: a shot meter, layups and dunks, a 12-second
shot clock, steals that can leave you off balance, two- and three-pointers.

**3D:** a raised sideline camera over a park court at first (sunset, a chain-link fence, trees), then gyms and arenas with a crowd.

## Research (I cannot play other games; I read about them)
- **NBA Playgrounds** (PC Invasion): a jump-peak release was hard to read until it got a proper shot meter; the reviewer ended
  up counting in his head. https://pcinvasion.com/?p=30460 — so the meter is always shown, and (to stop counting, and blind
  thumbs) every shot's meter runs at its own pace with its green a little higher or lower.
- **PBA Court Kings** (Inquirer, 2026): shooting is timing the meter; block, steal and rebound are "get in position and tap".
  https://technology.inquirer.net/?p=145882 — the same here: STEAL is a tap that only works when you are close.
- **NBA The Run** (ClutchPoints): 3v3 street arcade by NBA Street veterans; shot timing and protecting the ball take skill.
  https://clutchpoints.com/gaming/nba-the-run-review
- **Dunk City Dynasty / NBA 2K21 Arcade** (The Gaming Outsider, GameSpot): touch controls that are twitchy or inaccurate spoil
  them. https://thegamingoutsider.com/?p=20360 , https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/nba-2k21-arcade-edition-review-air-ball/1900-6417676/
  — so: a floating stick, three big buttons, PASS finds the best teammate by itself, and a drag only when you want a particular one.
- **NBA 2K18 mobile** (Gamezebo): rebounds that come or don't for no clear reason annoy. https://www.gamezebo.com/reviews/nba-2k18-review-gold-standard-for-mobile-basketball/
  — a loose ball goes to whoever reaches it, and the defenders (between their man and the basket) have the inside position.

## His directions, point by point
- *Landscape, slightly raised sideline camera; 3v3 on compact courts*: yes (the phone held sideways; the camera follows the ball).
- *Left joystick; right: pass, shoot, sprint; hold shoot and release in the highlighted area; on defence pass becomes switch and
  shoot becomes contest/steal*: exactly, the meter floats beside your player with its green band.
- *Tap pass for the best teammate, drag towards one to target, highlight the receiver*: the teammate a tap would find has a blue
  ring under them; dragging from PASS shows a line and picks the teammate that way.
- *Teammates move into space, cut, offer options*: they choose open spots (wings, top, corners, the dunker spot) and now and then
  cut to the basket.
- *Reward passing and getting open; open shots easier; layups automatic near the basket, dunks with space and momentum*: a shot's
  chance falls with the nearest defender (more if they are between you and the basket); assists count; close in, SHOOT is a
  layup, and a dunk if nobody is near and you are moving.
- *Defending: reward staying between attacker and basket; spamming steal leaves you off balance*: a steal is twice as likely from
  in front; two steal taps within a second and you are off balance for almost a second.
- *Short matches, quick play and a small tournament*: 2:20 to 2:50, a level is a quick match; 🏆 Tournament is three knockout games
  (quarter-final, semi-final, final) against stronger teams.
- *Opponent styles without unrealistic speed*: strong defence (tighter, more steals), quick passing, long-range shooters; their
  speed rises only a little.
- *Court sounds, whistles, bounces, squeaks, crowd; upbeat music in menus*: all, the music drops in play.
- *Summary of useful actions*: points, assists, passes, rebounds, steals, blocks, threes and dunks, and turnovers.

## Levels, spam, lessons
Levels: easy first (the other team slower and less accurate), harder for ever; styles from level 4. Robots: an ordinary
player wins about 0.9-1.0 of levels 1-5 and 0.3-0.7 at 40, a sharp one 0.8-1.0 at 40. Blind moves: stick anywhere, PASS, SHOOT
press, SHOOT release, SPRINT; shots without timing almost never fall, putbacks in traffic are hard, and the meter cannot be
counted. Lessons: 1 SHOOT IN THE GREEN (three good releases in a row), 2 GIVE AND GO (pass, run to the basket, layup on the
return, twice), 3 TAKE IT OFF THEM (two steals, without losing your balance).

## Engagement factors chosen
P6 every input answers back (meter, swish, net ripple, squeaks), P12 celebrations scaled (BUCKET / THREE / SLAM, the crowd),
P13 visible personal best (best coins per level), P15 nested goals (match, tournament, stars, kits). Sound rules S1-S11.
Longevity L1-L10: lessons; a stick and three buttons; turnovers explained; the clock and score; stats after every game; styles,
places, tournament; whom to pass to, when to shoot, when to steal; Play again at once; kits and the locker room; best per level.
