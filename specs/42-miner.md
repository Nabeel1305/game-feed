# 42. Star Miner

## The idea
My pick for set 7, the middle-intensity one: a little mining ship in an asteroid field, one floating stick to fly, the
laser doing the cutting by itself. 3D, because the field from above (tumbling rocks lit from one side, a coloured nebula
far below, the long mothership with its green ring) is the whole feeling of being out there.

## How it plays (one thumb, one floating stick)
Put a thumb down anywhere and drag: the ship flies that way, faster the further you drag; let go and it drifts. The mining
laser fires by itself at the nearest rock in reach: big rocks crack into two, then two again, then burst into ore. Fly over
ore to scoop it (the magnet pulls it in) until the hold is full, then follow the green arrow home into the ring under the
mothership to unload (docking also patches one dent). Deliver the ore asked before the mothership jumps away.
Gold rocks give gold (3), crystal rocks give gems (5). Meteors streak across after a flashing red warning line; pirates
keep their distance and shoot slow pink bolts. A hit dents the hull and spills some cargo; fly close to a pirate and the
laser turns on it (+4 coins and its loot).

## Levels
Goal from 18 ore rising to 60+, the clock set by the goal. Meteors from level 2 (more often, faster), crystal rocks from
3, pirates from 4 (up to four, firing more often). Four sectors (the Rust Belt, the Ice Ring, Crystal Reach, Pirate
Nebula). Help after three losses: more time, fewer meteors.

## Systems
Upgrades that stay (Hot laser, Bigger hold, Magnet, Plating); six ships in the hangar, unlocked with stars; risk and
reward on every trip (go home now, or fill the hold and risk a spill); ranks from Rookie to Legend of the void.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind move `d`: the stick dragged some random way and length (or let go), held.
- A drifting ship scoops a little but never carries it home in time: 576 blind-string games and 480 random ones, none won.
- Ordinary robots win the first levels every time and about 3 in 4 at level 40; sharp ones half the time at 80.

## Lessons
1. FLY AND MINE: one small rock at a time, six metres off; scoop three in a row, quickly. 2. HOME: unload twice.
3. DODGE: two meteors in a row. Skip tutorial on every lesson; Tutorial on home and pause.

## Engagement factors chosen
P18 the hold filling (each scoop a step higher); P14 the near goal (ore home on a bar, the cargo shown on it in amber);
P7 risk and reward (a full hold is worth more and loses more); P3 honest warnings (the red line shows exactly where a
meteor will fly); P22 ships to collect.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: drag to fly; the laser does the rest; ore glows.
- L2: the crack and burst of rocks, the ping of each scoop, the chime count-up at the dock.
- L3: every danger is shown first (warning lines, slow bolts); a hit is your own.
- L4: ore home against the goal on a bar, the mothership's clock.
- L5: your best spare time on each level; stars.
- L6: gold, gems, meteors, pirates, four sectors.
- L7: go home now or fill the hold; fight a pirate or run.
- L8: Try again is one tap and you start at the dock.
- L9: your ships and upgrades.
- L10: your best time to spare shown on the home card for this level.

## The card clip
A pirate exploding under the beam, gold ore spraying out, the ship sweeping through it as the hold fills.
