# 49. Ramp Riders

## The idea (mine, set 8)
Set 8 had four of his ideas, all one-thumb and mostly calm (The Bench 2, Haggle 2, Grand Hotel 3, Battle Lines 4). The feed
needed a two-thumb game, a sport, quick runs: a trials bike, side-on. 2D on purpose: the ground ahead and the bike's angle
are the whole game, and they read best flat.

## How it plays (two thumbs)
The bike drives itself. Hold the left half of the screen to lean BACK, the right half to lean FORWARD; both together brake.
On the ground, leaning forward pumps speed out of downhills. Off a kicker the bike leaves pointing along the lip, and in the
air a lean turns it: a dotted arc shows where you will come down, a bar shows the slope there, and a halo round the bike
goes green when the angle matches. Land within the tolerance and you ride on (a PERFECT inside a tighter one gives a short
boost); land badly, or hit the face of a landing because you came up short, and you crash: two seconds on the clock and
back to the last flag. A whole flip, landed, is a boost (two flips, twice as long). Reach the finish inside the time to
beat; stars for 90% and 80% of it. Your best run rides with you as a ghost, and as a dot on the track line.

## Levels
Each level is one fixed track (so the ghost means something): 300 m rising to 700, more kickers and bigger ones, table and
down-slope landings, hills and drops, checkpoints every 110 m. Four places (Dust Bowl, Pine Ridge, Frost Peak, Neon Docks).
The time to beat tightens; landings forgive a little more on the first levels. Help: more forgiving landings and time.

## Systems
Flips and boosts, perfect landings, checkpoints, the ghost of your best, the Garage (Engine, Shocks, Nitro tank), ranks
from Rookie to Legend, coins for stars and flips.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `l` and `r` (a short press of a side), `b` (both).
- At first a thumb that just pressed forward now and then won: a lip left the bike pointing only a little up, and flat
  landings needed nothing. Now the bike leaves along the lip (40-50 degrees), every landing needs a turn of the right
  size, there are more jumps, and a crash costs two seconds and the ride back: blind 5 of 5,696, random 0 of 480.

## Lessons
1. STICK IT: three clean landings in a row. 2. FLIP: two landed flips (a crash does not reset this one). 3. PERFECT: two
perfect landings in a row. Skip tutorial on every lesson; Tutorial on home and pause.

## Engagement factors chosen
P7 risk and reward (go for the flip, or land safe); P1 a skill that grows (reading the arc, letting go early); P3 honest
feedback (the arc, the slope bar, the halo); P27 your own ghost; P10 sound (engine pitch with speed, flip chords).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: lean back, lean forward: two thumbs, nothing to learn.
- L2: the bike turns the instant you hold, stops soon after you let go.
- L3: the arc and the halo show exactly why a landing failed.
- L4: the clock, the time to beat, the track line with its flags.
- L5: your best time on each level.
- L6: tables and down-slopes, big and small kickers, four places.
- L7: a flip for a boost, or the safe landing.
- L8: Try again is one tap; a crash puts you back at the flag at once.
- L9: the Garage, ranks.
- L10: the ghost of your best run.

## The card clip
A big kicker at Frost Peak: a backflip, the halo going green, a PERFECT and the boost streak.
