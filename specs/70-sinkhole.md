# 70. Sinkhole

## The idea (set 13: mine)
You are a hole in the road of a little town. Everything smaller than you falls in; the more you swallow the bigger you get,
until cars, buses, houses and towers go down. Like Hole.io, one of the most played phone games there is, because the growing
feels so good. 3D on purpose: things really tip over the rim and drop out of sight, and the town opens up as the camera rises
with the hole.

## How it plays (one thumb)
A floating stick: put your thumb down anywhere and slide; the hole glides that way (further for faster); lift to stop.
- **Growing**: ten sizes. Size 1 eats cones, bins and people; size 4 cars; size 6 buses; size 7 houses; then shops, blocks
  and towers. Each size up: a whoomp, the camera rises.
- **The town meter**: swallow the needed share of the town (half at first) before the clock runs out.
- **Red barrels** blow up in the hole: you lose growth (or a size) and stall for a second. From size 8 the hole swallows
  them harmlessly; from level 8 **fuel tankers** drive the roads, and they always blow up.
- **People run** from a hole big enough to eat them; cars, vans and buses drive the roads and turn at crossings.
- **Other holes** (from level 4; two from 15, three from 35) race you for the town: a bigger hole swallows a smaller one,
  which comes back two sizes smaller a moment later. With other holes, the biggest eater when the clock ends wins too.
- **Power-ups** on the ground: 🧲 magnet (pulls things in), ⚡ speed, ⏱ +8 seconds. Golden things: coins.
- **Combos**: swallow things close together and the combo climbs (more points, each gulp a step higher).
- **Boss towns** (every tenth): a great tower in the middle; grow to size 10 and swallow it.

## Levels
Bigger towns (3 by 4 blocks up to about 6 by 7), more barrels, more traffic, buses, vans, tankers, taller buildings in the
middle, other holes that are faster and cleverer. Four towns with their own light (Seaside Town in the morning, Downtown under
cloud, Old Town in golden light, Neon City at dusk). Help after three losses: more time and a bigger start.

## Systems
Size, the town meter, the board of holes, combos, power-ups, golden things, best score per level, stars (win; 15 seconds
left; no barrels), coins, the Hole Shop (Head start, Quick hole, More time), four hole colours opened by stars, ranks
from Pothole to Black hole.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `s` slide some way and keep going; `r` lift.
- A thumb that does not look wanders the roads, where there is little to eat, runs into barrels, and never grows: measured,
  blind strings, random moves and beat patterns all win nothing (they eat almost none of the town).
- Lessons: swallowing a red barrel starts the lesson again, and the yards have barrels among everything.

## Lessons
1. SLIDE AND SWALLOW: 12 cones and bins in a yard with red barrels.
2. GROW, THEN EAT THE CAR.
3. BIGGER EATS SMALLER: catch a smaller hole and swallow it.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P9 squash and juice (things tip, wobble on the rim, drop; the camera rises), P18 combos and streaks (the rising gulps), P14
the goal-gradient bar (the town meter with its mark), P12 celebrations scaled (a cone is a blip, a tower is a quake), P30 the
signature: a whole building tipping into your hole.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: one look: a hole, little things, slide.
- L2: the hole follows the thumb at once; every gulp sounds; big things shake the screen.
- L3: barrels are bright red and always in sight; a rival's size is written over it, with a ⚠ when it is bigger.
- L4: the town meter and the clock at the top.
- L5: best score per level; "so close" when nearly there.
- L6: traffic, people who run, rivals, tankers, power-ups, boss towns, four towns.
- L7: grow on little things first or chase a car now; go for a smaller hole or keep eating; the magnet now or later.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: your hole colours, the Hole Shop, your rank.
- L10: your best score on every level.

## Sound (S1-S11)
A gulp for every swallow, its pitch from the size of the thing and climbing with the combo; a whoomp and a chord at each size;
a boom for a barrel; a chomp and a fanfare for eating a hole; a sad slide when one eats you; ticks in the last ten seconds.

## The card clip
A big hole sliding into a block of houses: two houses tip in one after the other, the combo climbing, SIZE 8!
