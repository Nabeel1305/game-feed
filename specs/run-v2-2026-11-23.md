## What was there, and what it is now
| | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Steering | the car drove itself down four lanes; a swipe changed lane | a floating **stick** under the left thumb steers the car |
| Speed | none of yours: it accelerated by itself | **GAS** and **BRAKE** pedals under the right thumb; BRAKE held at a standstill **reverses** |
| Nitro | a tap anywhere, or a swipe up | its own blue **NITRO** button above the pedals, showing how many are left |
| The city | one straight avenue | a **grid of streets and corners**: the route to the drop turns at about six junctions in ten and is never straight for more than two |
| Knowing the way | there was only one way | **green chevrons** on the road along the route, a **green ring** round the junction to head for, and a **green marker** over it saying TURN LEFT, TURN RIGHT, STRAIGHT ON or THE DROP with the metres to go; at the screen's edge when the junction is out of sight; "TURN ROUND" if you face the wrong way. Take a wrong turn and it still points you back; a short cut that reaches a later junction counts |
| The ram | a cop pulled alongside and flashed; you swiped to another lane | the same warning, in a free car: he comes up on the kerb side, flashes ("◀◀ STEER AWAY"), and rams the line you were on: move a car's width off it. He only does it on a straight with room (never close to a corner: seen, it was unfair), never two at once, never into a roadblock's gap |
| Roadblocks | the gap was a lane | the gap is a place across the street: slow down and steer through it; the green ▼ marks it from far off |
| Spikes | a lane | a strip across part of the street, marked SPIKES in red |
| Walls | none | a wall scrape costs speed; a hard hit on a wall costs a hit, like a car |
| Cash, cans, heat, the helicopter, the garage, the foot chase, the safehouse | | unchanged in what they do; cash on the left half of a street now means meeting oncoming traffic |

## Research (I cannot play other games; I studied how they are written about)
- The controls are Police Chase's, researched for it (`specs/police-v2-2026-11-23.md`: a floating stick and two pedals is what phone
  driving games that are liked use; one that drives for you makes the player "a spectator").
- **The arrow should point at the next piece of road, not at the destination.** Crazy Taxi's green arrow is the famous one; a
  breakdown of its core loop notes that it points at road nodes rather than the destination itself, to give an immediate direction;
  Wikipedia's account is that it points in the general direction and does not adjust for obstacles, and players on Steam call it
  confusing where the roads are odd. So ours marks the next junction of the route and says which way it goes from there, and the
  chevrons lie on the road itself.
- Sources: itch.io ("Breaking down elements related to core loop of Crazy Taxi"), wikipedia.org (Crazy Taxi), steamcommunity.com
  (Crazy Taxi discussions), segaretro.org (Crazy Taxi manual: "shows the general direction of the destination").

## Rules (part2.js)
- The city is Police Chase's (`P` 84 m between streets, `RW` 8 m either side); the car's physics are Police Chase's, with nitro.
- `G.route`: junctions in order, each with the direction it is reached in; `G.ri` is the stretch you are on. `makeRoute`,
  `extendRoute` (lessons never run out of road), `segRel` and `segPt` (a place on a stretch: how far along, how far right of its middle).
- Cops follow your trail (`G.trail`); a ram needs the cop within 11 m, his own rest and the shared rest (`G.ramCool`) over, no
  other ram going, no roadblock within 75 m and at least 40 m (more at speed) to the next cross street.
- Lessons: job 1 the stick and pedals (three corners) and NITRO; job 2 the ram; job 3 roadblocks, then corners and obstacles on foot.
- The robots (`honest`) follow a line a ghost car drove along the route (`ensurePath`), with slow hands (a lag on the stick).

## His rules, kept
Large controls that never cover the action (stick bottom left, pedals and NITRO bottom right, the car in the middle); something to do in
the first seconds (you are moving, with chevrons to follow); mistakes explained ("INTO THE WALL!" with "BRAKE before the corner, then
turn"); difficulty from more to deal with (roadblocks from job 3, spikes from 4, the helicopter from 6), not only speed; separate Music,
Sound effects and Vibration; tutorial optional.

## Longevity (L1-L10)
