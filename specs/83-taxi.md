# 83. Taxi Driver

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His words (20 Nov 2026, set 14 list): "You are a Taxi driver."

Mine: a city of blocks in 3D, seen from just behind your cab. Someone waves on the kerb; you pull in and stop beside them,
and the meter starts. Then you find the way through the grid (a GPS arrow and a little map, but jams and road works mean the
shortest way is not always the quickest), weave past slow cars, and stop at their door before the meter runs out. Each
passenger is a kind: a rusher who tips double for speed, a nervous one who wants a gentle ride, a tourist who tips if you go
past the fountain. 3D because the street in front of you is the game (and the light and weather change from place to place).
It must feel nothing like Bus Driver (#78, two pedals, a set route): here the taxi drives itself, the route is yours to
choose, and the thumb steers.

## Research (20 Nov 2026; I cannot play these myself, so I studied reviews, store pages and the makers' own words)
- Crazy Taxi (the 2012 phone version of the arcade game): pick up fares and get them there fast; a fare's colour shows how
  far and how much; short sessions suit phones. Reviewers found its on-screen wheel and tilt controls clumsy and said your
  hands cover the view. (148Apps, TouchArcade, Pocket Gamer, Gamezebo)
- Crazy Taxi: City Rush (made for phones): the cab drives itself; swipe to change lanes, swipe at a junction to turn, tap to
  stop. Its designer said tap/swipe for lanes and swipe for junctions won out in testing. Reviewers liked the one-thumb
  controls but felt being led by the nose (no choice of route) took away skill. (PocketGamer.biz interview, Games Asylum,
  Wikipedia, GameSkinny)
- Taxi Sim 2022: some clients are in a hurry and don't mind a red light, others are frightened by dangerous driving; VIP
  clients pay more. Players ask for more variety and longer roads. (App Store page and reviews)
- What I took: City Rush's thumb (the taxi drives itself; swipes for lanes and turns), but with a real choice of route (the
  GPS is only a suggestion; jams and road works on the map); Taxi Sim's kinds of passenger, so how you drive changes from
  fare to fare; Crazy Taxi's meter, fare by distance and time bonus (a tip). Against the complaints: no on-screen wheel, the
  whole screen is the control, nothing covers the road.

## How it plays (one thumb)
- The taxi drives itself. HOLD anywhere to brake; let go and it drives on.
- SWIPE left or right to change lane (two lanes each way; the right one is the kerb lane).
- Before each junction the road glows: the TURN ZONE. A swipe there turns at the junction (swipe up: straight on). A big
  arrow over the junction and the box at the top show the GPS way; the arrow on the road shows the turn you have set.
- Pick up: the person waving under the green beam; move to the kerb lane and stop in their ring. Drop off: the same at the
  gold beam. The meter counts down: a tip if there is time left, half the fare if late, and 15 s after that they walk.
- Kinds of passenger: Passenger 🙂; Rusher ⚡ (less time, double tip, from level 3); Nervous 😰 (more time; a crash, a red
  light, a fast corner or hard braking scares them, and after three they get out, from level 5); Tourist 📷 (a tip for
  passing the fountain, from level 8).
- Red light: a ticket (-5). A corner taken too fast skids. Three crashes end the shift.

## His rules for every game (20 Nov 2026), kept
- Big controls that never cover the action: the whole screen is the control; the road stays clear.
- Fun within ten seconds: the first lesson has you stopping beside someone waving within seconds.
- Mechanics one at a time: brake and stop (lesson 1), turns (lesson 2), lanes (lesson 3); lights from 2, rushers from 3, road
  works from 4, nervous passengers from 5, jams from 6, tourists from 8.
- Autosave, pause anywhere, DRIVE AGAIN in one tap; mistakes said in words ("RED LIGHT! -5", "TOO FAST FOR THE CORNER!",
  "MISSED THE DOOR: GO ROUND") and on the end card fare by fare.
- Separate Music, Sound effects and Vibration (⚙ Settings).
- An original city and cabs; no real brands.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever (LESSONS rule 0)
- Level 1: two fares a block away, almost no traffic, no lights. Then more traffic, lights, longer trips, road works, jams,
  a tighter meter (the reference pace rises from 8 to 12 m/s and the slack falls from 14 s to 4 s). Every fifth shift is rush
  hour: one more fare. Six places in turn (Midtown, Riverside, Old Quarter, Rainy Avenue, Night Shift, Sunset Strip). Three
  losses: more time on the meter (help).

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
- Blind moves (BLIND in part2): `l` swipe left, `r` swipe right, `b` hold or let go.
- A pick-up needs the kerb lane, the right road and a stop in a 12 m ring; random turns wander off; people waiting give up.

## Lessons of the first three levels
1. HOLD TO BRAKE, STOP IN THE RING: pick up, drop off.
2. SWIPE IN THE TURN ZONE: two turns the GPS way.
3. SWIPE TO CHANGE LANE: pass three slow cars.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK: 3 to 5 principles, the sound rules)
- P6 Every input answers back: the indicator ticks when a turn is set, a whoosh past each car, the brake lights.
- P3 Honest near-miss: the meter's seconds to spare (or late) on every fare on the end card.
- P12 Celebrations scaled: the till and coins for a fare, a TIP, PERFECT SHIFT.
- P13 Visible personal best: your best coins on each shift.
- Sound: a petrol hum that climbs with speed, the hail whistle, the meter's chime, the till; music (its own switch): a
  bass and electric-piano groove that quickens when the meter runs low.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1 The green beam: go there. The arrow: turn here.
- L2 One thumb: hold, swipe.
- L3 Every loss says why.
- L4 The meter, the fare count, the next shift.
- L5 Seconds to spare and tips per fare; best coins per shift.
- L6 Kinds of passenger, lights, works, jams, six places.
- L7 GPS or your own way round a jam; a detour past the fountain for a tourist; upgrades (engine, brakes, Smart GPS, comfy
  seats).
- L8 DRIVE AGAIN is one tap.
- L9 Taxis in eight colours (won with stars), rank, coins.
- L10 Your best coins on every shift.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
Weaving past slow cars in the turn zone, the arrow over the junction, a rusher on board with the meter in the yellow.
