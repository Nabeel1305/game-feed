# 58. Fire Crew

## The idea (mine, set 10)
A firefighting rescue: the set's one intense game (4), with one thumb. There was no fire or rescue game. 3D on purpose: the
building towering over the street, its windows glowing, the smoke rolling up into the night and the ladder swinging up are
the whole scene.

## How it plays (one thumb)
A building on fire, seen from your fire engine. Hold your thumb on the screen and the hose sprays; the water lands just
above your thumb (so your thumb never hides the fire), a moment behind it. Slide to aim, lift to stop.
- Fire grows, and a window fully ablaze lights its neighbours, mostly the one above.
- The tank runs dry if you never stop: lift your thumb and it refills.
- People wave from windows, a smoke ring over each filling as the fire near them burns. Tap a person whose own window is
  not burning and the ladder swings up and brings them down; if their window flares up while it climbs, it comes back.
- Gas bottles (level 4 on) explode when fire reaches them, lighting every window round. Hot windows (level 7 on) glow and
  burst into flame (backdraft) unless you cool them first. Wind (level 5 on) pushes the water sideways and shifts.
- Win: every fire out and everyone down. Lose: more people lost than allowed, or half the building ablaze.

## Levels
The building grows from 4 by 4 windows to 5 by 6, more fires at the start, more people, more gas bottles and hot windows,
stronger wind, and fire that spreads sooner. Four places (Elm Street, The Old Mill, Harbour Flats, The Grand Tower), night,
dusk, rain, mist and storms. Help after losses: the fire spreads more slowly, people last longer.

## Systems
Spray and aim, the tank, fire growth and spread, the ladder and its rule (clear window), smoke and people, gas, backdraft,
wind, the blaze meter, stars (no one lost, under par time), the Fire Station (Big pump, Bigger tank, Fast ladder, Smoke
masks), ranks from Recruit to Legend, best time per level.

## Controls
Hold and slide one thumb; tap a person. Nothing else.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `h` (press: spray where the thumb is), `u` (lift), `m` (slide somewhere on the building), `t` (tap somewhere).
- Spraying at random wastes most of the water on bare brick while fire climbs; people start next to the fire and choke.
  Blind 0 of 13,056, random 0 of 480. (On a 3 by 3 building random spraying covered too much: buildings start at 4 by 4.)

## Lessons
1. THE HOSE: put out every fire. 2. THE LADDER: clear a person's window, then tap them. 3. THE TANK: lift to refill;
put every fire out.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P8 shake and permanence (steam, flames, the glow on the street, BOOM, the scorch left behind); P18 streaks (the hiss
climbs a note per fire in a row); P14 the near goal (people, blaze meter); P10 sound (the roar under it all); P1 just hard
enough (fire climbs and spreads; which fire, when to send the ladder).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: fire, hose, people: no explaining needed.
- L2: the water follows your thumb and hisses on the flames at once.
- L3: every fire, smoke ring and gas bottle is in sight; the ladder's rule is shown (TAP only when it can go).
- L4: people saved and lost, the blaze meter and the tank always on screen.
- L5: stars and your best time per level.
- L6: gas, hot windows, wind, taller buildings, four places, weather.
- L7: put out the big fire or the one by a person; send the ladder now or clear the next window first.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the Fire Station and your rank.
- L10: best time on the home card.

## The card clip
A floor bursting into flame, the water arc sweeping across it, steam and OUT! OUT!, the ladder swinging up and SAVED!
