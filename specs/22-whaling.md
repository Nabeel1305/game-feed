# 22. Whaling

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His list: "a whaling game... you're on a boat, you see a whale, you have to stab the whale and drag the whale, like... how
people used to whale before." Intense (5), two thumbs. The old way, in 3D: a grey heaving sea (golden some days), a wooden
whaleboat with six men rowing and the harpooner in the bow, seen from just behind.

## How it plays (two thumbs)
- The chase: the crew rows; the left thumb steers (slide from where it lands). She shows by her spout ("THERE SHE BLOWS!"),
  rolls along the top, dives and comes up elsewhere (an arrow points to her).
- The throw: swipe up with the right thumb while her back is up: direction is the aim, length the distance; the harpoon takes
  a moment to fly, so lead her. Three harpoons; miss them all and she is gone.
- The ride: she tows the boat. Hold the right thumb to haul (that tires her: the tension gauge in the green); lift to let the
  line run. Before a run her flukes rise and the water whitens ("LET GO!"): haul through it and the line snaps. Keep the bow
  pointing at her or the boat heels over. Run out of line and she is gone. Tire her out and she is yours.

## Levels
One whale at first, two later (three on every tenth); she stays up for less time, swims faster, shies from the boat; bigger,
more frequent runs with a shorter warning; swerves; less forgiving heel. Robots: ordinary all of 1 to 20, 4 to 9 in 10 at 40.

## Spamming cannot win
Blind moves: steer hard left, right or straight; a swipe up the middle or off to a side; hold to haul; let go. First found: a
blind throw "won" a one-whale level by losing the whale (the voyage counted whales met, not whales caught). Now it counts
caught whales: 0 of 77,504 blind strings; random 0 of 480.

## Lessons
1: row, throw; haul when she is calm, let go when she runs. 2: keep the bow towards her. 3: lead her with the throw.

## Engagement factors chosen
P4 fair, readable failure (every loss says why), P7/P8 the hit's jolt and shake, P10 sound with a visual twin (the line
creaks higher as it tightens), P13 fastest whale, P30 "THERE SHE BLOWS!" and the spout.
