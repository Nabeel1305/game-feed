# 19. Hiring

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His list (17 Nov 2026): "you're hiring... a bunch of people come to you with different resumes, and you have to decide whether
to hire or not." Calm (1), one thumb. **2D on purpose** (his go-ahead, 17 Nov 2026: 3D is my call): it is a reading game, and
papers read best flat and large. A wooden desk under a warm lamp; three open jobs pinned along the top; one CV at a time.

## How it plays (one thumb: a desk)
- Each job card says what it needs: years of experience in its field, a certificate still in date, a driving licence (later
  with a points limit), a language, nights or weekends, a minimum age, living close by.
- The CV: photo, name, born, age, how far away they live, experience (job and years), certificates with the date they run until,
  driving, languages, when they can work, a line about themselves.
- Pick up the CV and drag it onto the job it fits (everything that job asks for), or down into the bin if it fits none. Every
  CV fits exactly one job or none.
- Right: a HIRED stamp and it flies into the tray (the chime climbs with a run). Wrong: it shakes, the card turns red and says
  what was missing ("certificate out of date", "too many points"), the right job glows. Two wrong calls end the day.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever (LESSONS rule 0)
Level 1: one need per job. Then two needs (2-4), certificates and dates (3), up to four needs per job, near misses more and
more often (two years when three are needed, a certificate that ran out last month, one point too many, a birthday next
month), longer days (10 to 15 CVs, more on every tenth), and from level 8 candidates who will not wait for ever. Help after
three losses gives them more patience.

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
- Blind moves (BLIND in part2): drag the CV to the first, second or third job, or the bin, without reading.
- Four choices, about a quarter each, one mistake allowed in ten or more CVs. Every blind string of up to four moves on 8 beats
  on levels 1, 2, 3 and 6: 0 of 13,056. Random: 0 of 480.

## Lessons of the first three levels
1: drag a CV onto the job it fits (twice), then one into the bin. 2: jobs need two things. 3: certificates and today's date.

## Engagement factors chosen
P4 fair, readable failure (the card says exactly what was missing), P6 every drag answers (a tick as it passes over a target,
the stamp), P18 right calls in a row, P21 tidy satisfaction (paper into trays), P30 the HIRED stamp.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A near miss caught: a nurse's registration ran out last month, into the bin; the next one stamped HIRED with a run of five.
