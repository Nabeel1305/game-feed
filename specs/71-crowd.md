# 71. Crowd Run

## The idea (set 13: mine)
A crowd of little runners races down a track by itself; you steer it through gates that add, multiply, take away or divide,
past saws and swinging hammers, over narrow bridges and through red crowds, to a giant at the end. Like Count Masters, one
of the biggest phone hits, because every gate is a tiny sum you get right in half a second, and the number keeps growing.
3D on purpose: the track runs away from you over the sea, with the giant waiting at the far end.

## How it plays (one thumb)
Slide left and right anywhere: the crowd follows the thumb (the screen's width is the track's width).
- **Gates** in pairs, side by side: `+16` or `x2`? `-15` or `÷2`? The side the crowd is on is the gate it takes. Blue
  panels help, red ones hurt; sometimes both help (which is more?) and sometimes both hurt (which hurts less?).
- **Saws** slide and spin, **bars** sweep round a post, **hammers** swing across, **walls** block half the track (sometimes
  right behind the better gate, so you take it and slide away at once).
- **Bridges**: the crowd squeezes onto the planks; whoever is off them falls in.
- **Red crowds** meet you one for one; **grey runners** by the side join you if you touch them; **coins** in lines.
- **The giant** at the end has a number: be more than it. Everyone left climbs the **bonus stairs** (x1.5 to x6 coins).
- A big crowd packs tighter, and past 360 each runner you see stands for several.

## Levels
Longer tracks (190 m up to about 360), more bad gates, faster saws and bars, obstacles in pairs, walls behind gates, bigger
red crowds, bridges from level 5, hammers from level 8, and a giant that needs more of what a perfect run would give (70%
at first, 90% later). Every tenth is a boss run. Four places (Sunny Track over the sea, Canyon Run, Ice Bridge, Lava Dash).
Help after three losses: a bigger start and a smaller giant.

## Systems
The crowd's number, gates, obstacles, bridges, red crowds, grey runners, coins, the giant, the bonus stairs, best score per
level, stars (win; reach x2.5; reach x4), the Runners' Shop (Bigger start, Lucky gates, Gold rush), four crowd colours
opened by stars, ranks from Jogger to Crowd king.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `l` / `r` slide some way left or right.
- A thumb that does not look takes the bad gate half the time, runs into walls (half the track) and saws, and falls off
  bridges: measured, 0 of 2,400 random runs and 0 of 720 beat patterns win; most are wiped out within ten seconds.
- Lessons: lesson 1's giant needs every gate right (twelve of them: one blind run in 4,096 could), lesson 2 allows only a
  few runners lost to the saws, lesson 3 needs the better gates and the grey runners to beat two red crowds.

## Lessons
1. PICK THE BETTER GATE: twelve gate pairs, then the giant.
2. DODGE THE SAWS: saws, bars and walls; keep enough runners.
3. BIGGER CROWD WINS: two red crowds; gates and grey runners first.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P7 meaningful choices (every gate a quick sum, some of them traps), P18 streaks (the number climbing gate after gate), P12
celebrations scaled (a +5 is a chime, a x3 a burst, the giant falling a fanfare), P14 the goal-gradient bar (the track to
the giant), P30 the signature: the bonus stairs.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: one look: a crowd, two gates with numbers, slide.
- L2: the crowd follows the thumb; every gate chimes up or groans down.
- L3: everything is in sight well before you reach it; a loss is a bad gate, a saw you ran into, or a bridge you missed.
- L4: the bar to the giant, and his number, at the top.
- L5: best score per level; "so close" with how many the giant had left.
- L6: saws, bars, hammers, walls, bridges, red crowds, grey runners, boss runs, four places.
- L7: x2 now or +30? Grab the grey runners or keep to the better gate? Which side of the wall?
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: your colours, the Runners' Shop, your rank.
- L10: your best score on every level.

## Sound (S1-S11)
A rising chime through a helping gate (higher the more it multiplies), a falling groan through a hurting one; pops for
knocked runners, a splash for a fall, a scuffle for a fight, a deep drum for the giant, a note higher on every bonus step.

## The card clip
A crowd of 40 runs through x3 and becomes 120 with a burst, dodges a saw, and pours over the giant.
