# 9. Reel

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His list (17 Nov 2026): "Fishing: bait, wait, the bite, reel it in fast." All 3D, weather and light that change. Mine: a lake
seen from the end of a wooden jetty, the rod over your right shoulder; the calm game of set 1 (intensity 2), with a real fight in it.

## How it plays (one thumb)
- **Cast:** pull your thumb down and let go; a ring on the water shows where the float will land (pull further = further out,
  left = right). Fish are where the water rings (rises). It wanders a little with distance and wind.
- **Wait and strike:** small dips are nibbles. When the float is pulled right under, flick up. Too soon: the fish bolts and the
  lake goes quieter (the more he does it, the quieter); too late: the bait is taken.
- **Fight:** hold to reel; let go when it thrashes (the tell) and runs, or the line snaps; slack too long and it shakes the hook;
  drag the thumb against the way the line moves to turn it from the weeds (reeds left, lily pads and a dead tree right).
- **Land:** the fish is lifted to the camera with its kind and weight; "your biggest" per kind.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever (LESSONS rule 0)
Fish to land 2 (1-3), 3 (4-9), 4 (10-24), 5; the light runs out (time). Harder: smaller rings, longer waits, more and deeper
nibbles, a shorter moment to strike, a shorter tell, a line that snaps faster, stronger fish, quicker to the weeds. New kinds
come in by level (perch and trout, bass at 3, carp 5, pike 8, catfish 12). Every tenth: a named giant. Weather by level:
dawn, morning, golden hour, grey sky, rain, mist, sunset, midday, storm (lightning, thunder), dusk, night (moon, glowing float, lantern).

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
- Blind moves (BLIND): cast anywhere, cast at the rings, strike, hold, let go, swap the rod side.
- Structure: a strike before the bite scares the fish and makes the whole lake quieter (a habit is ruinous); reeling through a run
  is always too much for the line, on every level; letting go for long loses the fish; the rod must be on the side against the
  fish's way, which changes on its own clock. Proved by search: 46,528 blind games and 480 random ones, none won.

## Lessons of the first three levels
1: cast to the rings (2), strike when it goes under, not on the nibble (2), hold to reel (2). 2: let go when it runs (3),
steer it from the weeds (2). 3: land two fish. Nothing lost while learning; the light does not run.

## Engagement factors chosen
P4 fair failure (the thrash before every run, RUN! on the gauge, WEEDS on the side), P12 celebration scaled to the fish,
P13 personal best (your biggest of each kind), P17 rare surprise (one fish in fifty is a golden carp), P30 signature (the float's
deep "ploonk" and the fish held up to the camera). Sounds: lapping water, birds or crickets, rain, thunder, reel ratchet, line scream.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
The float goes under, the strike, the fish leaps.
