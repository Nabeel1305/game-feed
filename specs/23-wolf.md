# 23. Wolf Pack

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His list: "a wolf pack game, where basically you're an animal, you're a wolf, and you have to hunt down prey to eat."
Intense (4), one thumb. Open snow between dark pines in 3D (golden dusk, grey morning, falling snow, night). You run as the
lead wolf with two packmates; a herd of elk scatters and yours is marked.

## How it plays (one thumb, plus the pack)
- The thumb lands anywhere: slide left and right to steer, push up for a burst (your wind runs down; save it).
- Tap FLANK on a side: a packmate races up that side of her and she swerves away from it, towards you.
- Her head turns before she swerves (an arrow shows which way): cut the corner.
- Strike with a quick flick when you are close and BESIDE her ("STRIKE!" shows). From straight behind she kicks (a hunt
  lost); too far and you stumble. If she reaches the trees, she is gone.

## Levels
One hunt at first, up to three (four on every tenth); she is faster, tires less, starts further ahead, swerves more often and
more sharply with a shorter warning; the trees are nearer; the striking window is tighter. Robots: ordinary all of 1 to 20
and 9 in 10 of 40; sharp 9 in 10 of 40.

## Spamming cannot win
Blind moves: steer hard left, right or straight; burst on, off; flank; strike. First found: blind bursts and strikes caught her
0.9% of the time and a lost hunt still "won" a one-hunt level. Now she runs away from you (so a wolf that only runs straight stays behind her), a strike must be a flick towards her, a lunge at
nothing makes you stumble, from behind she kicks almost every time, and the level counts hunts won: 25 of 77,504 blind strings,
random 0 of 480.

## Lessons
1: run her down and strike from her side. 2: cut the corner when her head turns. 3: use the pack.

## Engagement factors chosen
P4 fair, readable failure, P6 every input answers (snow sprays on a burst), P7 the strike's jolt, P13 fastest hunt, P30 the
pack's howl.
