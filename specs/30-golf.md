# 30. Mini Golf

## The idea (his words, then mine)
Set 5, mine: the calm game of the set (1), one thumb, 3D (a little course in a garden, the windmill's sails turning, the ball
rolling off the rails is the whole charm).

## How it plays (one thumb: pull back, let go)
- Put the thumb down anywhere and **pull back**: a dotted line shows where the ball will go (longer with more power), and a
  ring round the ball fills with power. **Let go**: the ball goes the other way. A tiny pull does nothing (change your mind).
- **A round is three holes:** straights, doglegs left and right, a zigzag, a box with an island, a U-turn round a wall, a bend,
  a diamond. Wooden rails bounce the ball. **Par** for each hole; the round has a **stroke limit**.
- **Obstacles** (more of them as levels go on): red bumpers kick the ball; sand slows it; slopes (arrows) bend the roll;
  windmills (day 4) swat it with their sails; ponds (day 5) send it back to where you hit from, with a stroke; sliding walls
  (day 7). A ball too fast over the cup lips out.
- **Coins** on each hole: roll through them.
- Birdie, eagle, hole in one: a crowd's applause.

## The systems that carry over (set 4's rule)
- **Coins and the pro shop:** long sight (a longer aiming line; at the top rank it bends off the first rail), mulligans (take a
  shot back: a REDO button), a lucky cup (wider), a sand wedge, water wings (the first pond is free), a coin magnet.
- **Rank:** Beginner, Putter, Club player, Ace, Champion, Hole-in-one legend.
- **Themes:** the garden, the beach, the castle, candy land (five rounds each), with the weather changing.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever
More obstacles on every hole (one, then up to three or four), faster windmills and walls, a tighter stroke limit (par + 3 at
first, down to par + 1).

## Spamming cannot win
Blind moves: a pull in any direction, any length; the REDO button. The stroke limit is tight and the cup takes only a slow
ball: blind strings 0 of 256, random 0 of 240. In a lesson the hole must be sunk in two strokes or fewer, twice in a row.

## Lessons
1: pull back, let go: sink it in two, twice. 2: play off the rails round the corner. 3: time the windmill.

## Engagement factors chosen
P3 honest near-miss (LIPPED OUT!), P6 every input answers back (the putter's click, the rail's knock, the bumper's boing),
P12 celebrations scaled (par, birdie, eagle, hole in one), P16 natural stopping points (a round of three), P20 gentle palettes.
Beside them, set 4's systems: the pro shop and rank.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A long putt bending off a rail, through the windmill's gap and into the cup.
