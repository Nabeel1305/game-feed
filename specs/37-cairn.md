# 37. Cairn

## The idea (his words, then mine)
Mine, the calm one of set 6 (intensity 1): stack river stones into a cairn on a boulder by a lake. 3D: the lake, the mist, the
light and the stones' shapes are the whole feeling of it.

## How it plays (one screen, one thumb)
The next stone sways gently above the stack, its soft shadow on the top stone; tap anywhere to drop it straight down. A stone
stays if the weight of everything above each stone sits over the part it rests on; right on the edge it rocks for a moment and
goes; past the edge it tips, and those stones roll into the lake. Two stones wait in the basket: tap one to take it next (slabs
and long stones are wide and steady; round stones and pebbles are small and tippy; quartz is rare). Three perfect drops in a
row and you find another stone. Stack the height asked for before the basket is empty. No clock. Later a breeze crosses the
water (the reeds bend first, then it leans on tall stacks).

## Levels (LESSONS rule 0)
6 high from 9 stones at level 1, up to 11 high; the sway quicker and wider; more round stones and pebbles; the breeze from
level 5. Five places (mist, morning, golden, cloud, moonlight). Help after three losses: slower sway and a spare stone.
Robots: ordinary wins levels 1-10 (80-100%), 70% at 20; sharp 90% at 40.

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
- Blind moves: `d` (drop whenever), `p` (take a basket stone without looking).
- The stone sways wider than the boulder's top and dwells longest at the ends of its sway, where a dropped stone falls off or
  rocks and goes. 1,984 blind-string games and 480 random ones: none won.

## Lessons of the first three levels
1. DROP: four in a row near the middle. 2. JUST RIGHT: three perfect drops in a row. 3. CHOOSE: steady a leaning stack twice
(the slab in the basket is the way).

## Engagement factors chosen
P19-like calm (no clock, no fail until the basket is empty); P18 the perfect streak (a bell that climbs a pentatonic step each
time, a stone found every third); P14 pips to the height asked; P13 your highest here marked on the pips; P8 permanence: your
finished cairns stand in the shallows.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: one tap drops; the shadow shows where.
- L2: each stone clicks into place with a woody tock that rises up the cairn; a perfect rings a bell.
- L3: a stack about to go rocks first ("IT ROCKS..."), a breeze bends the reeds before it comes.
- L4: "4 / 7" and the pips; the basket shows what is coming.
- L5: your highest here on the pips; "one stone short!" at the end.
- L6: stone kinds that change where you aim, the breeze, five places.
- L7: which basket stone to take now: the slab to steady a lean, or save it.
- L8: one tap to stack again.
- L9: every cairn you finish stays in the shallows by the boulder, and in "Your cairns".
- L10: your highest here marked on the pips.

## The card clip
A tall cairn in golden light, the last stone swaying down onto it, "PERFECT x3".
