# Hunter 0.4.0: a fixed stick, and the lion is a fight (23 Nov 2026)

His words (17:31): "in Hunter, you're supposed to tap left to face left, tap right to face right. But every time I tap, the joystick
just goes there... every game needs to be particular. Each game is unique. Not every game is a one shoe size fits all... for Hunter, the
joystick should be in a set location... I would even recommend making it bigger because people need to hunt, people need to run, and they
need to be able to creep... A lion attacked me and then it just left me alone? No. A lion, if a lion's gonna attack me, you have to show
me, boom, lion attacking you, swipe to dodge... then you can tell me to tap to hit, tap to hit, tap to hit... I have to defeat that lion."

## Why taps made sticks
The left half of the screen was a floating stick. During a lion's growl taps went to the lion, but a lion that mauled you vanished
("gone") at once, so the taps you were still making to face it made sticks instead.

## The stick
Fixed bottom left, radius 74 (was a floating 56), drawn all the time; it works only where it is drawn. A gentle push creeps, a full push
runs; push hard to one side to dive from a charge. Dragging anywhere else moves the crosshair; THROW throws.

## The lion fight
1. A growl from one side: TAP THAT SIDE to face it (the screen's halves light up).
2. It leaps (it rears up over you): SWIPE to dodge (either way). A tap does not dodge. Facing the wrong way, it is on you.
3. It lands past you and stumbles: TAP, TAP, TAP: each tap is a spear thrust (its strength bar at the top). 5 thrusts at first, up to 10.
4. If it still lives, it turns, paces and leaps again: swipe, then tap.
5. A leap you do not dodge mauls you (one chance) and knocks you down; it is still there, and comes again.
6. It dies: THE LION IS DEAD! The hunt goes on.
Timing: the swipe window 0.8 s at first, 0.45 s far on; the stumble 1.7 s, 1.15 s far on.
