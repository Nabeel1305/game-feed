# No impossible physics: the sweep of 23 Nov 2026

His words (23 Nov 2026, 14:53, on Vine): "the vines are disappearing. Where are they going? They shouldn't disappear... If you're
scared that the player may not be able to see the screen because of the vine, you should just, you know, render it like invisible,
but it should still be kind of be seeable. It shouldn't disappear. I don't want any impossible physics in any of these games. So
check the games for impossible physics... And the graphics are still bad."

## Vine 0.3.0 (the game he was playing)
- **The vines vanished** because the camera cut away everything nearer than 2.3 m (`cam.near = 2.3`). The cut is gone (0.1); a vine
  near the camera fades, never below 20%, so it is always seen.
- **The swing was not a swing.** A catch set a full swing at once whatever speed you came in with; the body was moved on a made-up
  path; you could steer in the air. Now: a rope of fixed length, gravity, energy kept (the swing grows only by pumping your legs),
  steering only while you hold the vine (you lean the plane of the swing), none in the air; a catch is where your hand meets the rope
  and keeps the speed along it. The course is built by flying every gap with the same physics, so every gap can be made.
- Every vine swings on its own: the one you let go of swings back and settles.
- A fall into the river: the splash is seen, then the view goes dark and opens with you back on the vine.
- **Graphics:** a new jungle: painted bark and leaves, buttressed mossy trunks, leaf walls, ferns and broad leaves, mossy rocks with
  foam, lianas, the waterfall on the cliff, mist, light shafts, parrots, motes; the tree house built round a giant trunk with braces,
  a lantern, a ladder and a flag; vines with leaves and flowers (the next vine's flowers pulse). Lighter bark and a sky fill light so
  the green reads as green; the vine branches are limbs rising from the trunks, with leaves where each vine hangs (they were dark bars
  across the view).
- Measured (robots, `test_play`): ordinary player wins levels 1-3 0.9-1.0, sloppy 1.0 at level 1, sharp 0.3 at level 40, blind 0 of
  25,920.

## The sweep: every game, by the kinds of fault found in Vine
How: the code of every game was searched for each kind of fault below, every hit was read, and the faults that are really there were
fixed. Nothing here was seen played on a phone; each fixed game passes its own checks (`tools/check_game.py`).

### 1. Things cut away or hidden near the camera
Searched every 3D game's camera `near` and anything hidden or faded by distance to the camera.
- **Rex: fixed.** The camera's cut-away moved with the distance to the rex (up to 7 m), so a tree between the camera and the rex was
  sliced away. Now nothing is cut away: a tree in the way turns into a fine see-through screen (`W3.seeThrough` / `W3.setSee`, new in
  `tools/world3d.js`, for any game that needs it).
- Looked at and left: Police Chase and On the Run (near 3 m: the camera is 13 m behind and 10 m up, nothing comes within 3 m);
  Convoy, Pottery (1 m, nothing that close); Hippodrome (a rival near the camera turns ghostly but stays seen); Bandit (only the
  glow of a flare fades near the camera, not a thing).

### 2. Steering once off the ground (nothing to push on)
- **Kingdom Kart and Stunt Kart: fixed.** A kart in the air turned with the stick (a third of its grip): now it keeps the line it took
  off on. A glider still steers (it has wings), and a rocket.
- **Surf: fixed.** In the air your speed along the wave changed with the angle of the board: now you keep the speed you took off with;
  the stick still turns the board for the landing.
- **Splash: fixed.** The stick pushed you sideways in a jump (3 m/s every second, from nothing): now the jump is aimed when you leave.
- Looked at and left: Ramp, Powder (in the air the thumb turns the bike or board, the path is gravity only: riders really do this);
  Skydive, Soar, Balloon (a body, a cape, a canopy and the wind can steer); Kites (a slack fighter kite does turn on the spot); Dash
  (the yellow rings and pads are springs that throw you: the push comes from them); Horde (magic bolts).

### 3. Falling at a steady speed, stopping dead in the air
- **Kingdom Kart: fixed.** Over the edge, a kart stopped dead and sank at a steady 14 m/s: now it flies on and falls faster and
  faster.
- **Sandworm: fixed.** When you fell, the worm froze where it was (in the air if it was breaching): it now runs on without you and
  comes down; you fly off carrying its speed, on a real arc.
- **Climb: fixed.** A fall was a steady slide down to the last clip: now it is a real fall until the rope comes tight, then the stretch
  and the bounce.
- **High Wire: fixed.** The walker fell at a steady 4 m/s: now faster and faster.
- **Skydive: fixed.** At the door you fell at 3 to 4 times gravity: now gravity and air drag (9.8 m/s/s at the door, levelling off at
  the falling speed); tucking speeds you up and spreading slows you as the air would.
- Looked at and left: Bandit (a shot-down plane still flies, nose down, and sinks: it has lost its lift); Splash (in the water you sink
  slowly: water, not air).

### 4. Being put back after a fall, without the fall being seen
- **Drone: fixed.** A crash jumped the drone at once to the restart spot: the crash was never seen. Now it bounces back off what it hit,
  tumbles and falls; the view goes dark and opens with the drone before the hoop.
- **Kingdom Kart, Sandworm, Climb, Splash, High Wire, Vine:** the fall is seen first, then the view goes dark and opens where you are
  put back (it was a pop).
- Looked at and left: Dash (a run restarts at the flag, as the game is played); Kites (a cut rival puts up a new kite); Sinkhole.

### Not checked this way
Every ball and every throw was searched for gravity (Hoop Shot, Tennis, Skim, Fruit, Penalty and the rest use it; Hunter's spear and
Reel's fish fly an arc), but nobody has watched every bounce in every game. Cars passing through each other in the city games,
and the stacking in Cairn, were not part of this sweep.
