# 44. Drift Kings

## The idea
My pick for set 7, the fast one (intensity 4, the set's two-thumb game): kart racing with drifts, boosts and items.
3D, because a chase camera low behind a little kart, kerbs flashing past and sparks under the wheels is the whole rush.

## How it plays (two thumbs)
The kart drives itself. The left thumb steers: put it down anywhere on the left half and slide (further for a harder
turn; let go to go straight). Without a drift, a hard turn at speed runs wide and scrubs speed off (grip). Hold a hard
turn at speed and the kart DRIFTS: it slides round the bend (slide further for tighter, less for wider) and sparks build
under it, blue, then orange, then purple; straighten up (or let go) and you get a boost, longer for each colour. Drive
through a ? box for an item and tap the right half to use it: a Turbo can, an Oil slick dropped behind you, a Zap orb that
flies to the kart just ahead, a Bubble that blocks one hit. Grass slows you; the striped barrier bumps you. Five rivals,
each with its own line and a mind of their own with items.

## Levels
Four tracks in turn (Sunny Loop, Canyon Run, Neon City at night, Snow Peak), two laps at first then three. Finish top
three (levels 1-2), top two (to 6), then you must win. The rivals get faster and corner better as you go; from the middle
levels a race is won by drifting every long bend. Help after three losses: the rivals a touch slower.

## Systems
Karts to collect with stars (Rocket, Lime Zip, Wave, Bumblebee, Phantom, Golden King); the Pit Shop (Engine, Grippy tyres,
Hot sparks, Rocket start); best time on each level; ranks from Learner to Drift King.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `s` (steer some random way, or straight, and hold it), `t` (tap for the item).
- Blind steering grinds along the barriers and scrubs speed in every bend: 768 blind-string games and 480 random ones,
  none won. Ordinary robots win the first levels every time; sharp ones win two in three at level 40, half at 80.

## Lessons
1. STEER AND DRIFT: two boosts. 2. ITEMS: use two. 3. BIG BOOST: two orange boosts. Skip tutorial on every lesson;
Tutorial on home and pause.

## Engagement factors chosen
P18 the spark colours climbing (blue, orange, purple, each a chime higher); P7 risk and reward (hold the drift longer for a
bigger boost, or let go safe); P14 the near goal (your place against the place needed, the lap); P22 karts to collect;
P10 sound (engine pitch with speed, tyre squeal, the boost whoosh).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: slide to steer, tap for the item; the kart goes by itself.
- L2: the drift slides and sparks, the boost kicks, the camera shakes.
- L3: grip is honest: a corner taken too hot runs wide; the spark bar shows when the next colour comes.
- L4: your place against the place needed, the lap, the map with every kart.
- L5: your best time on each level; stars for the win.
- L6: four tracks, items, rivals that use them, faster rivals.
- L7: drift long for purple or let go now; use the Zap now or keep it.
- L8: Try again is one tap and the lights count down.
- L9: your karts and upgrades.
- L10: your best time on the home card.

## The card clip
Purple sparks through a long bend on Neon City, the release, the flame, overtaking two rivals on the straight.
