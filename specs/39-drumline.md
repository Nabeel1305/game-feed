# 39. Drumline

## The idea
From his list for set 6: a rhythm game, notes roll toward two drums, tap left and right on the beat; songs unlock as you play.
3D: you stand on a stadium field at halftime behind a snare and a tom, the crowd in the stands moving to your playing.

## How it plays (two thumbs)
Notes come down two lanes; tap the left half of the screen for the left drum and the right half for the right one as each
reaches the line. Gold notes cross both lanes: both thumbs at once. Long notes are drum rolls: keep tapping that drum till
they end. PERFECT, GOOD, OK by timing ("a little early", "a little late"). The crowd meter fills with hits and drains with
misses and taps where there is no note; empty, the song stops. A run of hits raises the multiplier (x2 at 8, up to x4);
sixteen in a row lights FEVER, double points for 8 s. Each level is its own song (Marching In, Friday Lights, Halftime Heat...),
made from its tempo and seed: phrases repeat so you can learn them, with a fill every fourth bar.

## Levels
84 beats a minute at first, up to 150; longer songs, busier (eighths, then sixteenths), gold notes from 2, rolls from 3; the
timing window narrows and a miss costs the crowd more. Four places (home field, night game, sunset bowl, rainy rivalry).
Help after three losses: a more forgiving crowd and a wider window.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `l`, `r`, `b` (a tap left, right, both).
- A tap with no note near costs the crowd; a note missed costs more. 5,696 blind-string games and 480 random ones: none won.

## Lessons
1. ON THE BEAT: eight in a row. 2. BOTH DRUMS: three gold notes in a row. 3. DRUM ROLL: two rolls kept going to the end.

## Engagement factors chosen
P18 the run and its multiplier (the crowd roars every ten); P14 the song's progress bar with your furthest point; P3 honest
timing ("a little early"); P13 your best score and accuracy for every song; P10 the music itself carries the beat.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: two halves, two drums; the note reaches the line, you hit.
- L2: a crack and a boom on every tap, the drum head flashes, the lane lights.
- L3: notes are seen coming two seconds ahead; every miss says early or late or no note.
- L4: the crowd meter and the song bar; "you got 70% of the way through".
- L5: your furthest point marked on the song bar; best score and accuracy per song.
- L6: gold notes, rolls, fills, faster tempos, new songs.
- L7: risk a FEVER run or play it safe; keep tapping a roll for points or save your thumbs.
- L8: Try again is one tap and the song starts with a count-in.
- L9: "Your songs": every song you have played, its stars, score and accuracy; tap to play it again.
- L10: your furthest point on the song bar.

## The card clip
FEVER: the stadium lights flashing pink and blue, a gold double note landing PERFECT.
