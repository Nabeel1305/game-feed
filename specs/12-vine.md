# 12. Vine

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His list (17 Nov 2026): "Vine swing: Tarzan, find the rhythm, grab vine after vine." Medium intensity (3). A rainforest gorge in
3D, a river far below, light through mist; you, seen from just behind, hanging by your hands; a tree house at the end.

## How it plays (one thumb: the thumb is your grip)
Keep it down and you hold the vine and swing (he kicks to keep a full swing). Lift it to let go: as you swing forward and up is
right; each vine needs its own moment (a gold notch on the swing meter in the first five canopies). In the air, move the thumb
sideways to lean; put it down when your hand reaches the next vine to catch it. Grab at air and the hand needs a moment.
Fall into the river: back to the vine you jumped from, one of three lives. Reach the tree house before the light goes.

## Levels
More vines (8 to 20), a smaller reach, vines further to the side (never further than a lean can carry you); every tenth canopy
is the Great Gorge. Each next vine is placed by flying the best release from the last, so every gap can be made, and the best
release angle differs vine to vine (0.42 to 0.80 rad), so no rhythm works.

## Spamming cannot win
Blind moves: thumb down, thumb up, lean left, lean right, lean straight: 0 of 25,920 and 0 of 480 random.

## Lessons
1: let go at the right moment (the catch is done for you), catch the vine (the letting go is done for you). 2: both. 3: lean.

## Engagement factors chosen
P4 fair failure (the swing meter's notch, "nothing there"), P6 every input answers back (whoosh, leafy slap), P13 best catches in a
row, P14 vines to the tree house, P18 catches in a row climb in pitch.
