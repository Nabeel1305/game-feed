# 52. High Wire

## The idea (his, 19 Nov 2026, set 9)
"Walk across a tightrope in 3D, keeping your balance and trying not to fall off." With his tips: simple controls that take
practice, visible lean and wobble, last-second recoveries, wind, obstacles, crouching and pauses brought in slowly, height and
sound for tension with the rope always visible, optional risks for points, short crossings first and checkpoints later.
3D: the height and the drop are the whole feeling.

## How it plays (one thumb, a floating stick)
Put the thumb down anywhere. Slide sideways to swing the balancing pole (you lean back the other way); push up to walk; leave
it in the middle to stand (steadier); pull down to crouch (slower, steadier, and the only way under a low banner). Lift the
thumb and you stand with the pole level. You are an upturned pendulum: a small lean grows faster the further it goes, so you
catch it early. Each step jolts you. The balance arc at the top shows your lean (green, amber, red); past half way the wire
creaks, a heartbeat starts and the screen darkens at that side: a last chance. Pull back and "SAVED!". Further, you fall:
back to the last platform, a try gone (three tries). Stars hang beside the wire: lean out towards one as you pass to grab it.

## Things on the wire (by level)
Wind from level 2 (the post flags stream and WIND shows a second and a half before a gust: stop and lean into it), birds from 3
(they shake the wire as they fly off), low banners from 4 (crouch), swinging sandbags from 5 (stand, let it pass, then go).
Longer wires, platforms every 40 m, quicker tipping and stronger wind later. Four places: Red Canyon (120 m up), City Towers
(180 m), Waterfall Gorge (90 m), Mountain Pass (300 m), with the level's weather. Help: slower tipping and gentler steps.

## Systems
Three tries per crossing, platforms, stars (risk), the Dressing Room (Longer pole, Soft shoes, Steady nerve), ranks from Rookie
to Legend of the wire, best times.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `d` (thumb down: a random lean, walking or not), `c` (crouch or not), `u` (lift).
- Random leaning tips you over within seconds: blind 0 of 5,696, random 0 of 480.

## Lessons
1. WALK AND LEAN: cross 20 m without falling. 2. WIND: cross 25 m through gusts (stop and lean into them). 3. REACH FOR IT: grab
two stars.

## Engagement factors chosen
P6 tension and release (the creak, the heartbeat, SAVED!); P7 risk and reward (lean out for a star); P1 a skill that grows
(catching the lean early); P3 honest feedback (the arc shows the lean exactly); P10 sound (wind, creak, heartbeat, crowd).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: one thumb, lean against the tip: everyone knows balancing.
- L2: the walker answers at once; the pole swings with the thumb.
- L3: the arc shows exactly how close you came; every fall is yours.
- L4: metres to go, the platforms, the stars.
- L5: your best time on each level.
- L6: wind, birds, banners, sandbags, four places and weathers.
- L7: walk on or stand; reach for the star or not.
- L8: a fall puts you back on the platform at once; Try again is one tap.
- L9: the Dressing Room, ranks.
- L10: your best time on the home card.
