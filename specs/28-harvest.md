# 28. Harvest Valley

## The idea (his words, then mine)
My idea for set 4: a calm game (1) among the intense ones, deep in the way a farm is deep (several crops, several jobs at once,
orders to plan for, upgrades that make the farm grow), with the easiest controls of the set: every move is a tap on the thing
itself. 3D, because the farm in front of you (beds of soil, crops growing up out of them, hens pecking, rain, the seasons) is
the pleasure of it.

## How it plays (one thumb: taps)
- **The cart wants** (top): today's orders, e.g. 6 carrots, 3 tomatoes, 2 eggs. Fill them all before the sun sets.
- **Pick a seed** in the bar at the bottom (carrot 12 s, tomato, pumpkin; corn from day 8, sunflowers from day 14; each shows
  how long it grows and how many drinks it needs), then **tap an empty bed** to plant it.
- A **💧 drop** over a crop: it is thirsty; tap it to water. Left too long it wilts (a red ring fills), then dies.
- When a crop **sparkles** it is ripe: tap it to pick it into the basket.
- The **hen house** shows eggs when they are laid: tap to collect. The **cart** says SELL! when you hold something it wants.
- **Crows** (from day 4) land on crops: tap them before the ring fills or they eat the crop.
- Wrong taps cost: water a crop that is not thirsty and it rots (tap again to clear the bed); poke the hens before they have
  laid and they sulk.
- Rain waters everything; the summer sun dries crops faster. Seasons every five days: spring, summer, autumn, snowy winter.

## The systems that carry over (set 4)
- **Coins and the farm:** every sale pays coins (stars add a bonus). Six upgrades that stay: more beds (another row), sprinklers
  (rows that water themselves), fertiliser (faster growth), a scarecrow (fewer crows), more hens (eggs sooner), a painted
  cart (more coins per sale).
- **Rank:** Farmhand, Grower, Farmer, Rancher, Landowner, Harvest king.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever
More and bigger orders, more kinds at once, shorter days, crops that grow slower, shorter thirsty windows, more crows, new
crops that need more drinks. Robots: ordinary all of 1 to 20 and 3 in 4 of 40 to 80; sloppy most of 1 to 20; sharp all.

## Spamming cannot win
Blind moves: a tap on any bed, the hen house, the cart, any seed. A tap on a bed does what that bed needs only when it needs
it; at any other time it rots the crop. Carrots need two drinks, so a tapping thumb rots nearly every crop before it is ripe.
Blind strings: 5 of 13,056; random 0 of 480. In a lesson, a rotten, dead or eaten crop starts the lesson again.

## Lessons
1: sow, water, harvest and sell three carrots. 2: water only when asked; tomatoes too. 3: eggs and crows.

## Engagement factors chosen
P6 every input answers back (the earth thumps, the can splashes, a pop and a bell when a crop is picked), P14 goal-gradient
bar (each order fills, each crop's growth bar), P16 natural stopping points (one day is one level), P18 combos (crops picked in
a quick run, a note higher each), P20 gentle palettes (spring greens to snowy winter). Beside them, set 4's carry-over systems:
the farm's upgrades and rank.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A row of ripe crops sparkling, picked one after another, the cart's till ringing.
