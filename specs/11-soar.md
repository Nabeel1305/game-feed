# 11. Soar

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His list (17 Nov 2026): "Flying: first person behind the shoulder like Superman, beautiful places, through rings; calming." The
calmest game of set 1 (intensity 1). You fly over a sea of islands (nine coasts), seen from just over your right shoulder, arms
forward, a red scarf streaming, and follow a trail of golden rings. No death: you skim off the sea or a hillside.

## How it plays (one thumb)
Put a thumb down anywhere: that point is the middle of a little stick. Move it right to lean and turn right, up to climb, down to
dive (faster). Let go to level out. Through the middle of a ring: a bell that climbs the scale while the chain lasts; a bullseye
near the middle. An arrow at the edge points to the next ring when it is off the screen.

## Levels
More rings in the trail (14 to 30), more of them needed, smaller, closer together, bending and climbing more; from level 4 the
trail runs low through the valleys; later, gusts of wind. Every tenth flight is a long one. Weather: golden, morning, sunset, dawn,
mist, midday, dusk, night, grey.

## Spamming cannot win
Blind moves: hard left, hard right, up, down, centre, gentle left, gentle right. The trail bends both ways and up and down on its
own, so a held or rhythmic stick never threads it: 0 of 77,504 blind games and 0 of 480 random ones.

## Lessons
1: turn (three rings, alternate sides). 2: climb and dive (three rings, high and low). 3: a chain of three.

## Engagement factors chosen
P18 chains (the bell climbing the scale), P19-P20 calm (no fail on contact, a gentle palette, a soft drifting chord), P13 best
chain per flight, P14 rings needed bar, P30 signature (the streaming scarf and the bells).
