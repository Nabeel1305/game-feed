# 13. Hunter

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His list (17 Nov 2026): "Caveman hunter: stalk slowly without startling it, throw a spear; deer, mammoth; sometimes a lion comes
for you." The intense game of set 1 (intensity 5), two thumbs. Ice-age steppe at the foot of snowy mountains, first person, crouched
in tall grass, a flint spear in your right hand, a heartbeat that quickens as you close in.

## How it plays (two thumbs)
- Left thumb held: creep. Lift: freeze. The animal grazes, flicks its ears and raises its head (the tell), then looks: move while it
  looks and it sees you (the eye over it fills red); see enough and it bolts. Some later looks come with no warning.
- Right thumb: pull back and let go to throw where the mark is. The mark sways with your arm, more the further you throw, never so
  much that a spear at the middle finds the heart; beyond 16 m a throw falls short (the mark turns red). Chest = down; haunch =
  wounded, it bolts; miss = it bolts. Bison take two spears; the mammoth (every tenth hunt) three.
- A growl from one side: a cave lion. Swipe that way to turn, tap the instant it leaps to brace the spear. Wrong way, too soon or
  too late: mauled.
- Three chances (escapes or maulings).

## Spamming cannot win
No luck in the throw (the spear goes where the mark is), the heart is forward of the middle, the tell is short, the lion must be
faced first. Blind moves: creep, freeze, throw at the animal's middle, throw anywhere, turn left, turn right, brace: 0 of 77,504
and 0 of 480 random.

## Lessons
1: creep and freeze when it looks (2), throw at the chest (1). 2: get close, then throw (2). 3: the lion (2).

## Engagement factors chosen
P4 fair failure (the eye filling, the ear flick, "IT SAW YOU!", the red mark beyond range), P7 hit-stop on the kill and the brace,
P13 longest kill, P17 the lion (a rare fright), P30 signature (the heartbeat).
