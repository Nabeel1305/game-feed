# 40. Strike Alley

## The idea
My pick for set 7, the calm one: bowling, the most familiar "one good throw" feeling there is. 3D, because the lane running
away to the pins, the ball curling in and the pins exploding are the whole pleasure; a neon alley at night.

## How it plays (one thumb)
Put your thumb down: the ball slides across the foul line to sit under it. Pull your thumb down toward you and let go: the
ball rolls up the lane, faster the further you pulled; tilt the pull to angle it (a slingshot: pull right, it goes left).
While you pull, a dotted line shows where the roll will go, including its curl, as far down the lane as your Aim line
upgrade reaches. Aim for the pocket, just beside the front pin, for strikes. Two balls a frame; a pin map at the top right
shows what is still standing.

Scoring: each pin 1, a spare +5, a strike +10, and strikes and spares in a row build a run that multiplies them (x1.5, x2,
x2.5, up to x3). A golden pin now and then is worth 5 coins.

## Levels
Ten-frame games with a score to beat (from 166 at level 1, rising 3.5 a level); every fourth of five a **spare challenge**
(a leave already standing: make 2 to 5 of them), every fifth a **strike-out** (5 fresh racks, 1 to 3 strikes). The lane's
oil runs further and the dry wood bites harder as levels go up, and a hand gets less steady (wobble). Four places: Starlight
Lanes, Retro Bowl, Cosmic Night, Gold Cup Arena. Help after losses: a steadier hand.

## Systems
Balls to collect with stars (House, Cherry, Galaxy, Lava, Marble, Gold, Neon: each curls more, some heavier); upgrades in
the Pro Shop that stay (Aim line, Steady grip, Strong arm, Lucky pins); rank from Rookie to Perfect 300.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind move `b`: a pull of a random length and tilt from a random spot, let go.
- Gutters score nothing, a careless ball leaves splits, and only a run of strikes and spares reaches the target.
  576 blind-string games and 480 random ones: none won. An ordinary robot wins levels 1-3 about 9 times in 10.

## Lessons
1. ROLL: 6 pins or more, twice. 2. THE POCKET: a strike or 9. 3. A SPARE. Skip tutorial on every lesson; Tutorial on the
home card and the pause menu.

## Engagement factors chosen
P3 the honest line (the dots show exactly where the ball will go); P18 the run multiplier (DOUBLE! TURKEY!); P14 the score
bar toward the target; P22 the collection of balls; P10 the sound of pins (each pin its own knock, so a strike is a crash).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: everyone knows bowling: touch, pull, let go, knock the pins.
- L2: the ball rumbles, pins clatter, a strike shakes the screen and flashes.
- L3: the dotted line is the truth; a miss is visibly yours.
- L4: the score against the target on a bar, frame by frame on the card.
- L5: your best score on every level on its card; stars.
- L6: hook balls, oil, spare challenges, strike-outs, four alleys.
- L7: which ball to bowl with; aim the pocket for strikes or play safe for spares.
- L8: Try again is one tap; a new game starts with the ball in your hand.
- L9: your balls, your upgrades, your rank.
- L10: a pale ghost ball at the foul line marks where you stood for your last strike.

## The card clip
A hook ball curling into the pocket from the low camera, the pins exploding, STRIKE! in gold.
