# 95. Bin Round

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His idea 20 (20 Nov 2026): "work at the back of a moving bin lorry, collecting rubbish from houses. The player does not drive.
This is a relaxing game with calming music, and the player cannot lose", with his directions (specs/his-list-2026-11-20.md).
Mine: the calmest card in the feed (intensity 1). One thumb: tap what to fetch, hold to empty, let go at the right moment.

**3D:** the street is the game's pleasure: gardens, houses, trees, the season and weather, the lorry's beacon. The camera sits
behind you at the back of the lorry, looking along the pavement at the gate you are working.

## Research (I cannot play other games; I read about them)
- **Garbage Truck Simulator** (PC Gamer's review): emptying each bin "slowly, tediously" was the dull part.
  https://pcgamer.com/garbage-truck-simulator-2011-review — so every lift here is about a second, and walking is automatic.
- **Garbage Crew!** (GameGrin): after a bin was emptied, players lost track of where to put it back.
  https://www.gamegrin.com/reviews/garbage-crew-review/ — so a bin goes back to its exact spot by itself after a good empty.
- An itch.io comment on a bin-lorry jam game asked for trash compacting as an extra mechanic.
  https://itch.io/post/16137931 — the compactor runs (and shows) when the hopper fills.
- **PowerWash Simulator**, the model "chore-core" calm game (GameSpot, EGM, CogConnected): no timers; a satisfying "ding" when
  each part of a job is done; each part has its own progress; a montage of your progress at the end; save midway.
  https://gamespot.com/reviews/powerwash-simulator-review-filthy-rich/1900-6417923/ , https://egmnow.com/?p=21809 ,
  https://cogconnected.com/?p=313777 — so: a ding on every neat empty, a dot per house filling in, a round summary,
  stop after any house and carry on later.

## How it plays (one thumb)
- The lorry drives to a house and waits. Signs float over each bin and bag.
- **Tap one**: you walk over and bring it to the back of the lorry (a second tap queues the next).
- **Hold COLLECT**: it lifts and empties. The ring round the button fills; **let go in the green** and it goes back neatly.
  Too soon: it comes down full (try again). Too long: it bangs, a few bits spill on the road, and it comes down to try again.
- **PICK OUT** (recycling with a bin bag on top): optional; a bonus and a "Good eye!".
- **NEXT HOUSE** when the house is done (bits left on the road: Sam sweeps them up). **Stop here for now** ends the shift and
  the round carries on from that house next time. **AUTO** sets off by itself when a house is done.

## Levels: easy first, more to do for ever (LESSONS rule 0)
Nothing can be lost, so a later street is a fuller one: 4 houses with 2 items at street 1, up to 9 houses with 2-5 items;
recycling at 2, garden waste at 3, armchairs at 4, bins round the back at 6; the green narrows a little (0.7 to 0.5 of its
size). Robots: a round takes about 74 s at street 1, 98 s at 3, 226 s at 40.

## Spamming cannot finish a round (LESSONS rule 1)
Blind moves: `t` a tap anywhere on the street, `p` press COLLECT, `r` let go, `n` NEXT HOUSE, `o` PICK OUT. A press only lifts
a bin already at the lorry, a lift needs a release in the green, and a wrong release costs a moment while the bin comes down.
Every blind string of up to four moves: 25,920 rounds, none finished; random moves: 480, none.

## Lessons of the first three streets
1. EMPTY A BIN (two, let go in the green). 2. KEEP THE RECYCLING CLEAN (PICK OUT, then empty). 3. FINISH THE HOUSE (a bag and a
garden bin, then NEXT HOUSE). Blind tapping for 90 s finishes none.

## Engagement factors chosen
P6 every input answers back (a ding and a buzz for a neat empty, the ring, the bin shaking), P16 natural stopping points (stop
after any house, the round waits), P19 no clock, no fail, P21 tidy-up satisfaction (bins back upright at their gates, signs
gone, the house dot fills in). Sound rules S1-S11. Longevity L1-L10: L1 the lessons; L2 one tap and a hold; L3 a slip costs only
a moment; L4 the house dots; L5 street count and coins, no grades; L6 new kinds of rubbish and seasons; L7 PICK OUT and AUTO;
L8 the next house at once; L9 your lorry and vest colours, crew-room upgrades; L10 the streets you have finished on the Levels grid.
