# 66. Stunt Kart

## The idea (set 12: his idea)
His words (19 Nov 2026): "what about another one like Super Mario Kart, a racing game, but one that you can do cool things."
Drift Kings (44) already races karts with drifting and items; this one is built round the cool things: ramps, tricks in the
air, a glider jump, boost pads, coins and a STAR. 3D: a race is the road rushing at you, the kart flying off a ramp.
Built on Drift Kings' 3D track engine (tracks, karts, rivals, items), with new tracks, physics in the air, and no drift.

## How it plays (two thumbs, both easy)
The kart drives itself. LEFT thumb: put it down anywhere on the left half and slide to steer. RIGHT thumb: one tap.
- **Ramps** (yellow and black) on the straights: you fly. In the air a tap is a TRICK: a flip, a spin, a barrel roll, a
  table-top, a corkscrew. Each takes 0.38 s. Fit in as many as the jump allows; land in the middle of one and you WIPE OUT
  (spin, drop coins). Land clean for a boost (bigger for each trick), PERFECT for landing just as the last trick ends.
  A ring round your kart empties as the ground comes up, with a tick showing how long a trick takes: TAP! shows when
  there is time.
- **The gold ramp** on the longest straight launches a GLIDER: three seconds in the air, steerable, through a line of coins.
- **STAR**: tricks (and pads) fill the meter; full, you go STAR for 4.5 s: faster, and any kart you touch spins out.
- **On the ground** the tap uses your item from a ? box: Turbo, Oil slick, Zap orb (homes on the kart ahead), Bubble.
- **Boost pads** (glowing arrows) on the straights; **coins** through the bends and in the air: each adds 1.2% top speed
  (up to 10), and you drop up to 3 when hit.
- Finish in the place the race asks for: top 3, then top 2, then first. 2 laps at first, then 3.

## Levels
Four tracks (Sunny Stunts, Canyon Leap, Neon Jumps, Snow Flyer), ramps placed where the track is straight long enough to land.
Rivals get faster, trick more (and wipe out less) and use items more with the levels; every tenth race a quicker field.
Help after three losses: the rivals a touch slower.

## Systems
Place, laps, time, tricks, perfects, wipeouts, the STAR meter, coins, items, stars (win; land enough tricks with no
wipeouts), best time and best stunt score per race, coins to spend, the garage of six karts unlocked by stars, upgrades
(Engine, Big springs: more air for another trick, Star charger, Coin magnet), ranks from Rookie to Sky king.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `s` steer some way and hold it, `t` tap the right side.
- Tapping all the time in the air chains tricks back to back, so it lands mid-trick: wipeouts. Random steering hits walls
  and grass. Lessons: a wipeout or a tap on the ground (with no item) starts the count again; the glider lesson needs five
  coins in one flight, which takes steering.

## Lessons
1. JUMP AND TRICK: one trick per jump, landed clean, two jumps in a row; tap only in the air.
2. TWO IN ONE JUMP: two tricks in one jump, twice.
3. THE GLIDER: catch five coins in one glide.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P3 honest near-miss (the air ring shows exactly how close the ground is; "one place short"); P18 combos (tricks in one jump
climb a note each, bigger boost for each); P12 celebrations scaled (CLEAN, PERFECT, STAR); P1 just hard enough (one more
trick: risk against reward); P30 signature (the glider jump).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: steer with the left, tap with the right; the ring and TAP! say when.
- L2: the kart really flips and spins; a landing thumps; a boost roars.
- L3: a wipeout is always your own late tap, and the ring showed it coming.
- L4: the place you need, the lap, the STAR meter, coins to 10.
- L5: best time and best stunt score per race, on the end card.
- L6: four tracks, the glider, items, rivals who trick too.
- L7: one more trick or land safe; Turbo now or later; the pad or the coins.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the garage of karts, upgrades, your rank.
- L10: your own best stunt score on every race.

## Sound (S1-S11)
A springy launch (a big whoosh for the glider), a whoosh and a note per trick climbing in the same jump, a thump and a chord on
a clean landing, a sparkle for PERFECT, a cartoon spin for a wipeout, coins pinging up the scale, a zip for a pad, a
shimmering run for STAR, and Drift Kings' engine, items and lap bells.
