# 61. Black Keys

## The idea (set 11: his brief)
"A game like Piano Master... you have to click the black tile and it goes, and it slowly goes faster." Piano Tiles, made so
the music is real: every tile is the next note of a real piece, so playing well IS playing the song. 2D on purpose: tiles on
a page, like the original.

## How it plays (two thumbs)
Four ivory lanes; rows of tiles scroll down. Tap START, then every black tile; each plays its note on a small piano.
- Tap white, or let a black tile slide off the bottom, and it is over (the wrong spot flashes red; a missed tile: the page
  rolls back to show it blinking).
- LONG tiles: hold your thumb on them as they pass for a held note and bonus; letting go early only loses the bonus.
- DOUBLE rows (from level 3): two black tiles at once, one per thumb, a melody note and its octave.
- The song speeds up as it goes. Finish it and the ENCORE starts: the opening again, faster.
- Taps just beside a tile still count (fat fingers), more so on the first levels.

## Levels
Sixteen pieces, all old enough to belong to everyone: Twinkle Twinkle, Ode to Joy, Frère Jacques, Jingle Bells, Brahms'
Lullaby, Minuet in G, Für Elise, Can-Can, Greensleeves, The Entertainer, In the Hall of the Mountain King, Turkish March,
Bach's Prelude in C, Vivaldi's Spring, Morning Mood, When the Saints. Each level is a song; later rounds play them faster,
with more double rows. Four rooms (Recital Room, Jazz Club, Concert Hall, Opera House). Help after losses: a little slower.

## Systems
Tapping, long tiles (hold), double rows (two thumbs), the speed ramp, the encore, fat-finger tolerance, stars (finish, every
long tile held, the encore), the Music Shop (Wide fingers, Steady hands, Second chance: once a song a missed tile slides back),
ranks from Beginner to Maestro, best tiles per song.

## Controls
Tap the black tiles; hold the long ones. Two thumbs.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `t` (a tap somewhere), `h` (a press held somewhere), `r` (a lift).
- Three lanes in four are white: a blind tap ends it within a few taps. Blind 0 of 5,696, random 0 of 480. Before START a
  stray tap does nothing (so the instant start is not lost to a slip). Lessons restart on any mistake.

## Lessons
1. BLACK, NEVER WHITE: play a short tune. 2. LONG TILES: hold three. 3. TWO AT ONCE: play three double rows. All slow.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P1 just hard enough (the speed rises in each song and level by level); P3 honest near-miss (how much of the song you played,
and the page rolls back to the tile that got away); P23 instant start and restart (a START tile, one tap to try again);
P13 visible personal best (tiles per song); P30 the signature: every tile is the real melody.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: black yes, white no: understood in one tap.
- L2: every tap rings a piano note; the song plays itself through your fingers.
- L3: tiles come from the top in plain sight; a miss is always shown.
- L4: the bar to the song's end, the three stars, the next song.
- L5: tiles per song and "90% of the song · so close".
- L6: long tiles, double rows, sixteen songs with their own rhythms, the encore.
- L7: two thumbs: which thumb takes which tile, when to let a long tile go.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the songs you have mastered, the Music Shop, your rank.
- L10: your best on every song.
