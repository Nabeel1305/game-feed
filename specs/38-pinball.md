# 38. Pinball

## The idea
From his list for set 6: a 3D table, two flippers (left and right thumbs), ramps, targets and missions that change the table.
3D, seen from where a player stands: the chrome ball, the rails and the light are the pleasure of it.

## How it plays (two thumbs)
Hold the left half of the screen for the left flipper, the right half for the right one; any touch launches a waiting ball.
Where the ball meets the flipper decides where it goes. One MISSION at a time on the backbox (up the ramp twice, clear the
three drop targets, round the orbit twice, later the ramp then the orbit within 6 s); its target blinks on the table. Each
mission done adds to the multiplier (x2, x3...) and relights the ball saver; the second earns an extra ball, the third starts
MULTIBALL. Finish the missions the level asks for before the balls are gone. Bumpers, slingshots and the A-B-C lanes score.

## Levels
2 missions at first (5 balls on levels 1-2, then 4), 3 from level 9, 4 from 17; a little more gravity and a faster ramp
needed. Missions: up the ramp 3 times, clear the drop targets twice, round the orbit twice, the ramp then the orbit. Four themed tables (Neon Nights, Space Station, Haunted Pier, Deep Jungle), five levels each.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `l`, `r` (a quick flip), `h` (hold a flipper up or let it go).
- A mission shot counts only straight off a flipper; a flipper pressed again within 0.3 s is weak; flapping overheats a
  flipper for 1.6 s; a ball saver is given once per ball. A shot is aimed by where the ball meets the flipper (catch it on a
  raised flipper, let it roll, flip).

## Lessons
1. FLIP: hit the ball up the table 8 times without losing it. 2. AIM: three drop targets. 3. THE RAMP: once.

## Engagement factors chosen
P6 every flip clacks and the ball answers; P18 the multiplier climbing with each mission; P14 the mission bar; P13 your best
score here; P12 small pops for bumpers, a fanfare for a mission, a siren for MULTIBALL.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: two halves, two flippers; the mission tells you what to hit and it blinks.
- L2: solid flipper clacks, punchy bumpers, a chrome ball that rolls and shines.
- L3: the ball is always in sight; a drain says so; the ball saver is shown.
- L4: the mission and its progress bar on the backbox; missions 1 of 3.
- L5: your best score here; the trophies.
- L6: four tables, mission kinds, multiball.
- L7: catch and aim, or keep it moving; go for the ramp now or keep the ball safe.
- L8: one tap of Try again.
- L9: trophies (first mission, ramp runner, multiball, each table beaten, a million in all).
- L10: your best score here on the home card and the end card.

## The card clip
MULTIBALL: two chrome balls in the bumpers, the table flashing.
