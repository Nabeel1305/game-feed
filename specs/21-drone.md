# 21. Drone Pilot

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His list: "a drone game where basically you're a drone pilot and you control the drone, and you have to take the drone
through certain hoops." Medium (3), two thumbs. A park on rolling hills in 3D (pines, broad trees, buildings, weather and
light by level); a course of glowing hoops; the drone seen from just behind.

## How it plays (two thumbs, like a real drone controller, mode 2)
- Left stick (thumb on the left half, centred where it lands): up climbs, down sinks; left and right turn.
- Right stick: up flies forwards, down brakes and backs; left and right slide sideways. Lifted, the sticks spring back and the
  drone holds level, but it carries its speed (it drifts) and the wind pushes it (an arrow shows the wind).
- Thread the next hoop (it pulses; an arrow points to it when off the screen). Each hoop gives back seconds. A rim, a tree, a
  wall or a hard landing is a crash (back before the hoop). Three crashes or the clock at zero end the run.

## Levels
Level 1: five hoops, flat, side to side. Then up and down, further apart and further across, smaller hoops, wind and gusts
(from 4), buildings, hoops that slide to and fro (from 12), less time per hoop. Robots: ordinary all of 1 to 20, half of 40.

## Spamming cannot win
Blind moves: sticks held in a corner or the middle. First found: flying straight on flat early courses threaded hoops by luck
(0.11%); the hoops now alternate side to side from level 1. 0 blind wins after; random 0 of 480.

## Lessons
1: forwards and turn (three hoops side to side). 2: up and down. 3: slide and brake (the drift).

## Engagement factors chosen
P6 every input answers (the props' whine follows the sticks), P13 seconds to spare as the best, P14 hoop count, P18 hoops in
a row (the chime climbs), "CLEAN!" through the middle (P12).
