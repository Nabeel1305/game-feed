# 55. Tri-Peaks

## The idea (mine, set 10)
His list is all built, so set 10 is mine, picked for a balanced feed. A calm card game: there was none. 2D on purpose: cards
read best flat.

## How it plays (one thumb)
Three peaks of 28 cards; the bottom row face up, each card turning over once nothing covers it. Tap an uncovered card one
higher or one lower than the card on the pile (aces go on kings and twos) and it becomes your card. Stuck: tap the stock. Every
card in a row without drawing builds the streak (score x streak); a streak of six makes your next stock card a joker (wild).
Jokers also hide in the stock. Locked cards (a padlock) wait until you play the gold key card. Clear all three peaks before the
stock runs out. Stars for stock cards left (or a run of 12).

## Levels
The stock shrinks from about 42 cards to 22 (two decks, so it can be long), jokers in the stock thin out, locks from level 4
(two rising to six). Help: more stock cards.

## Systems
Streaks and earned jokers, locks and the key, score and best score, the Card Room (Second thoughts: one undo a deal; Extra card;
Peek at the next stock card), ranks from Newcomer to Grand master.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `c` (tap a card somewhere), `s` (the stock).
- Random taps waste the stock and rarely find a card that fits: blind 0 of 1,984, random 0 of 480.

## Lessons
1. ONE UP, ONE DOWN: a run of four. 2. CHOOSE WELL: of two cards that fit, the one with the longer run (five without drawing).
3. TO THE TOP: clear a peak.

## Engagement factors chosen
P5 calm flow; P7 risk (play this card or that, draw now or later); P2 streaks that build; P14 the near goal (cards left, stock);
P10 sound (a flick that climbs a note per card in the streak).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: higher or lower: everyone knows it.
- L2: a card flies to the pile the instant you tap.
- L3: every card is face up when it matters; the stock count is always in sight.
- L4: cards left, stock left, the streak and how far to a joker.
- L5: your best score on each level.
- L6: jokers, locks and keys, four tables.
- L7: which of two cards to play.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the Card Room, ranks.
- L10: best score on the home card.
