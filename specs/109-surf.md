# 109. Surf (his idea 29, set 20)

## The idea
His words: play as a surfer, catching waves, riding along their faces, performing tricks and staying balanced until the
ride ends. Intensity 3, two thumbs, played sideways.

**3D:** yes. The wave is the whole game: a face that rises and peels, white water chasing you, a lip that throws over into a
barrel, the sea and the beach in changing light.

## Research
I cannot play other games myself, so I read store pages, reviews and players' comments of the phone surf games:
- **True Surf** (the World Surf League's game; App Store reviews, TouchArcade forum): the most praised surf game on phones,
  for real-time touch steering ("carving those liquid walls"), gorgeous waves and barrels. Players' complaints: the same wave
  over and over, stalling in barrels being the only way to score, tricks not worth learning, little explanation beyond a basic
  tutorial. So here: three different waves a session (fast sections, barrels), every move scores and the same move again
  scores less, a lesson for each part, and barrels are one way to score among several.
- **Surf or Die** (App Store reviews): tilt steering; players found getting onto the wave confusing ("a weird 360"). So
  catching is explicit and forgiving: hold PADDLE as the wave comes, swipe up when the ring turns green.
- **YouRiding** (App Store): named moves (snaps, laybacks), rankings on longest barrel; a player called the controls sticky,
  "getting stuck in the lip a lot". So the lip is never sticky here: at the top you either fly off (fast and steep) or turn.
- **SumbaSurf** (Surfertoday): build speed, carve, stall to position yourself in the barrel, hold your line as it breaks; a
  ride scored on speed, power and manoeuvres. Kept: crouching stalls you so the barrel can come over you.

Sources: toucharcade.com/community/threads/324992, apps.apple.com/au/app/true-surf/id1177819604, apps.apple.com/app/id392566571,
apps.apple.com/us/app/id1586421941, surfertoday.com/surfing/sumbasurf-first-person-surfing-game.

## How it plays (two thumbs, sideways)
- **Catch:** the wave rolls in. Hold PADDLE (the right button) to match its speed; when it lifts you the ring turns green:
  swipe up to stand. Early is forgiving (nothing happens; try again); too late and the wave passes (the next comes at once).
- **Left thumb: steer.** A floating stick: up the screen is up the face, down is down, right is along the wave. Down the face
  you gain speed, up you spend it; the wave's power is strongest in the pocket near the curl.
- **The curl** (white water) chases you along the wave; it races in fast sections (the face steepens ahead as a warning);
  you must stay ahead of it. The curl gauge on the left shows how close it is.
- **Moves:** a CARVE (a long turn), a SNAP (a hard turn down at the lip), a CUTBACK (turn back towards the curl and out
  again), an AIR (fly off the lip fast and steep; swipe to spin 180 at a time; push down to land; a full spin lands clean),
  a BARREL (hold CROUCH, stay low inside the tube, ride out of it). Linked moves build a combo; the same move again scores less.
- **Balance:** very sharp turns at speed wobble you (WOBBLY!); crouch to steady; a fall is a wipeout.
- **Session:** three waves; beat the target. A wave ridden to its end adds a bonus; a wipeout keeps a quarter of that wave.

## His directions, point by point
- View: 3D beaches, rolling waves, clear water (real reflecting sea), spray, changing light (four beaches, four times of day);
  the camera from the sea, a little behind the surfer, turning to show the wave and the way ahead (further ahead in a barrel,
  so its exit stays in view). Kept.
- Controls: drag to steer; up to climb, down to carve back down; a crouch button for stability and barrels. Kept.
- Catching: floating, the wave shown coming, hold paddle and swipe up as it lifts you, forgiving early timing. Kept.
- Basic round: catch, stay ahead of the break, optional moves, finish the ride. Kept.
- Wave behaviour: steepening face (and a warning line) before a fast section, white water for the broken part, the lip
  throwing over a hollow before a barrel; each level's waves are the same every time, so they can be learned. Kept.
- Speed and balance: down gains, up and sharp turns spend; wobble shown on the surfer, time to recover. Kept.
- Satisfying movement: a curved foam trail, spray off every turn and off the lip; linked turns score as carves. Kept.
- Tricks: snaps and cutbacks from the start, airs from level 5; swipe to spin as you leave the lip, push down to land. Kept.
- Barrel riding: crouch inside, stay low, ride out; the camera keeps the exit in view. Kept.
- Progression: small gentle waves first; bigger waves, faster peels, fast sections (level 4), barrels (level 3) and higher
  targets. Kept.
- Scoring: turns, varied tricks, clean landings, barrel time; repeats earn less (each repeat half). Kept.
- Sound and music: the ocean, rushing water, the board's hiss, a deep muffled roar in the barrel opening up on the way out,
  a relaxed island tune (its own Music switch). Kept.
- Vibration: light pulses for stands, moves and landings, a stronger one for a wipeout; optional, with camera shake. Kept.
- Failure: playful wipeouts with a one-line reason ("caught by the break", "lost balance", "landed sideways"), and the next
  wave at once. Kept.
- Replay: short scored sessions, the lessons as trick challenges, **Free surf** (endless, no target), boards to unlock with
  stars, upgrades that change the handling (thruster fins, soft rails, fresh wax). Kept.

## Engagement factors
P5 teach by doing (three lessons: catch and ride, snaps, barrels), P6 every input answers back (spray, trail, hiss), P12
celebrations scaled (a small chime for a carve, a big burst for an air or a barrel), P18 combos and streaks (linked moves),
P26 variety with rhythm (waves with sections, the repeat penalty).
- L1 understanding: ride the wave, stay ahead of the white water. L2 controls: one stick, one button, a swipe.
- L3 fair: fixed waves per level, warnings before fast sections and barrels. L4 near goal: the target bar.
- L5 improvement: scores, stars, best. L6 variety: five moves that each change how you ride. L7 choices: risk an air or a
  barrel, or play safe. L8 retry: the next wave comes at once. L9 yours: boards and upgrades. L10 your best score per beach.

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
Blind moves: `u`/`d` push up/down, `r` along, `a` anywhere, `c` hold CROUCH/PADDLE a while, `w` swipe. The same move again
scores half; a wipeout keeps only a quarter; the target needs a mix. Measured: every blind string of up to four moves (46,528
games) won 5; random moves 0 of 480; the first lesson is not finished by 90 s of blind tapping.
