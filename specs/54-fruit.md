# 54. Fruit Fight

## The idea (his, 19 Nov 2026, set 9)
"Face approaching monsters from a first-person view and shoot fruit at them using a slingshot. Fruits include watermelons,
oranges and bananas, with different damage levels. You must stop the monsters before they reach you." With his tips: fruits
with distinct abilities (melons burst, oranges fire quickly, bananas make monsters slip), monsters with behaviours and
weaknesses, a satisfying pull-aim-release, rewards for accuracy, weak points and multi-hits, close calls, fruits and monsters
brought in gradually, and bigger monsters that need a different plan. 3D, first person.

## How it plays (one thumb)
Your slingshot's fork at the bottom of the screen. Pull back anywhere: sideways to aim the other way, further down to send it
higher and further; a dotted arc shows where it will land (with a ring, wide for a melon). Let go to fire. Tap a fruit to load
it: 🍊 oranges reload fast and hit lightly; 🍉 a watermelon reloads slowly and bursts over everything near where it lands; 🍌 a
banana knocks a monster over (and a peel on the grass trips the next one).
Monsters come up the meadow: blobs (plodding), scuttlers (quick), shield-trolls (a board stops flat shots: lob over it or burst
a melon by it), hoppers (big bounds: hit them in the air, or leave a peel where they land), big ones (tough; the glowing eye on
top takes triple). One that reaches you takes a heart (three hearts). A shot that hits two or more is a combo. Clear every wave.

## Levels
Oranges only at first, melons from 2, bananas from 3; scuttlers from 2, shields from 3, big ones from 4, hoppers from 5; more
waves, more and quicker monsters, shorter gaps later; every tenth level ends with two big ones. Four places, weather by level.
Help: slower monsters.

## Systems
Three fruits with reloads, five monsters, combos, hearts, accuracy, the Fruit Stall (Strong band, Bigger crate, Picket fence),
ranks from Fruit picker to Fruit legend, best combos per level.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `s` (a pull some way and let go), `f` (tap a fruit).
- Random shots miss and monsters arrive: blind 0 of 1,984, random 0 of 480.

## Lessons
1. PULL AND LET GO: splat three blobs. 2. THE WATERMELON: hit two or more with one melon. 3. OVER THE SHIELD: lob two over a board.

## Engagement factors chosen
P7 risk and reward (the slow melon or quick oranges, a lob or a flat shot); P1 a skill that grows (the arc in your thumb);
P3 honest feedback (the arc, BLOCKED, EYE ×3); P14 the near goal (wave k of n, monsters left); P10 sound (twang, splat, slide-whistle).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: pull back and let go: everyone knows a slingshot.
- L2: the band stretches with your thumb; the fruit flies the instant you let go; juice on every hit.
- L3: the arc shows where it lands; misses are yours.
- L4: wave k of n, monsters left, hearts.
- L5: your best combos on each level.
- L6: three fruits, five monsters, four places and weathers.
- L7: which fruit for which monster, lob or flat.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the Fruit Stall, ranks.
- L10: your best on the home card.
