# 10. Wild Water

## The idea (mine, for set 1)
A kayak down a whitewater river, seen from the cockpit: the bow in front, the paddle in your gloves. Two thumbs are the two blades.
The two-thumb, medium-intense game of the set (intensity 4), between the calm fishing and flying games and the intense caveman hunt.

## How it plays (two thumbs)
- A swipe down on the left half is a left stroke: it drives you on and swings the bow right; on the right half, a right stroke
  swings it left. Swipe up to back-paddle (brake, swing toward that side). A quick tap is a light stroke. Both thumbs together work
  (the second stroke waits for the first).
- The current carries you; an angled hull ferries across it. Rows of rocks with one gap each (the gap moves row to row), fallen trees
  on the outside of bends (the current pushes you there), ledges to go over straight and through the smooth tongue (spray
  everywhere else), slalom gates for stars.
- Three smashes (or rolls) and it is over; three glancing knocks count as one smash.

## Levels
The river gets longer (330 m to 900), faster, narrower, the gaps smaller and further apart from row to row, ledges from run 3,
straighter needed over them; every tenth run is The Gorge (longer). Weather by run: morning, noon, golden, grey, rain, mist, sunset,
storm, dawn.

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
Blind moves: left, right, back-left, back-right strokes. The rows of rocks are tight (only the gap lets a boat through) and the gap
moves, so no beat of strokes finds it; strokes on one side over-turn more each time; glancing knocks add up.

## Lessons
1: left stroke (3), right stroke (3), find the gap (3). 2: back-paddle (2), through the gates (2). 3: over the ledge, straight (2).
Slow current in the lessons; nothing lost; the real run starts from the top once they are done.

## Engagement factors chosen
P4 fair failure (every smash says why; the ledge shows as a line with spray beside the tongue), P6 every input answers back
(the blade dips, spray, a "chuk"), P14 progress bar with the gates on it, P13 best time per run, P18 rows-clean streak.
