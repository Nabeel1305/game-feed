# Hunter, third version (0.3.0): animals with joints that run or charge; the throw

## His words (the Game Review page, 23 Nov 2026)
"Once again horrible animation with the animal and the spear throw. Plus the animal  just waits for me to kill it it should run away or
even attack if I run or come too close or throw a spear. The animation must better fot animal and spear"

## What was wrong (the evidence)
- The animal was one capsule with four cylinders that swung from the hip, a capsule neck and a capsule head (pictures of 23 Nov:
  a dark sausage on sticks).
- Nothing noticed you unless you moved while it looked or ran for several seconds: you could creep to 5 m of a grazing deer and stand there.
- The spear appeared above the top of the screen and came down; the arm did not move.

## Research (I cannot play other games; I studied how they are written about)
- A hunting prototype discussed on the Unreal forum spooks its deer when the player gets too close; Ecos La Brea shows a meter that
  fills as you move until the animal is spooked (The Escapist's guide): the ring and the alert bar are that.
- Vintage Story's players asked for animals that flee or turn on you when hit from afar, for "fair warning before an animal attacks",
  and said a boar's aggression should be defensive with a short pursuit: hence the pawing before a charge, and one pass, not a chase.
- theHunter: Call of the Wild's AI designer wrote that she began by learning real animal behaviour species by species: each kind here
  has its own distance and temper.
Sources: forums.unrealengine.com/t/deer-ai-player-avoidance/124258; escapistmagazine.com/?p=309866;
vintagestory.at/forums/topic/20591, /topic/11784, /topic/20826; gamedeveloper.com (theHunter: Call of the Wild, designing believable animal AI).

## A blind thumb
The first version let you spear a charging animal. The robot that only presses THROW then won 13 games in 1,000 (a miss made the boar
charge, and a spear thrown at a charge met it). Throwing at a charge was taken out: the answer to a charge is the dive.

## L1-L10 in a line each
L1 the metres tell you when to throw and when you are too near. L2 the stick does what it did, and the dive is the same stick. L3 an
attack is announced and has one named answer. L4 the animal and its eye are always in view. L5 best throw as before. L6 five kinds that
behave differently. L7 creep closer for a steadier spear, or keep back from its ring; provoke a boar on purpose for the winded throw.
L8 retry as before. L9 unchanged. L10 best distance on the card.
