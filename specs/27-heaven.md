# 27. Heaven or Hell

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His list: "Heaven or hell: souls arrive with their worst and best deed; you judge." Calm (2), one thumb, 2D on purpose: the
soul's card is the game, and a flat, readable card beats a 3D room. Set 4's rule: several parts and systems, easy controls.

## How it plays (one thumb: a drag and a tap)
- A soul's card: a face, a name, how they died and at what age; the BEST thing they did (gold, with its halos, 1 to 3) and
  the WORST (red, with its horns, 1 to 3); the deeds SEALED in the Book of Life (a scroll each, worth one halo or one horn).
- **Drag the card up** to Heaven (more halos), **down** to Hell (more horns), **sideways** to Purgatory (exactly even).
- **Tap the Book** to unseal a soul's hidden deeds: only a few looks a day. When the visible count is far from even the sealed
  deeds cannot change it, so a good gatekeeper saves looks for close calls.
- **Today's rule** (a scroll under the gates, from day 3; two rules from day 15): deeds of one kind (animals, family, money,
  truth, courage, work) count double; or mercy for children under 12; or rest for those over 100; or Purgatory closed.
- **Liars** (from day 6): their eyes dart and their best deed's ink shimmers; the Book shows it FORGED (and adds the lie).
- The sun crosses the sky: souls still waiting at sunset count as wrong. Three wrong judgements and you are sent home.
- A wrong judgement shows where the soul belonged and why (the sealed deeds, the rule): fair, readable failure.

## The systems that carry over (set 4)
- **Karma and blessings:** every day pays karma (a bonus for judgements in a row). Six blessings that stay: second sight (one
  more look a day), grace (one more mistake forgiven), long summer (the sun sets later), lantern (deeds today's rule doubles
  glow), clerk's quill (forged deeds smudge where you can see them), choir (more karma for runs).
- **Rank:** Gatekeeper, Cherub, Angel, Archangel, Seraph, Saint.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever
More souls (14 to 20), more sealed deeds and more close calls, fewer looks for them, a faster sun, more liars, two rules at
once. Robots: ordinary nearly all of 1 to 5 and most of 10 to 20.

## Spamming cannot win
Blind moves: drag up, down, sideways; tap the Book. Three ways for every soul and only two mistakes allowed in fourteen or more
souls, an even mix of Heaven and Hell with some Purgatory, and sunset counting unjudged souls as wrong (doing nothing loses).

## Lessons
1: weigh the deeds (no sealed ones): four right in a row. 2: the Book and Purgatory: four close calls right. 3: today's rule.

## Engagement factors chosen
P4 fair, readable failure (where the soul belonged, and why), P5 teach by doing, P13 visible personal best (best run in a row),
P18 combos and streaks (a choir note higher with each right judgement), P20 gentle palettes (a sky from dawn to dusk).
Beside them, set 4's carry-over systems: blessings and rank.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A close call unsealed in the Book, then the card rising into the golden light.
