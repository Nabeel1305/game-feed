# 85. Hoop Shot

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His words (20 Nov 2026, idea 13, full text in `specs/his-list-2026-11-20.md`): "play basketball purely through shooting,
with satisfying aiming and increasingly interesting shot challenges." With directions for the view, controls, the round,
feedback, challenges, progression, replay and sound; built from them point by point (below). 3D: the court in front of you
and the ball's flight are the game.

## Research (20 Nov 2026; I cannot play these myself, so I studied store pages and reviews)
- Swish Shot!, Streetball Game, World Basketball King: swipe the ball toward the hoop; the swipe's speed and direction set
  the shot. Easy to learn, but a fast flick is hard to repeat exactly. (App Store and store pages)
- Basketmania and BasketBlitz: hold on the ball and slide to set power and direction, dots mark the start of the path, let
  go to shoot: a slower, repeatable gesture. (App Store pages)
- Basketball Time: praised for factoring in both timing and angle and for its statistics; "easy to learn but challenging to
  master". (App Store, TouchArcade)
- Baller Legends: reviewers found a swipe-length bar and wind "a little clunky" and unbalanced. (Pocket Gamer)
- What I took: his drag-back-and-release (Basketmania's slower, repeatable slingshot pull rather than a flick); dots of the
  path (all of it early, then shorter, then none); feedback that says exactly what was wrong; streak statistics.

## His directions, point by point
- View and environment: the camera stands behind the ball facing the hoop, the basket always in view. A neighbourhood court
  (golden hour) first, then an old gym, a rooftop at dusk, a big arena at night (five levels each, in turn).
- Controls: thumb down anywhere, pull back (down the screen) for power, move sideways to aim, let go to shoot. The same
  gesture always. A power bar and the path's dots while you pull (the whole path on levels 1-3, half to level 8, a fifth to
  level 14, then none; Sharp eye upgrade lengthens it).
- Basic round: a run of shooting spots and a number of baskets to make with the balls you have; close and straight first,
  then angled and longer (2.2 m to 7.2 m, up to 43 degrees off).
- Shot feedback: a clean basket (nothing but net) is a SWISH: a crisp net sound, the net jumps, sparks; rim shots bounce on
  a real ring; a miss says TOO STRONG, TOO WEAK, TOO FAR LEFT or RIGHT, or OFF THE RIM, and the last shot's path stays faint
  on the screen while you aim the next.
- Interesting challenges, kept apart from ordinary shooting (every fifth level): BANK SHOTS (only baskets off the board
  count), MOVING SPOT (you slide while you shoot and the ball carries the slide), AROUND THE WORLD (score once from each of
  five marked spots).
- Progression: harder courts in turn; the challenges; PRACTICE on the home screen: unlimited balls, the whole path shown.
- Replay appeal: best coins per level, the best streak ever (kept), streaks and swish streaks on screen and on the end card;
  SHOOT AGAIN in one tap.
- Sound and music: a light rhythmic beat, ball bounces, the board's thud, rim clangs, the net's swish, sneaker squeaks between
  spots; the crowd cheers louder with your streak.

## His rules for every game (20 Nov 2026), kept
- The pull starts wherever the thumb lands, so nothing covers the hoop.
- Fun within ten seconds: lesson 1 is three close spots with the whole path shown.
- One thing at a time: power (lesson 1), aim (lesson 2), distance with a shorter path (lesson 3).
- Autosave, pause, separate Music, Sound effects and Vibration (Settings); original courts, no teams or brands.

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
- Blind moves (BLIND in part2): `f` a pull of any strength any way, `g` the same middling straight pull every time.
- The power window for a basket is a few hundredths wide and changes with every spot.

## Lessons of the first three levels
1. PULL BACK, LET GO. 2. AIM SIDEWAYS. 3. FURTHER OUT.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK: 3 to 5 principles, the sound rules)
- P6 Every input answers back: the dots and the power bar move with your thumb.
- P3 Honest near-miss: "too strong / too weak / left / right" from the real flight, and the faint path of the last shot.
- P12 Celebrations scaled: BUCKET, BANK, SWISH, ON FIRE x3, the crowd.
- P13 Visible personal best: best streak ever, best coins per level.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1 A hoop and a ball. L2 One pull. L3 The miss says why. L4 Baskets to make, balls left. L5 Streaks, swishes, best streak.
- L6 Four courts, three challenges. L7 Upgrades (sharp eye, soft touch, extra ball, fans). L8 SHOOT AGAIN is one tap.
- L9 Eight balls, rank, coins. L10 Best streak ever.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A long shot in the arena dropping through with a swish, the net jumping and the crowd up.
