# 74. Beat Dash

## The idea (set 13: mine, the intense one)
A cube runs to the right by itself, in time with the music; one tap jumps. Spikes, blocks, pits, pads, rings and gravity
portals, every one on the beat. Like Geometry Dash, one of the most addictive one-tap games ever made, because the music
teaches you the course and "one more go" is always a second away. 2D on purpose: a side-on run reads best flat, in neon.

## How it plays (one thumb)
Tap anywhere to jump; keep it held to jump again each time you land.
- **Spikes** and the **sides of blocks** crash you; land on top of blocks. **Pits** have no floor.
- **Yellow pads** throw you high by themselves. **Yellow rings** in the air: a fresh tap while passing through jumps again.
- **Hanging spikes** over the floor: do not jump under them.
- **Blue portals** flip gravity: you run on the ceiling, and a jump goes downward; a yellow portal flips you back.
- **Lives** (5 on the first levels, then 4, then 3) and **flags** at 25%, 50%, 75%: a crash puts you back at the last flag.
- **Secret coins** sit in risky spots.
- The music is the course: a kick on every beat (the obstacles sit on beats), a bass line, a lead; it picks up from the
  flag after a crash.

## Levels
Faster (118 BPM up to 150, the cube a third faster), longer (about 56 beats up to 120), closer obstacles, more double and
triple spikes, wider pits, pads from level 2, rings from 3, hanging spikes from 4, portals from 6. Every tenth is a boss
run (a quarter longer). Four looks (Neon City, Ocean Pulse, Lava Beat, Jungle Drum). Help after three losses: two more lives.

## Systems
Progress %, your best % marked on the bar, flags, lives, secret coins, best score per level, stars (win; at most one crash;
no crash), the Cube Shop (Extra life, More flags, Coin sense), four cubes opened by stars, ranks from Beginner to Legend.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `t` tap; `h` press and hold; `r` let go.
- Holding jumps into the spikes on landing and into hanging spikes; random taps fall in pits and hit walls. Measured: 0 of
  2,240 beat patterns and 0 of 1,000 random runs win (blind runs end around 10% of the course).
- Lessons start again on any crash.

## Lessons
1. TAP TO JUMP: single and double spikes, blocks.
2. PADS AND RINGS: pads over walls, rings over gaps.
3. DON'T JUMP!: hanging spikes, then a portal onto the ceiling.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P23 instant restart (back at the flag in half a second), P13 the best % always marked on the bar, P3 honest near-miss (the
% you reached, "so close"), P10 sound with the beat (the music is the course), P30 the signature: the beat-synced course.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: a cube, a spike, TAP TO JUMP.
- L2: the jump is instant and always the same height; the screen pulses on the beat.
- L3: every crash is a spike you touched or a jump you mistimed, and you see where.
- L4: the progress bar with its flags.
- L5: your best % marked; flags passed glow green.
- L6: pads, rings, hanging spikes, portals, faster music, four looks.
- L7: go for the secret coin or play safe; jump early or late in the window.
- L8: a crash is back at the flag in half a second.
- L9: your cubes, the Cube Shop, your rank.
- L10: your best % and score on every level.

## Sound (S1-S11)
The music (kick, hats, bass, a lead, a chord every eight beats); a short blip for a jump, a ding for a ring, a boing for a
pad, a whoosh for a portal, a chime for a flag, a crunch for a crash.

## The card clip
The cube hopping a triple spike on the beat, a ring over a pit, a portal flip onto the ceiling, all in neon.
