# 18. Lighthouse

## The idea (mine, for the calm side of the feed)
Set 2 needed a calm game beside Police Chase and Pilot. A wild night at sea, seen from the gallery at the top of a lighthouse
on the cliffs, in 3D: the beam reaching over a dark sea that moves, rain and lightning in a storm, stars and a moon on a clear
night, mist. Little fishing boats are lost out in the dark, each just a lamp; you are the light that brings them home.
Calm (1), one thumb.

## How it plays (one thumb: the thumb is the lamp)
- Touch the sea where you want the light; slide to move it. The lamp swings at its own pace. Hold your thumb near the side of
  the screen and the view turns that way with the beam. Arrows at the edge point to boats you cannot see.
- Hold the light on a lost boat until it sees you: its lamp blinks back (and its horn answers). From then on it follows your
  light, at a boat's pace. Move the light too fast and it loses you.
- Lead it to the harbour mouth (a red and a green light on the breakwaters); there it sees the harbour lights and goes in by
  itself. Rocks show only in your light, by their surf: lead round them.
- Boats you are not lighting drift towards the rocky shore. Several boats can follow the one light: fetch the next one with
  the first in tow and bring them home together.
- The night is won when the boats are in and you still have a lifebuoy; each wreck costs one (the night can take about four
  in ten of its boats).

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever (LESSONS rule 0)
Level 1: two boats, one at a time, no rocks. Then more boats a night (up to 7, 8 on every tenth), more at once (up to 3),
more rocks, boats drifting faster, a smaller pool of light, boats slower to notice it and quicker to lose it, a tide setting
them sideways (from level 5). The harbour is on a different side each night; the weather changes by night.
Robots: an ordinary player wins all of 1 to 20 and 9 in 10 of level 40; a sharp one 9 in 10 of level 40, 8 in 10 of level 80.

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
- Blind moves (BLIND in part2): touch anywhere on the sea, slide the light left, right, out or in, hold still.
- A boat sees the light only when it is held on it, and follows only if it is moved slowly; boats left alone drift on to
  the rocks. Every blind string of up to four moves on 8 beats on levels 1, 2, 3 and 6: 0 of 46,528. Random moves: 0 of 480.
- First found: doing nothing at all "won" level 2 (two boats wrecked, a life left). The night now needs most of its boats home.

## Lessons of the first three levels
1: find the boat and hold the light on it until it blinks; lead it home. 2: round the rocks. 3: two boats at once.
In a lesson a boat that touches a rock or the shore bumps off instead of wrecking.

## Engagement factors chosen
P6 every input answers back (the boat's lamp blinks and its horn answers), P10 sound with a visual twin, P18 boats home in a
row (the chord climbs), P19 in spirit (no clock: boats drift slowly), P20 a gentle palette (night blues, one warm light),
P30 the beam itself and the answering horn.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A storm: lightning, the beam finds a lost boat, its lamp blinks back, and it follows the light between two rocks into the
harbour; the bell.
