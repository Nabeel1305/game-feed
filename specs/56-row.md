# 56. Oarsmen

## The idea (mine, set 10)
A rowing race down a river. There was no rowing or water-racing game, and it is a two-thumb game in a set that is mostly
one-thumb. 3D on purpose: the river bending away in front of you, the trees on the banks, the rival boats and the weather
are the game.

## How it plays (two thumbs)
Your left thumb is your left oar, your right thumb your right oar. Each oar has a ring that fills as it comes back, and turns
green when it is ready. Tap on the green for a perfect stroke; too early is weak, much too early "catches a crab" (the oar
digs in and slows you). Both thumbs together (within a moment of each other) drive the boat straight and build RHYTHM,
each step of rhythm a little more speed. One oar alone turns the boat: the left oar turns you right, the right oar left.
That is how you steer round the bends, the rocks and the logs. Touching the bank or a rock costs speed and your rhythm.
Race the other boats to the finish buoys; first wins.

## Levels
The course gets longer (300 m to 750 m), the bends tighter, the river narrower, more rocks and, from level 4, logs. One
rival at first, up to three; they get faster. Four places in turn (The Willow Reach, The Gorge, The Lowland Canal, The Lake
Run) and ten kinds of weather from morning to storm. Help after losses: slower rivals.

## Systems
Stroke timing (crab, early, perfect, late), rhythm that builds and breaks, steering by one oar, obstacles, rival boats,
places, weather, stars for place and perfect strokes, the Boathouse (Wide blades, Slim hull, A cox for a longer green
window), ranks from Novice to Olympian.

## Controls
Tap the left half or the right half of the screen. Nothing else: no aiming, no dragging.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `l`, `r`, `b` (both) at fixed beats.
- Steady both-thumb tapping goes straight down the middle and into the first bend's bank; random tapping catches crabs.
  Blind 4 of 5,696 (the closest pattern, 8 of 100), random 0 of 480.

## Lessons
1. TOGETHER: six perfect strokes together. 2. STEER: 150 m of bends without touching the bank (one oar turns you).
3. RHYTHM: reach a rhythm of 5.

## Engagement factors chosen
P3 timing that feels good (the green window, a perfect stroke's surge); P2 rhythm that builds; P11 rivals in sight;
P8 places and weather that change; P10 sound (the splash and pull, a rising note with the rhythm).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: two oars, two thumbs: you see the boat and the rings.
- L2: the oar pulls and the boat surges the instant you tap.
- L3: the ring shows exactly when the oar is ready; a crab is always your tap, never luck.
- L4: your place and the metres to go are always on screen, with a strip showing every boat.
- L5: your best time on each level, and stars.
- L6: bends, rocks, logs, rivals and weather change where and when you steer.
- L7: steer now and lose rhythm, or hold the line and risk the bank.
- L8: Try again is one tap and you are on the water.
- L9: the Boathouse and your rank.
- L10: your best time on the home card.

## The card clip
Rhythm at its top, the boat surging past a rival round a bend in golden light, the finish banner ahead.
