# 14. Police Chase

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His list (17 Nov 2026): "Police chase: a car chase, then the criminal runs on foot; run, catch him, he tries to break free, into
the car." The intense game of set 2 (intensity 4), two thumbs. A city avenue at night (rain, storm, fog, dusk...): towers of lit
windows, wet tarmac, street lamps, traffic, your patrol car's light bar throwing red and blue, a two-tone siren.

## How it plays: three parts, one chase
- **Pursuit:** hold the left side to steer left, the right side right, both to brake. Weave through traffic (and cars crossing at
  junctions from case 4) and ram the getaway car's back corner from the side: a PIT spins him. Into his bumper does nothing.
  1 PIT on the first cases, up to 4 (5 on every tenth case). Fall 150+ m behind for 4 s and he is gone.
- **On foot:** down a brick alley, running by yourself. Swipe left/right round crates, up over fences, down under pipes; a stumble
  costs ground. Right behind him, tap to tackle; too far and you dive at air.
- **Arrest:** he twists one way (shoulders turn: the tell; an arrow on the first cases); swipe the other way to hold him. Wrong way,
  late, or flailing when he is still: he breaks free and the foot chase starts again.
- Three lives: a crash or a break-free costs one.

## Spamming cannot win
Holds and swipes blind: 0 of 182,976 blind strings and 0 of 480 random. Steering on a beat crashes; a PIT needs the right place
and a steer into him; swipes on a beat stumble; taps from far dive at air; a swipe when he is still counts as flailing.

## Lessons
1: steer through traffic (3 cars passed), the PIT (forgiving). 2: dodge, jump, slide (4), tackle. 3: hold him (3).

## Engagement factors chosen
P4 fair failure (every crash and stumble says why), P7 hit-stop on the PIT and the tackle, P14 three steps on the screen
(pursuit, on foot, arrest), P13 fastest arrest per case, P30 signature (the light bar washing the screen, the siren).
