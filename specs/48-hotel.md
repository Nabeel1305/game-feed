# 48. Grand Hotel

## The idea (his, 18 Nov 2026, set 8)
"You're a hotel attendant delivering room service, cleaning rooms and attending to guests."
2D on purpose: a doll's-house cutaway, where every room, bubble and patience ring reads at once on a phone.

## How it plays (one thumb)
The hotel is a cutaway at dusk: the lobby at the bottom (front desk, kitchen, laundry), two to four floors of three rooms
above, the lift up the left. Requests pop up as bubbles with a ring that empties green to red: 🧳 a guest at the desk (carry
the bags to a clean room), 🍝 room service (from the kitchen), 🧺 towels (from the laundry), 🧹 a room left dirty after a
check-out (clean it; a new guest needs a clean room). Tap a bubble to take the job. You hold three jobs, done in the order
you took them (they show along the bottom with the room number), and your hands carry two things. At the kitchen or laundry
you fetch for every job of that kind you hold: two plates, one trip. The attendant walks and rides the lift by himself.
Tips grow with how fast you are and climb in a streak. A guest left too long costs a star; a job you took and then failed
costs twice (you promised); letting a job go (tap it again, or tap it in the bar) disappoints them. Make the tips target by
the end of the 100-second shift; under three stars the manager sends you home.

## Levels
Two floors at first, three from level 3, four from 12. Food and bags at level 1, towels from 2, check-outs and cleaning
from 3 (before that housekeeping cleans), VIPs (gold, double tips, shorter patience) from 4. More jobs and less patient
guests as it goes. The shift is dealt (fixed shares of each kind, spread through the shift) and the target is a share of
what the shift's jobs could tip, so skill decides it. Help: guests wait longer and the target is lower.

## Systems
Promise and deliver (the three-job memory, two hands, double fetches, clean before check-in), the hotel's stars, VIPs,
streaks, the Staff Room (Soft shoes, Trolley for a third hand, Lift service, Warm smile), ranks from Bellhop to Hotelier,
four hotels (the Seaview to the Grand Palace).

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind move: `t` (a tap anywhere in the hotel: it may take or let go of a job).
- Taking everything as it comes used to win; now letting a job go costs patience and a quarter star, failing a job you took
  costs a whole star, and three stars is the floor: blind 0 of 448, random 0 of 480. Ordinary robots (that take the job
  that will run out first, group kinds, and only promise what they can do in time) win 90-100% of levels 1-3.

## Lessons
1. BAGS UP: two guests, the VIP first. 2. ONE TRIP: two food orders, both taken before the kitchen. 3. A CLEAN ROOM: the
dirty room first, then the guest. Letting a job go is a miss. Skip tutorial on every lesson; Tutorial on home and pause.

## Engagement factors chosen
P14 the near goal (tips bar against the target, the clock); P7 risk and reward (take a third job or not: a failed promise
costs double); P5 flow (a steady rhythm of bells and tips); P3 honest feedback (every tip shows, every lost guest shows why);
P10 sound (desk bell, phone trill, lift ding, a tip chord that climbs in a streak).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: bubbles above rooms, a ring that empties: everyone knows waiting guests.
- L2: one tap takes a job; the attendant does the walking.
- L3: lost guests show why (too slow, a promise broken); no luck in the shift's mix.
- L4: the tips bar and the clock.
- L5: your best shift's tips on each level.
- L6: four kinds of job, VIPs, check-outs, more floors.
- L7: which job first, and whether to promise a third.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the Staff Room, ranks.
- L10: your best shift on the home card.

## The card clip
Rush hour: three bubbles at once, two plates fetched in one trip, a +£30 streak.
