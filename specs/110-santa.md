# 110. Santa's Round (his idea 31, set 20)

## The idea
His words: play as Santa, fly the sleigh between houses, go down chimneys and deliver the correct presents before moving to
the next neighbourhood. Intensity 2 (the calm one of his four in this set), two thumbs, played sideways.

**3D:** yes. The snowy village at night under the northern lights, the sleigh coming down onto a roof, and the cosy room seen
from above are the charm of it.

## Research
I cannot play other games myself, so I read store pages and players' comments of the Santa games:
- **Ho-Ho-Home Invasion** (itch.io page, OpenCritic review): arrive, deliver, leave without being seen. Players said sneaking
  was hardly slower than walking so there was no point to it, and that it was too hard early with no difficulty option.
  So here: tiptoeing is half speed and silent, running is fast and loud (a real choice); the first levels have no creaks or
  cats; help after losses lengthens the night; and the relaxed round has no noise and no dawn.
- **Secret Santa**, **Santa is Real** (Steam pages), **Sneaky Santa: Gift Heist** (CrazyGames: tiptoe on a held key): Santa
  stealth works; the best ones make quiet movement a held, deliberate choice. Kept: a gentle push tiptoes.
- **Chimney Hop**, **Santa's Present Run**, **Santa's Busy Day** (App Store and store pages): the sleigh-and-chimney part is
  usually a one-button drop. Here the sleigh is flown, and landing is a held button over a big glowing marker (forgiving).
- **Santa vs. The Robbers** (itch.io): fly over the chimney and press a button to hop down; when done, back to the sleigh.
  The same simple loop here: land, down the chimney, back up.

Sources: whitepotstudios.itch.io/hohohomeinvasion, opencritic.com/kervin619/review/10814/ho-ho-home-invasion,

## How it plays (two thumbs, sideways)
- **Flight:** the left thumb's stick steers the sleigh (up is ahead). The next house has a glowing ring on its roof and a soft
  beam above it; the list of tonight's houses is top left (any order). Hold LAND over the ring: the sleigh slows and settles.
  Off the ring it bounces back up to try again.
- **Down the chimney:** narrow chimneys need three taps of ACT to wriggle down; icy roofs need a push up as you land.
- **Inside (from above):** a gentle push tiptoes; a full push runs (fast, loud). The wish list (top left) says who wants what;
  tap a present in the sack (bottom) to hold it, ACT by the tree to put it down; ACT by the tree with empty hands picks the
  last one back up. ACT by each stocking to fill it, ACT by the fireplace to go back up when all is right.
- **Noise:** running, bumping furniture, creaky boards (darker planks), a curious cat (ACT near it to toss a toy mouse). The
  meter on the right; past halfway the sleeper stirs; full, someone wakes and that house starts again (earlier ones stay).
- **Win:** every house delivered before dawn (the moon bar fills towards sunrise). Stars: nobody woken and time to spare.

## His directions, point by point
- View: snowy village, glowing windows, lights along the eaves, falling snow, the northern lights. Kept (cities' apartment
  blocks come in as taller houses from level 10).
- Flight controls: left joystick, a right button to slow and land, forgiving landings with a visible marker. Kept.
- Inside: a smooth switch to an elevated view; the stick walks, one contextual button (ACT) for stockings, presents, the
  cat and the chimney. Kept (doors are left out: one room per house).
- Basic round: read the list, reach the house, choose the right gifts, place them under the tree, back to the sleigh. Kept.
- Gift selection: names on stockings and an illustrated wish list, always on screen. Kept.
- Sneaking: running, bumping and dropping make noise, a meter, stirring sleepers and creaks as warnings. Kept.
- Playful obstacles: narrow chimneys, icy roofs, curious cats (toss a toy). Kept (awkwardly shaped presents are not in).
- Satisfying deliveries: presents appear wrapped under the tree, stockings fill out, delivered houses glow gold. Kept.
- Choices: you pick the order; houses differ in their roofs and chimneys. Kept.
- Progression: one small house with one or two gifts first; more houses, bigger families, creaks (3), cats (4), narrow
  chimneys (6), icy roofs (8), taller buildings (10). Kept.
- Sound and music: sleigh bells, snow crunch, a crackling fire, footsteps, snores, creaks, a meow; a gentle tune that softens
  indoors. Kept.
- Vibration: light pulses for landings, presents and stockings, a stronger one for a delivered house; optional. Kept.
- Fair mistakes: wrong presents can be picked back up; a woken house restarts on its own. Kept.
- Replay: short missions (a neighbourhood per level), the dawn timer, a relaxed round (no noise, no dawn), sleighs to unlock
  with stars, upgrades (soft boots, swift reindeer, muffled bells). Kept.

## Engagement factors
P1 just hard enough (the noise meter and the night bar), P4 fair, readable failure (each noise says what made it), P12
celebrations scaled (a chime per present, a jingle per house), P16 natural stopping points (one neighbourhood a level),
P20 gentle palettes (warm lights on a blue night).
- L1 understanding: fly to the glowing house, give the right presents. L2 controls: one stick, one button, a tap.
- L3 fair: warnings before anyone wakes. L4 near goal: houses left, presents left. L5 improvement: stars, wake-ups.
- L6 variety: cats, creaks, chimneys, icy roofs. L7 choices: order, tiptoe or run. L8 retry: a house restarts on its own.
- L9 yours: sleighs and upgrades. L10 your best stars per neighbourhood.

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
Blind moves: `f` push forward, `a` push anywhere, `t` a gentle push anywhere, `o` let go, `l` hold LAND a while, `c` ACT,
`p` tap a random present. A house only counts when exactly the wished presents are under the tree and every stocking is full;
leaving is refused until then. Measured: every blind string of up to four moves (77,504 games) won none; random moves 0 of
480; the first lesson is not finished by 90 s of blind tapping.
