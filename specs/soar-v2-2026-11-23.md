## Was, and now
| | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| The rings | one yellow tube each | a hoop of light: a bright core, a glow, a halo, a pearly film that turns, twelve gems that travel round it; each ring is the next colour of the rainbow; one flown through blooms and fades |
| Finding the next ring | it was brighter | a trail of sparks runs from you to it and on to the two after it |
| You | a small figure seen feet first | a crimson cape with a gold hem and a gold clasp, rippling from collar to tail, its corners flapping, billowing in a turn, leaving sparks |
| The sea | dark teal | brighter; turquoise shallows and white foam round every island |
| The land | dull green, some dark pines | pale sand, three greens, meadows of pink, yellow and violet flowers, warm rock, snow; pines above, broad-leaved trees below (one in eight in blossom), palms on the beaches |
| The sky and the distance | flat clouds, gulls | heaped clouds at every height, hot-air balloons in stripes, sailing boats, a rainbow |
| Speed | 22 m/s on every flight | 11 m/s on flight 1, 13 on flight 3, 16 on flight 10, about 22 on flight 40 |
| Rings | 9.5 m across, 70 m apart | 11 m across on flight 1, 56 m apart, and the trail bends less |
| The camera | close over the shoulder | further back and higher, so that the whole cape is seen |

## Research (I cannot play other games; I studied how they are written about)
Sky: Children of the Light (thatgamecompany) is the game of a flying cape: its players fly by a cape, and its flight always moves forward
with push down to tilt down and push up to glide up (Gameranx's beginner guide), which is this game's stick. Its reviews dwell on
gliding slowly through clouds and ruins (GameSpot) and on a world made "dreamlike" (Apple's behind-the-scenes story): slow, and
beautiful to be in, which is what he asked for. Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_(video_game); gamespot.com (Sky: Children of the
Light review); gameranx.com (5 beginner tips); apps.apple.com/us/story/id1465180456.
