# 26. Tag

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His list: a game of tag. Intense (4), one thumb, 3D: a schoolyard you can see round you matters (the climbing frame, the
slide, the trees to run round), and kids running about look alive in 3D. Set 4's rule: several parts and systems, easy controls.

## How it plays (one thumb)
- **Drag anywhere to run:** the thumb is a floating stick (it follows the thumb if it wanders); let go and you stop.
- **Tap twice quickly: DASH,** a burst of speed; a ring round your feet fills while it comes back.
- **Four games, by level:**
  - **CHASE:** you are IT. Tag every kid before the bell. Kids run from you, use the frame and the trees, and dodge sideways
    when you are right on them.
  - **RUN:** they are IT (two or more, in red bibs). Keep away till the bell. Three hearts: each catch costs one, and the
    chaser is dazed for a moment.
  - **FREEZE:** you are IT. Tagged kids freeze; free kids come and unfreeze their friends with a touch (a ring fills). Freeze
    them all at once.
  - **ZOMBIE TAG:** one kid is IT, and everyone he tags joins him (and they want you most). Be the last one standing.
  - Every tenth level: **THE BIG KID**, faster than you, chases you alone.
- **Power-ups on the ground:** ⚡ speed, 👻 ghost (chasers lose you), 🛡 shield (one catch for free), 🧊 freeze whistle (kids
  near you stop).
- Arrows at the screen's edge point to the kids you cannot see (red: chasing you).

## The systems that carry over (set 4)
- **Tokens and the tuck shop:** every game pays tokens (stars, tags, close shaves). Six upgrades that stay: fast sneakers,
  spring legs (longer dash), big lungs (dash sooner), lucky pockets (power-ups last longer and come more often), an extra heart,
  a friendly bell (more time when you are IT).
- **Rank:** New kid, Sneaky, Speedy, Slippery, Untaggable, Playground legend.
- **Places:** the playground (from 1), the park in autumn (10), the beach (20), the snowy schoolyard (30).

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever
More kids, faster kids and chasers (up to your own speed), smarter (they aim where you will be, cut across when you run in
circles, juke more often), less time when you are IT, more time to last when they are, faster unfreezing.

## Spamming cannot win
Blind moves: the stick held one of eight ways, let go, dash. First found: running a big square round the yard (a fixed
loop) won 28%, because a slower chaser chasing where you are never closes on someone running in circles, and the kids' dash
was eased in so slowly it was only half a dash. Now chasers intercept (aim where you will be), cut across your loop, a dash
is a burst at once, a kid pinned on a wall is not counted as running, zombies want you most, and RUN has two chasers
outside the lesson.

## Lessons
1: drag to run and tag three; then dash to tag the last two (only dash tags count). 2: they are IT: keep away for fifteen
seconds. 3: freeze tag: freeze four, though their friends free them.

## Engagement factors chosen
P5 teach by doing (each game taught as it comes), P6 every input answers back (the dash puff, footsteps, the heartbeat when a
chaser is close), P12 celebrations scaled (TAGGED!, DOUBLE!, three in a row), P18 combos (tags in a row, a note higher each),
P26 variety with rhythm (four games in turn, places, weather, the big kid). Beside them, set 4's carry-over systems: the tuck
shop, rank and places.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A dash tag on the last kid with the bell about to ring.
