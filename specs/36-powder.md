# 36. Powder

## The idea (his words, then mine)
Mine, for set 6 (his notes, 18 Nov 2026: longevity): snowboard down a mountain against the clock, racing the ghost of your own
best run. 3D: the mountain in front of you is the game (pines, peaks, gates, kickers, falling snow, five skies).

## How it plays (one screen, one thumb)
Hold your thumb and slide it left or right to lean and carve (from wherever it went down); let go to go straight and tuck, the
fastest. A quick tap jumps (an ollie, over rocks). Off a kicker you fly: hold a side to spin, let go to stop, land with the board
down the hill (or right round backwards) for a 180/360/540... and a burst of speed; land crooked and you fall. Ride between the
flags of every gate (a miss: +3 s). Trees and rocks knock you down (a fall costs time). Snowflakes on the good line are coins.
Ice patches slip. Beat the clock to the finish; your best run rides beside you as a pale ghost, with a split at every gate.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever (LESSONS rule 0)
Level 1: a short, wide run with no rocks. Then longer runs (420 m to 900 m), a narrower piste, gates closer, narrower and further
apart, more trees on the piste, rocks (2+), kickers (3+), ice (6+), a slightly faster clock. Five places with their own sky.
Help after three losses: 7% more time. Robots: ordinary wins 80-100% of levels 1-10, 60% at 20; sharp 60% at 40.

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
- Blind moves: `l` (a slide some way, or letting go, without looking), `t` (a tap: a jump).
- Gates swing from side to side, so a straight run misses them (+3 s each); rocks and trees wait on the piste; spinning blind
  lands crooked. 1,984 blind-string games and 480 random ones: none won.

## Lessons of the first three levels
1. CARVE: three gates in a row. 2. JUMP: hop two rocks in a row. 3. SPIN: land two spins off kickers.

## Engagement factors chosen
P13 the ghost of your best, on the course bar and on the snow; P14 the course bar with each gate ticked; P3 honest splits
("+0.4 s vs ghost") and "x s too slow"; P18 each gate in a row rings a step higher; P12 a 180 is a pop, a 1080 is a fanfare.
Sounds: wind rising with speed, the edge scraping in a carve, pop and thump for jumps, crunch in a fall, tinkles for flakes.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: slide to carve, let go to fly straight, tap to jump; the first run has only gates.
- L2: the rider crouches, leans into carves, sprays snow; every gate dings; a spin dial in the air shows when to let go.
- L3: rocks, trees, gates and kickers are all in sight long before you reach them; a fall says why (TREE!, ROCK!, CROOKED LANDING!).
- L4: the clock on screen, the course bar to the flag, three stars, the next place.
- L5: the ghost and the splits; "x s faster than your ghost!" at the end.
- L6: kickers, rocks, ice, narrow pistes, five skies.
- L7: the kicker (time lost carving to it, speed won by a trick) or the straight line; a risky 540 or a safe 180.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: boards to open with stars and tricks (Spinner for a 540, Pro 1080), the one you ride is on your feet.
- L10: the ghost of your best run rides beside you.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A 360 off a kicker at dusk, landing clean past the ghost.
