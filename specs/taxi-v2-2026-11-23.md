# Taxi Driver, second version (0.2.0): you drive it

## His words
On Police Chase (23 Nov 2026): "the car controls should be a joystick with a button for break acceleration anf reverse".
On On the Run (23 Nov, 09:49): "when I tell you to fix one thing on one app, you shouldn't just keep to that app. You should think, is
there any other apps that need? So imagine the controls I said on the fixes that I made on police chase. Why don't you apply those to
on the run?" Taxi Driver had the same fault (evidence: its first rule was "Your taxi drives itself"; a swipe changed lane, a swipe in a
"turn zone" turned at the junction; there was no steering and no reverse), so it was changed without asking.

## Was, and now
| | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Steering | the taxi drove itself in one of two lanes; a swipe changed lane | a floating **stick** under the left thumb steers it anywhere on the street |
| Turning | a swipe in the glowing turn zone before a junction | you steer round the corner yourself; the GPS arrow over the junction and the panel still say which way |
| Speed | it accelerated by itself; a thumb held anywhere braked | **GAS** and **BRAKE** pedals under the right thumb; BRAKE held at a standstill (after a moment, so stopping in a ring does not roll back) **reverses** |
| The other side of the road | cars drawn there for show | real cars: cross the yellow line and you can meet one |
| Crashes | the car in front, in your lane | driving into any car, or hard into a kerb or road works; a car that comes up behind a stopped taxi stops for it |
| Red lights | at the junction line | coming into the junction on red |
| A fast corner | turning at over 34 km/h | the same speed, steering hard for a third of a second: a screech, speed scrubbed off |
| Missed the ring? | round the block | round the block, or reverse back to it |
| Fares, the meter, tips, rushers, nervous passengers, tourists and the fountain, the GPS and its map, jams, road works, taxis and upgrades | | unchanged |

## Research (I cannot play other games; I studied how they are written about)
The controls are Police Chase's, researched for it (`specs/police-v2-2026-11-23.md`). The first version of this game followed Crazy
Taxi: City Rush, whose cab drives itself and is swiped between lanes; its own reviewers "felt being led by the nose" took away skill
(`specs/83-taxi.md`), and that is the fault he named. The original Crazy Taxi is driven freely: what was kept from it here is the
arrow that points at the next piece of road and the ring to stop in.

## Rules (part2.js)
- The taxi is at `x, y` (east, north) heading `th`; `locate(G)` works out each frame the road it is on (`i, j, h`), how far along (`s`)
  and how far right of the middle (`lx`), so the fares, the GPS, the lights and the other cars go on working by those.
- `onRoadT` is the street: the grid lines, 7.6 m either side, not past the edge of town, not through road works.
- A junction is entered (red light check; the GPS's turn is noted) and left (the turn you made is counted against it).
- Rings are 20 to 38 m along their road (they were 14 to 40): room to pull over after a corner.
- The meter allows a little more (it was set for a taxi that never slowed for a straight-on junction).
- Lessons: shift 1 GAS, BRAKE and the ring; shift 2 the GPS arrow round two corners; shift 3 passing slow cars.
- The robots steer with a lag on the stick, take the inside lane for a corner and the kerb for a ring; blind play is the stick and the pedals.

## His rules, kept
Large controls that never cover the action; something to do at once (a fare is waving within a block); mistakes explained ("MISSED THEM:
GO ROUND THE BLOCK", "TOO FAST FOR THE CORNER!"); difficulty from lights, jams, road works and passenger kinds; separate Music, Sound
effects and Vibration; tutorial optional.

## Longevity (L1-L10)
Unchanged from `specs/83-taxi.md`, except L2 (two thumbs: a stick and two pedals) and L7 (round the block or reverse; overtake over the line or wait).
