# 79. Cake Designer

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His words (20 Nov 2026): "Cake designer: you design cakes."

Mine: a calm bakery game in 3D. A customer comes in with an order card (their occasion, the tiers, the icing colour, a drip,
the swirls, the toppings), and you make the cake in five stages, each with its own control: STACK, COLOUR, ICE, PIPE, TOP.
The customer scores it out of 100, and a great cake gets a tip. Calm (intensity 1), one thumb, no clock: a customer only
says "Is it ready yet?" and the stage finishes as it is.

## Research (20 Nov 2026; I cannot play these myself, so I studied reviews, guides and store pages)
- Icing on the Cake (one of the best-loved cake games on phones): the cake spins and you smooth the icing round it with a
  finger; reviews call it relaxing and satisfying. (App Store, reviews)
- Cake Maker games: frosting drawn on with your finger. (store pages)
- Bake It: one finger only, with satisfying icing and toppings. (store page)
- Cake Mania: customers' orders, a score for each, and the shop upgraded as you go. (reviews)
- What I took: the spinning turntable with the knife where your thumb is (the heart of it), orders to match, toppings placed
  by hand, and a shop to improve. Each stage gets its own simple control instead of one control for everything.

## How it plays (one screen, one thumb)
- STACK: a tier glides from side to side above the stand; tap to set it down, centred (PERFECT, NICE, A BIT OFF).
- COLOUR: four bowls of icing; tap the one on the card.
- ICE: the cake spins; hold your thumb on it and a palette knife spreads icing where your thumb is; slide up and down to cover
  it all (the meter shows how much; lift at 86% or more, or keep going to 100). A thumb off the cake does nothing.
- PIPE: the cake turns under the piping bag; a dot on the rim marks each swirl's place; tap as it passes under the bag.
- TOP: tap on top of the cake to place each berry, candle, cherry or macaron; faint rings show the spots on early orders.
- The verdict: Stacking, Colour, Icing, Swirls, Toppings, each out of 100, and a score; the wrong colour is not their cake.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever (LESSONS rule 0)
- One tier and 4 swirls first; toppings from level 2; two tiers from 3; drips from 7; three tiers on show cakes (every
  fifth) and later; more swirls (up to 12) and toppings (up to 8). The customer's standard rises from 66 to 95; tiers glide
  faster. Six bakeries in turn. Three losses: an easier customer (help).

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
- Blind moves (BLIND in part2): `t` a tap anywhere, `h` a thumb put down anywhere, `u` a lift.
- The wrong icing (three times in four for a blind thumb) fails the cake; tiers set down blind are off-centre; blind swirls
  miss their dots; blind toppings miss the top or their spots.

## Lessons of the first three levels
1. HOLD TO ICE: ice a cake all over.
2. TAP TO PIPE: 4 swirls on their dots (one off its dot starts again).
3. TAP TO TOP: 4 strawberries on their spots.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK: 3 to 5 principles, the sound rules)
- P21 Tidy-up satisfaction: icing filling in round a spinning cake, swirls landing on their dots.
- P19 No clock, no fail pressure: calm; the customer waits, the music box plays.
- P6 Every input answers back: the knife's "shhh", each swirl a squeeze a step higher, each tier a soft plop.
- P12 Celebrations scaled: the customer's "ooh", a tip for a great cake, DREAM CAKE for 94 and over.
- P13 Visible personal best: your best score on each order.
- Sound: a soft music box over a warm pad, the knife's sweep, squeezes climbing the scale (S5), plops, the shop bell, the till.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1 The order card says what to make; each stage says what to do in one line.
- L2 The cake spins and the icing appears under your thumb.
- L3 The verdict shows each part's score, so a loss says what to improve.
- L4 The stages along the top; the next order; the next stand or upgrade.
- L5 Scores per part, your best per order.
- L6 Tiers, drips, more swirls, different toppings, show cakes, six bakeries.
- L7 Lift early or ice to 100; upgrades (slow turntable, wide knife, steady hands, shop sign).
- L8 BAKE AGAIN is one tap.
- L9 Cake stands (won with stars), rank, coins and upgrades stay.
- L10 Your best score on every order to beat.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
The last swirls piped onto a finished, dripping cake as it spins.
