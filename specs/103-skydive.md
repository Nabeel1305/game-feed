# 103. Skydive (his idea 28, 21 Nov 2026)

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His: jump from an aircraft, control your freefall, open the parachute and land on a target, with beautiful 3D landscapes
below, rings, wind, a flare to land soft, a predicted landing marker, and a relaxed scenic mode.
Mine: one jump is about 50 seconds: out of the door, a dozen seconds of freefall (fly through gold rings if you want them),
open between the yellow marks, then fly the canopy down onto the X, into the wind, and flare. Intensity 2, two thumbs,
played sideways.

**3D:** yes. The land far below and the X coming up at you are the game (his direction asks for it).

## How it plays (two thumbs, sideways)
- **Freefall:** a floating stick under the left thumb. Forward flies you forward (tracking), left or right turns you, back
  slows your forward drift. **Hold TUCK** (right) to dive head-down, faster (64 m/s against 44); let go to spread out.
- **CHUTE** (big right button, tap): the canopy snaps open with a big slowdown. The altimeter on the right shows the yellow
  marks (220-440 m) to open between; the button glows in them. Too low (140 m) and the safety opens it for you (-200).
- **Under the canopy:** the stick turns you (left, right) and pulling back is the brakes (slower forward and down). The
  wind arrow (top left) shows the wind at your height; the ring on the ground shows where you will land if you fly straight
  on (white inside the circle, yellow near the X, red outside; it turns **green** when it is time to flare).
- **Hold FLARE** (the same big button) about a house high: forward speed and sink drop for a couple of seconds. Too early
  and it runs out: a hard landing.
- **Landing:** inside the circle to pass; 1000 points at the X falling off to the edge, +300 soft, +100 fair, +150 a ring,
  -200 if the safety opened it. A hard landing loses from level 4 (levels 1-2 flare for you, his "first jump with
  assistance"). After a miss the path you flew is drawn in yellow behind the end card, and JUMP AGAIN is one tap.
- **Scenic jump** (second home button): no failing, the circle bigger and the wind softer, jump after jump.

## Places and progression
- 1-3 green countryside (morning); 4-7 the islands (land in the sea and it is a splash); 8-11 a mountain valley; 12-16 the
  city (from 12 the X is on a rooftop: off the roof is the street); then the places in turn, and the coast at sunset.
- Circle 55 m -> 24 m; wind 1.5 -> 6.5 m/s; from 5 the wind **turns** below a height you are told ("turns below 180 m")
  and the windsocks and flags show it; rings from 2 (off the direct line: extra points for height and distance); every
  fourth level from 8 is a **ring course** (every ring needed); jumps get higher and the X farther.
- The plane flies across, never at the X; the wind blows across the line from the door to the X: nothing carries you there.

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
- Blind moves: `m` the stick anywhere, `c` let go, `u` stick forward, `t` hold tuck a while, `o` tap CHUTE, `f` hold flare.
- Why they lose: the X is 360-640 m from the door and off the plane's line; without steering a canopy flies straight on its
  heading at 14 m/s and lands hundreds of metres away; hard landings lose from level 4 (and levels 1-2 only flare for you).
- Proved in the simulator: 0 of 864 blind strings, 0 of 120 random.

## Lessons of the first three levels
1. **FLY YOUR BODY:** fly through three gold rings in freefall (the fall is endless while learning).
2. **OPEN BETWEEN THE MARKS:** tap CHUTE between the yellow marks twice (too high or too low says which).
3. **FLARE:** under the canopy, facing the wind, hold FLARE when the ring turns green; two soft landings.

## Research (I cannot play other games; I read about them)
- **Skydiving Fever** (App Store): tilt to move in freefall, touch the pack to open, land on the zone; a skydiver's review
  says the controls were not good enough and that under the canopy you should be able to flare
  (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/skydiving-fever/id713590692). So: no tilt; a stick, and a real flare that decides the landing.
- **Skydive Sim** (Steam): freefall turns and tracking, canopy physics, and practising canopy landings again and again
  without restarting (https://store.steampowered.com/app/1410000). So: tracking forward, brakes, and a lesson and a Scenic
  mode that repeat landings back to back.
- **SkyDive Adventure** (UNB round-up): an obstacle course with markers that make checkpoints easy to see, simple controls
  (https://unb.com.bd/category/Tech/top-skydiving-games-for-android-ios-enjoy-parachute-jumping-on-smartphone/76710). So:
  bright gold rings, the count at the top, ring courses.
- **Sky-Diving** (App Store): rings for points and a landing area that multiplies the score
  (https://apps.apple.com/fi/app/sky-diving/id1580582353). So: rings are optional points; the X's inner rings score more.
- **Go! Sports Skydiving** (GamesRadar): pretty, but controls too touchy and little to unlock
  (https://www.gamesradar.com/go-sports-skydiving-ps3-network-review). So: gentle turn rates with lag, and canopies, the kit
  room upgrades and ranks to unlock.
- **Skydiving Challenge** (Pocket Gamer): too simplistic at heart, then hurdles piled on
  (https://www.pocketgamer.com/skydiving-challenge/review). So: the depth is in the wind and the landing, not in obstacles.

## His directions, point by point
- View: 3D landscapes (countryside, islands, mountains, city), camera behind, horizon level, the X marked on screen. Kept.
- Controls: drag to steer, hold TUCK, a large CHUTE button; under canopy drag to turn, hold FLARE near the ground. Kept.
- Basic round: jump, optional rings, open, land near the centre. Kept.
- First jump: lessons with generous rings, the yellow deploy marks, automatic emergency opening (the safety). Kept.
- Freefall feel: body pose shows (spread, tucked head-down, leaning into a turn) and changes speed and direction. Kept.
- Choices: rings off the direct route, worth points but costing height and distance. Kept.
- Parachute flight: a satisfying slowdown on opening (shake, whump); the wind shown; plan the approach. Kept.
- Landing: reward lining up into the wind, slow approach (brakes), timing the flare; the ground ring, with a switch to turn
  it off (Settings). Kept.
- Progression: large zones first; smaller targets, crosswinds, rooftops, ring courses. Kept.
- Fair challenges: wind changes signalled by the windsocks, flags and the on-screen arrow and a "turns below" note. Kept.
- Sound: plane engine, rushing wind, a strong opening sound, quiet wind under the canopy; light music. Kept.
- Vibration: pulses on opening and touchdown (and rings); vibration and camera shake both switchable. Kept.
- Scoring and retries: accuracy, touchdown, rings; the path shown after a miss; JUMP AGAIN at once. Kept.
- Replay: precision (stars), ring courses, Scenic mode. Kept.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK)
P3 honest near-miss (every landing says its metres; a missed ring says by how much), P5 teach by doing (three short lessons),
P13 visible personal best, P20 gentle palettes (morning fields, blue islands, golden valleys). Sound rules S1-S11.
Longevity L1-L10: drag and you fly in the first second; a stick and two buttons; the ring on the ground makes every landing
readable; the X always marked; metres and score after each jump; places, winds, rooftops and courses change the plan;
rings or not, high or low opening; JUMP AGAIN in a tap; canopies by stars and kit-room upgrades; best per level.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
The canopy snapping open with the land rushing up, then the last seconds: the ring turning green, the flare, a soft landing
on the X.
