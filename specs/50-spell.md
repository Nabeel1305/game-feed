# 50. Spellshop

## The idea (his, 19 Nov 2026, set 9)
"Play as a real magician whom people visit with different problems and needs. You choose and perform spells or create potions
to help them... understand symbolism, select ingredients and perform the magic correctly... a large collection of diverse
requests... Some customers return; if you got their spell wrong, they complain and you fail the level." With his tips: clues in
the story, consistent symbols, hands-on preparation, several solutions, returning customers, funny recoverable mistakes,
stranger problems and rarer ingredients over time. 2D on purpose: words, jars and runes read best flat.

## How it plays (one thumb)
A customer tells their trouble; the clue words are in bold. Every jar always means one thing (16 jars: rose love, moonwater
sleep, thistle courage, gold luck, mint health, salt protection, ash forgetting, sage clear thinking, honey friendship, feather
travel, iron strength, spider silk keeping, acorn growth, raindrop rain and tears, mirror truth, ember warmth). Tap the jars,
BREW, then by hand: hold to pour to the line they asked for (a little, usual, strong; overfill and it fizzes over), drag round
the cauldron to stir (clockwise draws it IN, anticlockwise drives it OUT), tap the rune for what it acts on (heart, mind, body,
home, fortune, the road) and trace its dots, and tap SEAL when the brew glows the colour of the ring.
Perfect or good: they leave happy (some troubles have a second jar that also works, with a different result). A little wrong:
they come back after the next customer with a funny side effect that tells you what went wrong (the wrong jar's own effect,
"it did the very opposite", "it worked on my goat") and you brew it again. Badly wrong, or a fix that fails: they complain to
the whole town and the day is lost. A day is won when everyone left happy and the spells averaged the town's pass mark.

## Levels
57 troubles, dealt per day without repeats. Jars arrive over the levels (6, then ash and sage, honey and feather, iron and
silk, acorn and rain, mirror and ember); runes from 3 to 6; IN and OUT from level 2, pouring from 2, the seal from 3, two-jar
spells from 4. More customers, a faster seal and a higher pass mark later. Help: a slower seal and a lower pass mark.

## Systems
The Grimoire (every jar mastered once it works in a good spell), returning customers with funny side effects and fixes, the
Workshop (Reading lamp makes clue words glow, Silver spoon, Hourglass), ranks from Apprentice to Archmage.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `j` (tap a random jar), `b` (the next step done anyhow: brew, pour, a random stir, a random rune, seal, retry).
- Random jars and runes make bad spells, which come back angry: blind 0 of 1,984, random 0 of 480. The first lesson asks
  for three spells in a row that are just right, so blind tapping cannot pass it by luck.

## Lessons
1. READ THE STORY: three spells just right. 2. IN OR OUT, AND HOW MUCH: two good ones. 3. SEAL IT: two good ones.

## Engagement factors chosen
P22 the Grimoire filling (mastery you can see); P3 honest feedback (the side effects say exactly what went wrong); P7 risk
(how strong, which second jar); P14 the near goal (customer k of n, who is coming back); P10 sound (bell, clinks, swirls).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: a story, a shelf of jars, a cauldron: everyone knows potions.
- L2: tap, hold, stir, trace: each step is its own small pleasure.
- L3: the side effects show what was wrong; nothing is luck.
- L4: customer k of n, who is coming back.
- L5: your best day's average on each level.
- L6: 57 troubles, 16 jars, 6 runes, two-jar spells, stranger troubles later.
- L7: a second jar that also works, but differently; how strong.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the Grimoire, the Workshop.
- L10: your best day on the home card.

## The card clip
A trouble in bold, two jars in, a clockwise stir, the heart rune traced, SEAL, and a burst of gold: PERFECT.
