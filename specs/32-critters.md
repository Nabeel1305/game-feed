# 32. Critter Clash

## The idea (his words, then mine)
Set 5, mine: the light game of the set (intensity 2), one thumb, 3D (a flowery meadow, round little critters that bounce and
squash). Turn by turn, no clock: thinking is the game, plus one timing tap on every attack.

## How it plays (one thumb, taps only)
- Your team of critters against a trainer's team. **Your turn:** ATTACK, GUARD, or tap a critter on your bench to send it in.
- **ATTACK:** a gold ring appears on the foe and, after a short wait (never the same), a white ring closes on it. Tap anywhere
  as they meet: CRITICAL (double). Too soon: it glances off. No tap: a weak hit.
- **Types:** fire beats leaf and spark; water beats fire and stone; leaf beats water and stone; spark beats water and leaf;
  stone beats fire and spark. Double one way, half the other. The ATTACK button shows ×2 or ×½; a bench critter that beats the
  foe shows ×2.
- **The foe shows what it will do next:** attack, BIG ATTACK (guard it, or send in a critter it is weak against), guard (your
  hit does little: switch instead), switch, heal.
- **Wild critters** every fourth level: weaken it, then CATCH (a charm, the same timing tap). Each one caught joins your
  collection (three of each type) and makes that type stronger for good.

## The systems that carry over (set 4's rule)
- The **collection** (15 stars), the **critter shop** (training camp, sharp claws, hard shell, berry basket, lucky charm),
  **rank** (Rookie to Critter legend). Your critters grow with the levels; new ones join at levels 3, 10 and 20.
- Places change every five levels (meadow, beach, autumn wood, snowfield) with the weather.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever
One against one at level 1, two at level 2, three from 5. Foes get tougher, hit harder, bring big attacks more often, and
learn to switch to a counter, guard and heal; the timing ring gets quicker.

## Spamming cannot win
A blind thumb taps somewhere: the buttons are at the bottom, so most taps do nothing; while a ring closes, any tap is the
timing tap, but the ring starts after a random wait, so a steady beat cannot learn it, and a tap too soon glances off. Big
attacks unguarded do almost four times the damage. Blind strings 0 of 448, random 0 of 480.

## Lessons
1: two criticals in a row. 2: send in Puddlewump against fire: two super effective hits in a row. 3: guard two big attacks.

## Engagement factors chosen
P6 every input answers back (a pop, a chime, a shield's ring), P7 hit-stop on a critical, P12 celebrations scaled (critical,
super effective, caught!), P15 nested goals (this battle, the collection, the rank), P30 signature identity (the bouncing
critters and their type puffs).

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A critical, super effective hit: the white ring meets the gold, a fireball, the foe flashes and faints.
