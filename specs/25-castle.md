# 25. Castle Siege

## The idea (his words, then mine)
My idea for set 4, under his rule of 17 Nov 2026 (evening): "make the games more... like how Pirates is kind of complicated
and has numerous things to it... make the controls easy." A tower defence: medium intensity (3), one thumb, all taps. 3D,
because the valley in front of the walls is the game: you look down it from your castle, the road winding out of the forest
to your gate, the weather and light changing from siege to siege.

## How it plays (one thumb: taps only)
- **Build:** the stone pads by the road glow gold. Tap one: four towers appear above it, with their price. Tap one to build.
  - **Archers:** quick, hit fliers, bounce off plate armour, cannot touch ghosts.
  - **Catapult:** slow, a stone that splashes a crowd and breaks armour; cannot hit fliers.
  - **Mage:** magic bolts that go through armour and hit fliers and ghosts; dearer.
  - **Frost:** slows everything near it (not ogres or giants), a little cold damage.
- **Upgrade or sell:** tap a tower: upgrade it (three ranks, taller and trimmed with gold) or sell it for 60%.
- **The meteor:** tap METEOR, then the road: fire falls where they are thickest. It comes back in 30 s.
- **The waves:** top left shows what the NEXT wave brings (icons). Between waves, CALL NOW brings it at once, for gold.
- Every foe that falls pays gold. Foes that reach the gate strike it; when the gate falls, the siege is lost.
- The foes: goblins (quick), soldiers, knights (plate), bats (fly), ogres (huge, cannot be slowed), shamans (heal those near
  them), ghosts (only magic), battering rams (plate, hit the gate hard), and on every tenth level a boss: the DRAGON in the
  air, and on every twentieth the GIANT.
- **Eight roads:** from level 4 the enemy comes by one of eight roads across the valley; the pads stand on a grid, so the
  good pads are different every siege.

## The systems that carry over (set 4)
- **Crowns and royal upgrades:** every siege pays crowns (a lost one a few for the waves held). Six royal upgrades bought on
  the end card stay for ever: treasury (more gold to start), steel arrowheads, heavier stones, arcane focus (each a tower kind
  hits harder), fire priests (the meteor sooner), stone walls (the gate takes more).
- **Rank:** the total crowns ever earned: Squire, Knight, Baron, Earl, Duke, King.
- **Stars:** three when almost nothing reaches the gate; best stars per level kept.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever
More waves (4 to 7), stronger and more foes in each, new kinds arriving (bats at 2, knights 3, ogres 5, shamans 7, rams 9,
ghosts 12), a shorter rest between waves, bosses on every tenth. More gold to start as levels go up. Help after three losses
(more gold, weaker foes). Robots: ordinary all of 1 to 5, about 8 in 10 of 10, half of 20; sharp about 4 in 10 of 40.

## Spamming cannot win
Blind moves: build any tower on any pad, upgrade or sell any tower, call the wave, drop the meteor anywhere. First found:
with one road and plenty of gold, a thumb building anything anywhere held 45% of sieges. Now: the pads are a grid over the
whole valley (about two in five reach no road at all), the gold is tight (two towers to start), the gate takes only twelve
blows, waves mix kinds that need different towers (no more than about half fliers), the call is only between waves and pays
less. Lesson 1 is only passed by holding both waves with at most one goblin through.

## Lessons
1: build archers by the road and hold two goblin waves. 2: the right tower (bats and knights). 3: upgrade, and the meteor.

## Engagement factors chosen
P6 every input answers back (stone thud and dust when a tower goes up, the tower swells on an upgrade), P12 celebrations
scaled to the achievement (a coin for a goblin, SLAUGHTER for a run, the screen shakes when a boss falls, a fanfare when the
siege lifts), P15 nested goals (this wave, this siege, three stars, the next rank), P18 combos and streaks (falls in a quick
run, a note higher each), P26 variety with rhythm (eight roads, the weather, a boss every tenth). Beside them, set 4's
carry-over systems: royal upgrades and rank. Sound rules: the bowstring, the catapult's creak and thud, the mage's shimmer;
war horns for a wave, the gate's boom.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
The meteor falling on a packed road in front of upgraded towers, coins flying.
