# 78. Bus Driver

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His words (20 Nov 2026): "Bus driver: you pick people up on a designated route, must keep to time."

Mine: you drive a city bus along its route through a 3D town. The route is set, so the bus steers itself; your thumbs are the
pedals, GO and BRAKE. The skill is the driving a bus driver is proud of: pulling up with the front door exactly on the stop's
mark, leaving on the minute (never early), stopping for red lights, people on zebra crossings and the car in front, and doing
it all so smoothly that the passengers' tea stays in its cup. Calm (intensity 3), with a few decisions: wait for someone
running for the bus, or keep to time?

## Research (20 Nov 2026; I cannot play these myself, so I studied reviews, guides and store pages)
- Bus Simulator 21 and Bus Simulator Ultimate: points for keeping to the timetable, obeying lights, using indicators and
  kneeling the bus; points off for speeding, potholes, hitting kerbs and stopping badly at stops; fares are collected as
  people board. (store pages, reviews)
- City Bus Simulator guides: do not skip stops, and stop exactly at them. (players' guides)
- Bus Arrival and the timetable games: fares are collected automatically, and new districts open as you progress.
- What I took: pedals instead of a wheel (the route is set, so the thumbs are free for what matters), a stop meter that shows
  where your door is against the mark, a timetable you must not beat as well as not miss, a comfort score shown as a cup of
  tea, people who walk up and climb aboard, and districts that open in turn.

## How it plays (one screen, two thumbs)
- Hold the right half: GO. Hold the left half: BRAKE. Both ease in over about half a second, so a tap is gentle and a hold is
  hard. Let go and the bus coasts. Arrow keys or W/S on a computer.
- Stops: a yellow box and a mark on the road, a shelter with people waiting. A meter at the top shows your front door against
  the mark: green is close enough, gold is PERFECT (a bonus). Stopped there, the doors open by themselves: people step off,
  then on (a card-reader beep each, climbing the scale). When all are on: leave at the stop's time (shown, counting down);
  leaving before it counts as early. Sometimes someone runs for the bus: wait for them and they tip you.
- Red lights (the light ahead is shown at the side), zebra crossings (people step out as you come), the car in front, speed
  limit zones with cameras. Corners need slowing for: too fast and the tea spills.
- Lose the shift: a crash (the car in front, people on a crossing), two red lights, driving past a stop, or reaching the depot
  later than allowed.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever (LESSONS rule 0)
- Level 1: three stops and the depot, no lights. Lights from level 2, zebras from 3, cars in front from 4, speed zones from 5;
  more of each, more stops (up to six) and a tighter timetable as levels go on; every fifth route is rush hour (one more stop,
  a tighter timetable). Six districts in turn (Harbour Town, Downtown, Old Town, Rainy Ridge, Night Line, Sunset Coast).
- Three losses on a route: a kinder timetable (help).

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1)
- Blind moves (BLIND in part2): `g` GO down/up, `b` BRAKE down/up, `n` both off.
- A thumb that does not look drives past stops (the shift ends), runs red lights, or never gets there: 0 of 6,656 blind games
  won in the balancing runs.

## Lessons of the first three levels
1. GO, BRAKE, STOP ON THE MARK: serve 2 stops.
2. KEEP TO TIME: leave 2 stops on their time (an early departure starts the count again).
3. RED LIGHTS AND ZEBRAS: get past 3 lights or crossings safely (a red light or a crossing driven through starts again).

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK: 3 to 5 principles, the sound rules)
- P6 Every input answers back: the pedals fill as you press, the engine revs, the air brakes hiss, the bus dips and rolls.
- P14 Goal-gradient bar: the stop meter closing on the mark, the countdown to the time to leave.
- P13 Visible personal best: your best coins on each route.
- P21 Tidy-up satisfaction: the perfect stop, everyone aboard, the bell, on time.
- P26 Variety with rhythm: six districts with their own weather and light; lights, zebras, cars and zones arrive in turn.
- Sound: a diesel hum that rises with the revs, air brakes, the doors' sigh, a beep a step higher for each passenger (S5), the
  "ding ding" bell, the city's murmur, rain on the roof.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1 Two pedals and a mark on the road: understood at the first stop.
- L2 The bus pitches, rolls and hisses; the pedals ease in, so it feels heavy and real.
- L3 Every loss names its cause (the red light, the crossing, the stop you passed, how late).
- L4 The next stop, its time and the meter are always on screen; the next route; the next upgrade.
- L5 Stops on time, perfect stops, the tea level and your best coins per route.
- L6 Lights, zebras, cars, speed zones, rush hours and districts change what you watch for.
- L7 Wait for the runner or keep to time; how hard to brake (time against tea).
- L8 DRIVE AGAIN is one tap.
- L9 Liveries (won with stars), rank, coins and upgrades (engine, brakes, springs, seats) stay.
- L10 Your best on every route to beat.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
The bus gliding up to a stop and stopping on the mark (PERFECT STOP), the doors opening and people climbing aboard.
