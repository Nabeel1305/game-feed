# 72. Word Wheel

## The idea (set 13: mine, the calm one)
A wheel of letters and a little crossword: slide your thumb through the letters to spell the words that fill it. Like
Wordscapes, one of the most played calm games on phones, because finding a word is a small "aha" every few seconds, with
no clock. 2D on purpose: a crossword and a wheel read best flat, on a painted landscape.

## How it plays (one thumb)
Put your thumb on a letter, slide through others, lift: that is the word you try. Sliding back onto the letter before
takes the last one off.
- A word in the crossword fills in (tiles pop, a warm chord); quick words in a row make a run (NICE! GREAT!).
- Any other real word is a **bonus word**: a coin, and it goes in your book; ten fill the **jar** (+20 coins).
- A made-up word spills a **drop of ink**; out of ink, the page starts again. A found word gives a drop back. Bonus words
  and words already found cost nothing.
- **💡** shows one hidden letter (25 coins); a word fully shown counts as found. **⟳** turns the wheel round (free).
- The long word that uses every letter is always in the crossword.

## Levels
4 letters (levels 1-3), 5 letters (4-12), 6 letters (13-35), then 7. More words to find (4 rising to about 7-8), four-letter
words at least on bigger pages, rarer words from level 25, and less ink (5, then 4 from level 15, 3 from level 40). Every
tenth page is a big one. Five places (Meadow, Seaside, Autumn Woods, Mountain Lake, Starry Night). Help after three losses:
two more drops of ink. A first gift of 100 coins.

## Systems
The crossword, the wheel, runs of quick words, bonus words, the jar, the book of bonus words, ink, hints, shuffles, coins,
best score per level, stars (win; no hints; no spills), the Word Shop (Bigger inkwell, Bright bulb, Small jar), four tile
colours opened by stars, ranks from Reader to Lexicon. Words: the SCOWL lists (everyday words for the crossword, more for
bonus words), rude words removed.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `s` slide through some letters at random; `h` tap the bulb; `f` turn the wheel.
- Random letters are rarely words: a blind thumb spills its ink long before the crossword is full, and hints cost coins.
  Measured: 0 of 2,160 beat patterns and 0 of 700 random runs win.
- Lessons: ink runs out the same way (the lesson page starts again), and only two hints are free.

## Lessons
1. SLIDE TO SPELL: HEART, with EARTH, HATE, TEAR, HAT.
2. STUCK? A HINT: STONE, with NOTES, TONE, NEST, ONE, SET; two free hints.
3. BONUS WORDS: PLANE; find one bonus word and fill the crossword.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P19 no clock (only the ink, generously), P21 tidy-up satisfaction (tiles popping in, the full page), P20 gentle palettes (the
five painted places), P15 nested goals (this word, this page, the jar, the book), P5 teach by doing (the lessons).

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: one look: letters in a circle, empty squares; slide.
- L2: each letter plays a note as you pass it; the found word rings.
- L3: a lost page is only made-up words; the ink drops are always on show.
- L4: the empty squares left; the jar filling.
- L5: best score per level; "so close" when nearly done.
- L6: longer words, rarer words, bonus words, five places.
- L7: a hint now or think a bit longer; turn the wheel to see new words.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: your book of bonus words, your tiles, the Word Shop, your rank.
- L10: your best score on every page.

## Sound (S1-S11)
A soft marimba note for each letter, rising along the word; a warm chord for a found word (fuller for long words and runs);
a twinkle for a bonus word; a soft blop for a made-up one; a gentle arpeggio for a full page.

## The card clip
The long word swiped in one smooth curve, its tiles popping across the crossword, THE LONG ONE!
