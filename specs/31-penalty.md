# 31. Penalty Kings

## The idea (his words, then mine)
Set 5, mine: football (his list has none), intensity 3, one thumb, 3D: a floodlit stadium, the net bulging, the crowd jumping.
A shootout is two games in one, shooting and saving, and both are about reading a person.

## How it plays (one thumb, taps only)
- **Your kick** (the camera behind your striker): **tap the goal** where you want to shoot, then **tap again** while the needle
  on the power bar is in the **green**. The green is narrower for a corner. Green: the ball goes where you aimed. Just outside
  (yellow): it wanders and is slow. Red: it flies over the bar or is scuffed at the keeper.
- **Read the keeper:** before you aim he leans to the side he means to dive to. Shoot the other way. Some keepers bluff (more
  as the levels go on), and every keeper remembers: shoot the same way twice and he is waiting there. Top corners beat any
  keeper ("TOP BINS!") but are the hardest to hit.
- **Their kick** (the camera behind your goal, the kicker running in at you): when he **plants his foot**, his body turns to
  where he will shoot. Tap that side of the screen to dive, the middle to stay. Go before the plant and he sees you and puts
  it the other way. Go too late and it is past you. Some kickers stutter in their run-up to draw you early; some chip it down
  the middle.
- **Five kicks each, then sudden death.** It stops as soon as one side cannot catch up.

## The systems that carry over (set 4's rule)
- **The cups:** every level is a tie: Round of 32, Round of 16, quarter, semi, final; then the next cup (Village, County,
  National, Continental, World, and round again). Every tenth tie a boss team: better kickers and a bigger keeper.
- **Coins and the boot room:** golden boots (a wider green), ice in the veins (a slower needle), big gloves (more reach), spring
  heels (a faster dive, so you can wait longer), a scout report (each kicker's favourite side), a captain's armband (retake one
  failed kick a match).
- **Rank:** Sunday league, Academy, Pro, Star, Captain, Legend, Penalty King.
- **Stars:** goals plus saves in the first five kicks each.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever
Keepers who bluff more, remember more and reach further; a faster needle and a narrower green; kickers who hide their cue more,
punish an early dive more, hit the top corners more and shoot faster; stutters from level 3. On levels 1 to 5 an arrow on the
grass shows the kicker's cue at the plant; after that only his body.

## Spamming cannot win
A blind thumb taps somewhere on the screen: aiming, only a tap on the goal or close round it counts (18% of a phone screen,
measured); the needle stops wherever it is (the green is in a new place on every kick); a dive goes to whichever third was
tapped, and a tap while the kicker sets the ball or runs up is an early dive. Blind strings 0 of 448, random taps 0 of 480.

## Lessons
1: shoot (score twice in a row against a keeper who stays), then save (twice in a row; going early is punished). 2: read the
keeper's lean (twice in a row). 3: the stutter (two saves in a row).

## Engagement factors chosen
P4 fair, readable failure (TOO EARLY: HE SAW YOU GO, TOO LATE, OFF THE POST), P5 teach by doing (the lessons and the cue arrow
on early levels), P12 celebrations scaled (goal, TOP BINS, WHAT A SAVE, CUP WINNERS), P14 goal-gradient (the dots of the
shootout), P30 signature identity (the net bulging and the whole stadium jumping). Sounds: the whistle, the boot, the net, the
post's clang, the gloves, a crowd that hushes during the run-up.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A top-corner goal off a clean green strike, the keeper diving the wrong way, the net bulging and the stands jumping.
