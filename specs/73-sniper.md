# 73. Sharpshooter

## The idea (set 13: mine)
A rooftop over a busy town square, a dart rifle, and a card: the thief wears a yellow shirt, a cap, a briefcase. Find him
in the crowd and put him to sleep before he gets away. Like Sniper 3D, one of the biggest phone hits, but with sleep darts
(no blood: he lies down and snores). The fun is in two halves: the search (reading a crowd against the card) and the shot
(the scope, the held breath, the lead and the wind). 3D on purpose: you look down into a real square from a real roof.

## How it plays (two thumbs)
- **Left thumb: drag** anywhere to move the sight (the sight follows the thumb; finer through the scope).
- **Right thumb: hold the FIRE pad**: the scope comes up and you hold your breath (the sight steadies for 3 seconds, then
  you gasp and it shakes); **let go** to fire. Both thumbs work at once: aim while holding.
- Darts take time to fly (under half a second): aim **ahead** of a walking man; the **wind** carries a dart sideways.
- The **card**: a shirt colour at first; then a hat; then a bag. Look-alikes share some of it, never all.
- A dart in a **passer-by** fails the job. A **miss** scatters the crowd and the thief runs for an exit (6 seconds). Three
  darts. When the clock runs out he walks off to a car.
- From level 15 a **bodyguard** in black walks beside him (a dart in a guard is no fail, but the square scatters);
  boss levels have three.

## Levels
More people (8 up to 30), more look-alikes, a longer card, faster walkers, more wind, a bigger square, less time (40 s down
to 26), more sway. Four places with their own light: Town Square (morning), Harbour (golden), Market (overcast), Night
Plaza (dusk, lamps lit). Help after three losses: more time and an extra dart.

## Systems
The card, the crowd, look-alikes, the scope, breath, wind, lead, bodyguards, the exits, darts, stars (win; one dart; half
the time left), best score per level, the Gun Shop (Steady hands, Long breath, Extra dart), four scopes opened by stars,
ranks from Rookie to Legend.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `d` drag some way; `h` hold FIRE; `f` let go.
- The sight starts on the rooftops; darts fired without looking land on the ground (the crowd scatters, the thief runs)
  or in passers-by. Measured: 0 of 2,400 beat patterns; 2 of 2,400 random runs.
- Lessons start again on a passer-by, a miss of all darts, or a getaway.

## Lessons
1. AIM AND FIRE: the thief stands still by the fountain.
2. READ THE CARD: five people standing; only one matches every clue.
3. AIM AHEAD: he walks across the square, and the wind blows.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P4 fair, readable failure (the card is always on screen; a wrong man is told at once), P7 hit-stop and the held breath (the
moment before the shot), P13 the best score in sight, P17 small surprises (bodyguards, the night plaza), P30 the signature:
the slow-flying dart and the thief lying down to snore.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: the card, the crowd, the FIRE pad: one look.
- L2: the scope snaps up under the thumb, the breath steadies it, the dart puffs out.
- L3: everyone's clothes are in plain sight; a wrong man is the card misread.
- L4: the clock and the darts at the top.
- L5: best score per level.
- L6: look-alikes, wind, walkers, bodyguards, four places.
- L7: shoot now while he is clear, or wait for the crowd to open? Lead him or wait till he stops?
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: your scopes, the Gun Shop, your rank.
- L10: your best score on every level.

## Sound (S1-S11)
A click and slide as the scope comes up; a soft "thup" for the dart; a boing and a rising chime when he falls asleep; a
sour note for a wrong man; a hubbub when the crowd scatters; a gasp when your breath runs out; ticks in the last 5 seconds.

## The card clip
The scope sliding over the crowd, finding the yellow shirt and the cap, the breath bar draining, the dart, GOT HIM!
