# Taxi Driver, third version (0.3.0): a fare is a drive across town, and you see them get in and out

## His words (23 Nov 2026, 11:25)
"In the game Taxi Driver, you're literally taking them just around the corner. I mean, come on. You know, at least let the people drive
for at least for some time. I'm talking like 3 to 5 minutes at least, man. At least let them go some rounds. And you don't animate the
person entering the car, nor do you animate them leaving the car. You need to be more proactive. So fix Taxi Driver."

## What was wrong (the evidence)
- `makeCfg` in 0.2.0: `far: N <= 2 ? 1 : ...3.5`: on shifts 1 and 2 every passenger was going **one block**, later two to three and a
  half; a shift was two or three of those. He was right: round the corner.
- Getting in: the waving figure vanished and the meter started. Getting out: the fare vanished and "+13" appeared. Nobody was drawn
  doing either.

## Was, and now
| | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| How far a passenger goes | 1 block (shifts 1-2), 2 to 3.5 later | **7 to 10 blocks**: across town, with five or six corners to find |
| Fares in a shift | 2 or 3 | 4 on shift 1 (no lights, few cars: it is the quick one), 3 on shifts 2-5, then 4 |
| How far the next person waving is | next to you | one to three blocks on |
| How long a shift lasts | about 40 s to 1.5 min | **about 4 to 5.5 minutes** (the robots' driving, measured; see below) |
| The meter | set for a short hop | the same rule over the longer drive: time for the distance at an easy pace, a block's grace a block, and the same slack |
| Pay | 4 + blocks | the same rule, so a long fare pays a long fare's money (13 to 15 coins before the tip) |
| Getting in | they vanished | the taxi stops, the **back door on the kerb side swings open**, the person walks from the kerb to the door and gets in, the door shuts (two door sounds); you can see them **on the back seat through the glass** for the whole ride |
| Getting out | they vanished | the door opens, they climb out, wave, and **walk away across the pavement**; the door shuts |
| While that happens | | the taxi is held still for 1.8 s (in) and 1.9 s (out); the meter starts when the door has shut |
| The robot's GPS | always right | a careless robot sometimes misreads the map (people do, on a long drive) |

## Research (I cannot play other games; I studied how they are written about)
Kept from 0.2.0's research: Crazy Taxi's fares are graded by distance (its long fares are the ones people remember) and its passenger
runs to the cab and jumps in, then leaps out at the door; that small picture is what tells you the stop counted. Sources: `specs/83-taxi.md`.

## The same fault elsewhere ("one criticism, every game it fits")
| Game | Looked at | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Police Chase** | when his car is stopped the screen cut straight to the alley | **fixed (0.3.1)**: his door side: he is out and running, then your officer is out after him, before the picture changes |
| **On the Run** | DITCH THE CAR cut straight to the alley | **fixed (0.2.1)**: you are out of the driver's side and running before the picture changes |
| **Bus Driver** | people getting on walked to the door; people getting off were only a number ("GETTING OFF 1/2") | **fixed (0.1.2)**: each steps down from the front door and walks off up the street. Its route was looked at too: 3 to 6 stops, about 2 minutes for the robot, every stop a different job; not "round the corner", left alone |
| Fire Crew, Zenith, Santa's Round, Balloon Drift, the kart racers | nobody gets into or out of a vehicle in play | left alone |

## L1-L10 in a line each
L1 unchanged (wave, ring, arrow). L2 unchanged controls. L3 the meter is fair over the distance: measured below. L4 the next turn and
blocks to go are always on screen. L5 best shift and stars as before. L6 a long fare crosses lights, works and jams, so the route is a
decision. L7 GPS or round the jam matters more over ten blocks than over one. L8 retry as before. L9 taxis and upgrades as before.
L10 best coins on the card.
