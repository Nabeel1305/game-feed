# Tutorials that work (26 Nov 2026)

His words (26 Nov, 13:15): "make sure that the tutorials actually work... it tells me to swipe. I swipe up and then nothing happens, and then I
just skip the tutorials. Most of the time, honestly, I just skip the tutorials... take every game, understand how it's played, and make sure the
tutorial is correct for each game and is actually working for each game. And when a person does something, just make sure the tutorial is good
and informative." And: "after the tutorial, the first level should be the only one that is given, but after that it should actually engage the
players... by a certain level you need to have a skill of the game."

## How it is checked
`tools/test_lesson_touch.py [ids]`: for every lesson step, real touches doing exactly what the coach draws at that moment (gesture, direction,
place), read from the coach itself. Reports per step: done by touch / TRY AGAIN / the cue moved on / NOTHING. Results in out/lesson_touch/.
Where a lesson is long (Balloon), a headless "cue follower" (does whatever the cue says, a third of a second late) times it.

## The common faults found
1. One cue for a whole lesson that needs several moves (Balloon: HOLD TO GO UP for a lesson done by STOPPING in the ring).
2. Several moves in one cue ("SLIDE, TAP OFFER"): the hand shows one of them.
3. No place: the hand shows a tap in a little box, the thing to tap is somewhere else.
