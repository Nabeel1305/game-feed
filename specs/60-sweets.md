# 60. Sugar Swap

## The idea (set 11: his brief)
"Really simple, really engaging", in the style of the big hits; he named Candy Crush. A match-3 with everything that makes
that genre last: specials, combos, cascades, varied goals with a move limit, and a signature finish. 2D on purpose: a
match-3 board reads best flat, like the originals; the sweets are glossy and the board soft so it still feels tactile.

## How it plays (one thumb)
Swipe a sweet into its neighbour (or tap one, then the one beside it). Three or more of a colour in a line pop; sweets fall,
new ones drop in, and new lines pop by themselves (cascades, each one a step up the scale and a bigger word: Nice!, Yummy!,
Scrumptious!, Sugar storm!).
- Four in a line: a STRIPED sweet (clears its row or column when popped). An L or T: a WRAPPED sweet (a blast round it).
  Five in a line: a COLOUR BOMB (swap it with a sweet: every sweet of that colour goes).
- Two specials swapped together: stripe + stripe a cross; stripe + wrap three rows and three columns; wrap + wrap a huge
  blast; bomb + stripe every sweet of that colour turns striped and fires; bomb + bomb the whole board.
- A swap that makes no line swaps back. The first is free; another soon after costs a move, the one after that two, fading
  by one every twenty quiet seconds (shown by the moves: "Next miss costs 1 move"). A slip is cheap; spamming is not.
- Goals, each with a move limit: collect sweets of a colour; clear the JELLY under the sweets; bring the CHERRIES down and out
  of the bottom; break the FROSTING (pops beside it crack it); reach a score. Caged sweets cannot be moved until a pop frees
  them. Boards come in five shapes.
- Goal done with moves left: the SUGAR RUSH, every move left turns a sweet striped and fires it, then every special on the
  board goes off (tap to hurry it).

## Levels
Goals rotate (collect, jelly, cherries, frosting, score), five colours to start and six from level 6, board shapes from
level 4, cages from level 9, two-layer jelly and frosting later, two colours to collect from level 14; fewer spare moves as
levels rise. Four places (Lollipop Lane, Toffee Town, Mint Meadow, Candy Castle). Help after losses: four more moves.

## Systems
Matching, three specials, six special combos, cascades, five goals, blockers (frosting, cages, holes), the move limit and the
miss rule, the Sugar Rush, stars (moves to spare), hints after a pause, shuffles when stuck, the Sweet Shop (Extra moves,
Striped start, Quick hints), ranks from Sweet tooth to Sugar royalty, best score per level.

## Controls
Swipe one sweet towards a neighbour, or tap two neighbours. Nothing else.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind move: `s`, a swipe somewhere in some direction.
- About nine swipes in ten make no line. With the miss rule a blind thumb loses its moves within seconds: blind 0 of 448,
  random 0 of 480. The first two levels are collect goals (a score goal could be reached by lucky cascades), and level 3's
  jelly is a wide strip at the bottom, not a small patch a lucky cascade can clear.
- In lessons a second quick miss starts the lesson board again, so luck cannot pass them.

## Lessons
1. MAKE A LINE: three lines. 2. FOUR IN A ROW: make a striped sweet (the board is set so one swipe makes exactly four), then
fire it. 3. THE JELLY: clear a strip of jelly.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P18 combos (cascades climb a note and grow the word); P12 celebrations scaled to the achievement (a pop, a special's chord,
a combo, GOAL!, the Sugar Rush); P14 the goal-gradient bar and the goal counters; P9 squash and stretch (sweets land and
squash, pops swell); P30 the signature: the Sugar Rush.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: swap two, make a line: understood in one move.
- L2: the swap slides, the pop bursts and sings, sweets fall and squash; every move answers.
- L3: everything is on the board; a hint shows a move after a pause; misses are free once and the price is shown.
- L4: the goal counters and the bar, moves left, the next level.
- L5: stars and best score per level; "so close" with the real percentage on a loss.
- L6: five goals, blockers, shapes, specials and combos keep changing what a good move is.
- L7: pop now, or set up a special; spend a move on the cherry or the jelly.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: the Sweet Shop and your rank.
- L10: your best score on each level.

## The card clip
A colour bomb swapped into a striped sweet: every red turns striped and fires, beams across the board, cascades, GOAL!, and
the Sugar Rush.
