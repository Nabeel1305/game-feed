# 99. Rock Climb

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His idea 22 (20 Nov 2026): "climb rock faces, choosing handholds and footholds, managing your grip and finding a route to the
top. Start with easy climbs and gradually introduce taller, more complex routes", with his directions
(specs/his-list-2026-11-20.md). Mine: a calm, one-thumb climb held upright (the phone is the shape of a rock face). Two lines
leave the same start: one wanders with big ledges to rest on, the other goes straighter on smaller holds (bonus coins). A rope
clips in at every bolt you pass and catches you if your grip runs out; three falls and you are done for the day.

**3D:** yes: the face in front of you is the game, and the land opening up below as you climb is the reward (his "sense of
height"). A sunny crag, then a forest cliff, sea cliffs and a mountain face; the sky changes from climb to climb.

## How it plays (one thumb, the phone upright)
- **Press on a hand and drag it.** The holds it can reach light up green, with a dashed ring for how far it reaches. Let go
  near a lit hold and it grabs it (no need to be precise: it snaps on). Let go on bare rock: a slap at nothing, which tires
  you, more each time (that is what stops a thumb that does not look).
- **Feet:** to level 5 they follow by themselves. From level 6 they are yours: they still scramble onto the nearest footholds
  that work when a hand moves, but dragging a foot onto a bigger hold takes weight off your arms. **🦶 Feet: AUTO** on the home
  card keeps them fully automatic (his "optional assisted mode").
- **Grip** (the bar): small holds and no feet drain it; jugs with good feet barely do; both hands on big holds with good feet
  fill it, and a big ledge fills it fast. Empty: GRIP GOING!, shaking arms, faster breathing and 1.3 seconds to get to a rest
  before you slip.
- **The holds read by their shape:** jugs (big round), ledges (long shelves), edges (thin), crimps (tiny), pockets (dark holes),
  slopers (smooth domes: only hold below your shoulders), side-pulls (upright: only from the side they face), small grey
  footholds (feet only). No hold ever fails at random.
- **Jumps** (from level 10): some holds glow orange, too far to reach. Drag a hand onto one and keep your finger there: you
  sink and load up while a bar fills; let go in the green to catch it.
- **👁** runs the camera up the whole route and back (the clock waits); drag the bare rock to look around (it comes back).

## Research (I cannot play other games; I read about them)
- **Cairn** (PC Gamer, Push Square, GamingBolt, 2026): limb-by-limb climbing; stamina shown on the body, limbs shake more and more
  when strained; pitons let you rest; an easier Explorer mode made reviewers' progress possible.
  https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/cairn-review/ , https://www.pushsquare.com/reviews/ps5/cairn ,
  https://gamingbolt.com/cairn-review-rise-from-ruin , https://www.dawn.com/news/1980987
  — so: shaking arms and quicker breathing are the warning, the rope at each bolt is our piton, and auto feet is our Explorer mode.
- **Crux** (App Store, Kotaku, Pocket Gamer App Army): the mobile climbing puzzler: plan the sequence of moves; each hold only
  lets you hang so long; a climber found that hold sizes "just mean different time limits".
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1522414390 , https://kotaku.com/mobile-game-crux-lets-you-rock-climb-without-going-to-t-1845024500 ,
  https://www.pocketgamer.com/crux-the-great-outdoors/app-army-assemble
  — so our holds differ in more than time: slopers need your shoulders above them, side-pulls need your body on their side,
  ledges rest you, and feet matter.
- **The Climb** (6DOF Reviews): a stamina bar per arm that drains faster the farther your hands are from your body.
  https://www.6dofreviews.com/reviews/the-climb-review — so a stretched position (arms near straight) drains faster and looks strained.
- **Free Climb, Handy Climber** (AppAdvice, App Store): drag a hand to a rock; ragdoll bodies end up in impossible positions.
  https://appadvice.com/review/quirky-app-of-the-day-move-up-the-mountain-one-handhold-at-a-time-in-free-climb
  — so the body is solved, not ragdolled: no splits, feet under you, hips below your hands; impossible holds never light up.
- **An itch.io climbing jam game's feedback**: the grab and the miss made the same "pop", so players could not tell if they had
  grabbed. https://itch.io/post/10670130 — so a grab is a slap or tap by hold kind, a miss is a scrape with words on screen.

## His directions, point by point
- *3D view from behind and slightly to the side; nearby holds and limbs visible; a short sunny face, then forest, coast,
  mountain*: yes, the camera sits behind and to the right; level 1 is a 9-metre sunny crag; then the forest cliff (6-12), sea
  cliffs (13-20), mountain face (21 on).
- *Follow smoothly without sudden rotations; drag empty space to look around; a route-preview button; camera separate from
  limbs*: the camera eases after you; a press that does not start on a hand or foot looks around instead; 👁 previews.
- *Drag a hand or foot onto a reachable hold; highlight the limb and suitable holds; snap on release*: exactly (a white ring on
  the limb you hold, green rings on the holds it can reach, snap within about a third of a metre).
- *The body shifts naturally; stable looks comfortable, stretched looks strained; reach indicators*: the hips settle where every
  limb reaches; straight arms when stretched; the dashed reach ring while dragging.
- *Early routes: hands only, feet automatic; manual feet gradually, with an assisted mode*: feet automatic to level 5; yours
  from 6 (helped: they never leave you stuck); Feet: AUTO on the home card.
- *A simple stamina meter; arms drain faster, good feet slow it; resting holds recover*: the grip bar; exactly.
- *Readable holds; ledges secure, edges tiring, some need an approach; no random failures*: the shapes and rules above.
- *Several paths: longer with rests, shorter with hard reaches; inspect first*: the left line and the right line; 👁 says so.
- *Traverses, overhangs, timed reaches gradually; jumps later with a preparation and release gesture*: traverses from 5,
  overhangs from 8 (feet hold less), jumps from 10 (hold to load, let go in the green).
- *Fair slipping with warning*: shaking, GRIP GOING!, breathing and 1.3 seconds to reach a rest.
- *A visible rope catching you after a short fall; resume from the latest checkpoint quickly; non-graphic*: the orange rope
  through every clipped bolt; a short drop, a bounce, and you are back at the last bolt with your grip refilled.
- *Landscape revealed gradually, distant scenery, wind, occasional downward views; strong effects optional*: the land below
  and around opens as you rise; wind grows with height; the only big camera move is 👁 and the view from the top.
- *Quiet music, birds, wind, shoes on rock, breathing, equipment sounds*: all (gulls at the sea cliffs; a quickdraw's clack
  when you clip in; breathing quickens on a low grip and goes quiet at rest).

## Levels, spam, lessons
Levels: easy first (short faces, jugs, auto feet), harder for ever (taller, smaller holds, overhangs, jumps, faster drain).
Robots (node, 6-8 games each): ordinary and sloppy win all of levels 1-6; at level 40 ordinary about 0.5, sharp about 0.7.
Blind moves (BLIND in part2: a hand pushed straight up, any limb dragged anywhere, a foot up, a jump let go at a random moment,
the route preview): 0 of 640 strings and 0 of 120 random runs in node. A slap at bare rock costs grip, more each time, so a
blind thumb is soon out of grip and falls three times.
Lessons: 1 REACH (three GOLD holds in a row; any other move starts the count again), 2 REST (both hands on the ledge until the
bar is full), 3 THE ROPE (climb past two bolts).

## Engagement factors chosen
P4 the near goal always in sight (the next lit hold, the next bolt, the top ledge), P6 every input answers back (a grab sound
per hold, chalk puffs, the clack of the clip), P13 visible personal best (best time and coins per level), P17 a calm game with
tension that comes and goes (rest, push, rest). Sound rules S1-S11.
Longevity L1-L10: three short lessons; one thumb, drag and let go; falls explained (where your grip went); the top in sight;
height, time, moves and rests after every climb; places, overhangs, jumps; the hard line or the restful one; Play it again at
once; kits and the kit bag (chalk, shoes, forearms); best time per level.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A jump to an orange hold: the climber sinks, the green bar, the catch, chalk bursting, the land far below.
