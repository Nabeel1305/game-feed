# 69. Helix Drop

## The idea (set 12: mine)
A bouncing ball on top of a tall tower of round platforms, each with a gap. Turn the tower with your thumb so the gap comes
under the ball and it drops a floor; red pieces end the run. One of the most played kinds of phone game there is, because
it is understood in a second and every drop feels good. 3D on purpose: the tower turning in front of you, the ball falling
down it, is the whole game.

## How it plays (one thumb)
Slide left or right anywhere: the tower turns with your thumb. The ball bounces on the platform it is on; turn the gap under
it and it drops through. Reach the gold platform at the bottom.
- **Red pieces**: land on one and the run ends.
- **Drop streak**: each platform dropped in a row scores more (10, 20, 30...) and plays a note higher.
- **Fire**: three drops in a row without a bounce and the ball burns; the next platform smashes, red or not.
- **Moving platforms** (from level 6) turn by themselves; **sliding reds** (from level 10) walk round their platform.
- **Timing**: turning while the ball is high is safe; a red slid under a falling ball is not. The ball falls faster the
  further it drops, so a long chain asks you to be ready for the platform below.

## Levels
Taller towers (20 platforms up to about 42), more red, narrower gaps, platforms that turn, reds that slide. Every tenth is a
Boss tower (a third taller). Four places with their own colours (Candy Tower, Ocean Spire, Lava Column, Sky Needle). Help
after three losses: less red, wider gaps and a free shield.

## Systems
Score and best score per level, the drop streak and fire, smashes, stars (win; a 5-drop streak; few bounces), coins, the Ball
Shop (Shield, Quick fire, Smooth turn), four balls opened by stars (Your balls), ranks.

## Spamming cannot win
- Blind moves: `l` / `r` slide some way left or right without looking.
- Turning blind puts red under the ball as often as a gap; with 20 or more platforms a blind thumb never reaches the bottom.
  Measured: blind strings 0 of 1,984, random moves 0 of 480.
- Lessons need what blind turning does not do: reach the bottom (red ends the lesson tower and it starts again), and
  catch fire and smash a platform.

## Lessons
1. TURN AND DROP: a little tower of 10, one-piece gaps.
2. CATCH FIRE: drop 3 in a row and smash one.
3. MOVING PLATFORMS: the platforms turn by themselves.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK numbers)
P18 combos and streaks (the drop streak climbing the scale, fire); P9 squash and stretch (the ball); P14 the goal-gradient
bar (this level to the next); P13 the best score always shown; P30 the signature: the fire smash.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: one look and you know: a ball, a gap, red is bad.
- L2: the tower follows the thumb exactly; the bop, the whoosh, the smash.
- L3: every red is in plain sight before the ball gets there; a loss is a red you turned under it.
- L4: the bar to the next level at the top.
- L5: best score per level, NEW BEST as you pass it.
- L6: moving platforms, sliding reds, boss towers, four places.
- L7: drop now for the streak, or wait a bounce for a safer gap.
- L8: Try again is one tap.
- L9: your balls, the Ball Shop, your rank.
- L10: your best score on every level, gold when you beat it.

## Sound (S1-S11)
A rubbery bop for each bounce (a little different each time), a whoosh and a note for each drop, a step higher for each in a
row; a roar for fire; a crash and a chord for a smash; a low bonk on red; a fanfare at the bottom.

## The card clip
The ball on fire falling through three platforms in a row, then SMASH, with the pieces flying.
