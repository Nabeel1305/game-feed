# 101. Balloon Drift (mine)

## The idea
Mine, for set 18 (the set has two intense sports games, a calm climb and a calm clinic): a calm, one-thumb flying game built on
the real sport of balloon competitions. A hot air balloon has no steering; the wind blows different ways at different heights,
so you steer by climbing or sinking into the wind that goes your way. Each flight: drop a marker on each X on the ground
(closer is more points), then land softly near the landing field. Intensity 2, one thumb, upright.

**3D:** yes: the world below and ahead (the crosses, the field, the wires, the fields and farms, other balloons far off) is
the game, and the view from up there is the pleasure of it.

## How it plays (one thumb)
- **Hold anywhere** to fire the burner: the envelope warms and the balloon rises, a little late. **Let go** and it cools and
  sinks. **Flick down** to vent (sink faster). Hold it level with short burns.
- **The wind bar** on the right shows the wind at every height as an arrow, turned to your view (straight up the screen is
  towards the next X). Your height is the dot; the arrow at your height glows.
- **🎯 Drop** a marker when the yellow ring on the ground (where it would land, worked out with the winds it falls through) is on
  the X. The ring turns green inside 10 m. Points: 1000 at the centre, fewer further out, none past 120 m.
- **Land** once the X are done: sink slowly, a short burn before the ground. Close to the field is up to 1000 more points; a
  hard landing is a bump (three end the flight).
- **Fuel** (the bar) runs out if you burn without thinking; **wires** (red balls) are low and end the flight if you touch them;
  the morning ends (a countdown) and then it is time to land.

## Research (I cannot play other games; I read about them)
- **FAI ballooning commission, balloon competition explainers** (fai.org, abf.net.au, balloonaloft.com, sibf.jp): balloons
  cannot be steered; pilots read the winds at different heights and climb or sink into the one that carries them; tasks are
  marker drops on crosses, scored by distance; choosing a target and timing the drop is the skill.
  https://www-2025.fai.org/node/24330 , https://www.abf.net.au/how-competition-works ,
  https://balloonaloft.com/blog/ballooning-competitions-what-are-they-and-how-do-they-work/ , https://www.sibf.jp/en/balloon/
  — so the whole game is those two things: the right height, and the moment to drop.
- **Hot Balloon Race** (Pocket Gamer): a one-button burner; "bouncing along on gusts of momentum" feels good.
  https://www.pocketgamer.com/hot-balloon-race/review — so one thumb is the whole control, with a little lag that makes it feel
  like a balloon.
- **Small balloon games on itch.io** (player comments): players missed that you steer by height until a flag showed the wind
  where they were; slow controls felt right "without getting annoying". https://itch.io/post/448273 , https://itch.io/post/448022 ,
  https://itch.io/post/2147195 — so the wind bar shows every height at once and glows at yours, a lesson teaches steering by
  height, and the burner answers within a second or two (not twenty).
- **Endlessly side-scrolling Balloon Simulator** (store page): burner and vent; the higher, the stronger the wind.
  https://rawg.io/games/endlessly-side-scrolling-balloon-simulator-2024 — so a vent (flick down), and stronger winds higher up.

## Levels, spam, lessons
Levels: 1 one X and two winds, 2 two X and the landing field, 3 a third wind, 4 power lines, 6 three X, 7 winds that turn as
the morning goes on, 9 warm air rising over some fields; places: farmland, vineyards, red canyon, alpine meadows, lakes at dusk.
Each X is placed by flying a plan through two different winds, so it can always be reached, but never by staying at one height.
Robots (node, 10 flights each): ordinary and sloppy win levels 1-3 (0.9-1.0); at 40 ordinary 0.6, sharp 0.5.
Blind moves (burner held a while, a quick blip, vent, drop, let go): 0 of 640 strings and 0 of 120 random runs in node.
Lessons: 1 BURN TO RISE (stop inside the gold ring, twice), 2 STEER BY HEIGHT (into the wind that blows to the flag, twice),
3 DROP ON THE X (within 40 m, twice).

## Engagement factors chosen
P3 honest near-miss (every marker shows its distance in metres), P12 celebrations scaled (points chime that rises with them,
BULLSEYE), P13 visible personal best, P20 gentle palettes (a morning sky, fields, dusk over the lakes). Sound rules S1-S11.
Longevity L1-L10: three lessons; one thumb; the ring and the wind bar make every miss readable; the next X always ahead;
points, distances, fuel after every flight; more winds, wires, turning winds, warm air; which wind, when to drop, when to land;
Next flight at once; balloon designs by stars, the kit bag (bigger tank, double burner, heavy markers); best per level.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
The ring sliding onto the X, the marker falling, BULLSEYE!, the balloon drifting on over the fields.
