# 35. Deep Bite

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His shark idea from his engagement notes (18 Nov 2026): eat the small fish, avoid the larger predators, "catch two more fish to
grow", a valuable fish swimming near a predator, an aquarium of every creature you have found. 3D: the sea is the game (light
rippling on the sand far below, kelp, coral, reef walls, sea snow), seen from above at an angle so you read the whole bay round you.

## How it plays (one screen, one thumb)
Put a thumb down anywhere and drag: the shark swims that way, faster the further you drag; all the way out is a dash (a blue
bar under the shark, it tires you and makes you hungrier). Swim into fish smaller than you (green rings) to eat them; fill the
bar and you grow, and the camera lifts to show more of the sea. Bigger fish wear red rings; the hunters among them notice you
(a "!" and a growl), then come for you (pulsing red, a heartbeat); off screen they show as red marks at the edge. Grow past a
hunter and it turns green: lunch. Hunger runs down all the time, so hiding does not work. Jellyfish sting and slow you; puffers
puff up (inedible) when you come slowly, so dash at them. A golden fish is worth three and always flees; it likes to keep close
to a hunter. Reach the size the level asks for to win. Places: the reef, the kelp forest, the open ocean, the deep (dark, a light
of your own), five levels each, round and round.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever (LESSONS rule 0)
Level 1: grow to size 3, no hunters. Then more hunters, faster and quicker to notice; more jellyfish; prey more skittish; the
size to reach goes 3 (1-7), 4 (8-15), 5 (16-23), 6 (24+). Fewer hunters while you are small. Help after three losses: slower hunger.
Robots: an ordinary player wins levels 1-5 every time, about 60% at 20, 30% at 40; a sharp one 40% at 40.

## Spamming cannot win (LESSONS rule 1): the wrong move costs, more each time; the right move needs one read
- Blind moves (BLIND in part2): `d`, a thumb that drags some way (near or far) without looking, or lets go.
- Fish dart aside when you swim straight at them, schools turn away as one, the golden fish always runs: a straight line
  misses, steering after a fish catches it. Hunger runs down faster than blind eating fills it. 448 blind-string games and
  480 random ones: none won.

## Lessons of the first three levels (each move, two or three times, before play)
1. EAT: five small fish, each soon after the last. 2. GET AWAY: a barracuda comes for you; dash until it gives up.
3. THE GOLDEN FISH: grab it from beside a hunter and get away.

## Engagement factors chosen (PLAYBOOK: 3 to 5 principles, the sound rules)
P4 fair, readable danger (red rings, "!", growl, heartbeat, edge marks before any bite); P14 the "N more to grow" bar; P13 the
best size here marked on the size pips; P18 bites in a row raise the snap a step ("FRENZY x3"); P12 small "+1"s, a big BIGGER!
flash, and the golden fish's sparkle. Sounds: a snap and gulp per bite (rising with the streak), a chime and a low swell to grow,
a growl and a heartbeat for danger, bubbles and the hum of the sea under everything; no buzz on a loss.

## Longevity (PLAYBOOK H)
- L1: drag and the shark goes there; green rings say "eat", red say "run"; one prompt ("drag anywhere to swim").
- L2: the shark banks into turns, beats its tail faster at speed, opens its jaws on each bite; every bite pops and snaps.
- L3: hunters are seen before they bite (ring, "!", growl, a mark at the edge), they give up after a few seconds of chase.
- L4: "3 more to grow" on screen, the size pips to the flag, three stars, the next place.
- L5: the camera lifts as you grow; your best size on this level is marked; the end card says how close you were.
- L6: schools, darting mackerel, puffers, jellyfish, turtles, the golden fish; four places; hunters that become prey.
- L7: the golden fish beside a hunter, worth three; dash now (fast, hungrier) or save it.
- L8: one tap of Try again and you are swimming.
- L9: the aquarium: every kind you have eaten, with how many; shadows for the ones still to find. Upgrades that stay.
- L10: your best size here is marked on the pips while you play.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
A size-3 shark dashing out from under a pulsing red barracuda with the golden fish in its jaws.
