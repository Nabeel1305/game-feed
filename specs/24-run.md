# 24. On the Run

## The idea (his words, then mine)
His list: the other side of Police Chase, "you're the one running from the cops". Intense (4), one thumb, 3D (the same lit
city as Police Chase, from the getaway car). Set 4's rule (17 Nov 2026, evening): deeper, with several parts and systems,
easy controls.

## How it plays (one thumb: swipes and a tap)
Each level is one job, in three phases:
1. **The getaway (drive).** Four lanes of night avenue. Swipe left or right to change lane, tap (or swipe up) for nitro.
   Traffic to weave through; cash bags and nitro cans in the lanes. The cops come up behind (more as the HEAT stars rise);
   one pulls alongside and FLASHES before it rams the lane it is aiming at: swipe away. Roadblocks leave one gap (the green
   arrow shows it early); spike strips from level 4; from level 6 a helicopter's spotlight sweeps the lanes and raises the heat.
2. **Ditch the car and run (foot).** Through the alleys, the cop on your heels (the bar at the bottom): swipe left and right
   round crates, up over fences, down under pipes. Coins on the way. A stumble lets him close in.
3. **The safehouse.** Payout: the cash, a clean-run bonus, stars (no crashes and at most one stumble for three).
Three crashes and you are wrecked; if he reaches you on foot you are caught (you keep a third of what is in your pockets).

## The systems that carry over (set 4)
- **The bank and the garage:** every job pays into the bank; six upgrades bought on the end card stay for ever: engine, armour
  (one more crash), nitro (more cans), feet (run faster), magnet (cash from the next lane), scanner (roadblocks seen earlier,
  cops' rams slower).
- **Rank:** the total ever earned: Pickpocket, Joyrider, Getaway driver, Wheelman, Ghost, Legend.
- **Heat:** within a job, one to five stars, set by time and the spotlight; more heat, more cops, bigger bags.

## Levels: very easy first, then harder for ever
Longer drives, denser traffic, up to three cops, shorter ram warnings, more roadblocks and spikes, the helicopter, a faster
cop on foot with a smaller lead, more obstacles; every tenth job is a big score (longer). Robots: ordinary all of 1 to 3, 9 in
10 of 5, 6 to 8 in 10 of 10 to 40; sharp 4 in 10 of 40 and 2 in 10 of 80.

## Spamming cannot win
Blind moves: swipe left, right, up, down; tap. First found: blind swiping won 15% (random moves 59%) because traffic was thin
and the cop on foot slow. Now traffic is two to three times as dense (a lane picked blind meets a car every few seconds) and
on foot the cop is almost as fast as you with a lead of about ten metres, so three stumbles lose. Lesson 1 needs five bags in a
row without a crash, so blind swiping never gets through it.

## Lessons
1: change lane, five bags in a row without a crash; then nitro. 2: dodge two rams (swipe away from the flashing car).
3: two roadblocks through the gap; then on foot, clear six obstacles.

## Engagement factors chosen
P3 honest near-miss (THROUGH THE GAP; a ram dodged by a lane), P6 every input answers back (the swish, the lane change at
once, the nitro flame), P13 visible personal best (best haul per level, stars), P14 goal-gradient bar (the progress bar to
the drop, the cop's distance on foot), P18 combos and streaks (cars weaved in a row, cash a note higher with each bag).
Beside them, set 4's carry-over systems: the garage and rank. Sound rules: the siren follows the nearest cop.

## The card clip: its most exciting moment
Through a roadblock's gap at full nitro with the cops' lights behind.
