## What each game is

1. **Bottle Cap Flick**: Flick bottle caps to knock the other side's caps off a wooden stool. Capture line, legs at each end, a ladder of four computer levels from Beginner to Legendary (beat one to open the next), best of 1/3/5/7. APK repo: `Nabeel1305/bottle-cap-flick`.
2. **Knockout**: First-person two-thumb boxing. Tap to punch, flick to dodge, flick up for an uppercut, hold both to block. Endless levels, rounds and points decisions.
3. **High Noon**: Cowboy quick-draw duel. Thumb on the holster, wait for DRAW (watch for fake calls), slide up to aim, release to shoot. Headshots win; endless levels.
4. **Steel & Honour**: First-person medieval sword duels. Your blade follows your finger in any direction; tap to poke an exact spot. His cuts come from any angle and his thrusts aim at a spot: block by swiping across his blade on the side it comes from (or through the spot of a thrust). Perfect parries open him up. Endless levels, rounds, champion knights, huge variety of knights.
5. **Zenith**: Superhero rescue patrols over Brightwater City. Alert after alert: fly through gold rings, then catch falling people, zap meteors with laser eyes, punch bad guys, blow out fires, lift hanging buses, and dodge-and-zap giant monsters and super villains. Missions are mixed from long lists so they rarely repeat.
6. **Hippodrome**: Greek four-horse chariot racing seen from behind your own chariot. Two thumbs: left steers the reins, right taps the whip on the beat of the gallop or holds to rein in. Turning the post is the skill: rein in, hug the inside, whip out, and pass rivals by turning tighter than they do. Ram a rival who is level with you by swerving into them, dodge when a rough rival winds up to ram you, and break a chariot to put it out of the race; damage shows on the chariot. Live leaderboard, 4 to 8 chariots with Greek names, 12 venues from Olympia to Troy, a champion of legend every 10th race, endless levels.
7. **Bandit**: A one-on-one fighter-jet dogfight above the clouds, seen from just behind your own jet. Two thumbs: the left flies (push it where the nose should go; hard turns cost speed), the right fires and drops flares. Get behind the bandit and hold him in your ring to lock on, then fire a missile; up close, hold FIRE for the cannon. He weaves, drops flares, then hauls round and comes for you: a mirror shows him on your tail, and when his missile is in the air you turn hard and tap FLARE as the ring closes on the button. Bandits with their own call signs, jets and paint, an ace every 10th sortie, one to three bandits a sortie, stars for taking no hits, endless levels.
8. **Plunder**: A pirate game in two halves. You set sail on an expedition and steer your sloop from above until the lookout sights a ship or a fleet of up to three, merchant, navy or both: the card shows the loot and the danger, and you raid or sail on. Navy ships must be beaten with cannons first: their guns only reach out of their sides (shown in red before they fire); yours fire from the wheel when a ship is in your gold arc (going round and round hard over spoils the aim), ROW gives a short burst of speed until the crew tire, and a quick sloop can sit on a heavy ship's bow or stern. Then you come alongside and board (the first three boardings teach every move, slowly, and the fight starts only once you can do each one two or three times): first person, on deck, below deck and in the captain's cabin. Block his blow with one swipe across his blade, on the side it comes from, and strike while he is open; or attack first, hammering his sword until his guard breaks (but just after, he is set, and a blow then is turned and punished); pull down to dodge; one pistol ball at a time, slow to reload, three in all, and you drag the sights onto him. One bar is the tide of the fight: fight well and your crew takes the ship, fight badly and they break. Spamming cannot win, at sea or on deck, on any expedition: no repeated pattern of moves does, at any speed. The win screen counts out the loot. Calm shanty while cruising, fast with a drum once a sail is sighted. Expeditions get harder for ever; a treasure fleet every 10th; stars, best haul and pirate rank.
9. **Reel**: A fishing game in 3D, seen from the end of a wooden jetty on a mountain lake, with the weather and light changing by level (dawn, rain, mist, storm, sunset, night with a glowing float). Pull your thumb down and let go to cast to where fish are rising; leave the little nibbles and flick up to strike when the float is pulled right under (too soon scares them, too late loses the bait). Then fight it home: hold to reel, let go when it thrashes and runs or the line snaps, and drag against its way to turn it from the reeds and lily pads. Land the fish asked for before the light goes; three lines. The first three levels teach every move. Six kinds of fish plus rare golden carp and a named giant every tenth level; your biggest of each kind is kept.
10. **Wild Water**: A whitewater kayak game in 3D, seen from the cockpit with the bow in front and the paddle in your gloves. Your two thumbs are the two blades: swipe down on the left for a left stroke (it swings the bow right), on the right for a right stroke; swipe up to back-paddle. The current carries you down a winding river through rows of rocks with one gap each, past fallen trees on the outside of the bends, over ledges you must take straight through the smooth tongue, and between slalom gates for stars. Three smashes or rolls and the boat is done. Weather and light change by run. The first three runs teach every stroke.
11. **Soar**: The calm one: a 3D flight over a sea of islands, seen from just over your shoulder, arms forward and a scarf streaming, under a real sky that changes by flight (golden hour, dawn, mist, sunset, night with stars). One thumb steers like a little stick wherever you put it down: right to lean and turn, up to climb, down to dive. Follow a trail of golden rings; each ring is a bell note that climbs the scale while the chain lasts. Nothing hurts you: touch the sea or a hillside and you skim off it. Fly through enough rings to finish the flight; all of them for three stars.
12. **Vine**: Swing through a 3D rainforest gorge on vines, a river far below, light falling through mist, weather by canopy. Your thumb is your grip: keep it down to hold the vine and swing, lift it to let go as you swing forward and up (each vine needs its own moment), lean in the air by moving the thumb, and put it down to catch the next vine when your hand reaches it. Grab at air and the hand needs a moment; fall and you are back on the last vine, one of three lives. Reach the tree house before the light goes. The first three canopies teach every move.
13. **Hunter**: The intense one: an ice-age hunt in 3D, first person, crouched in tall grass on a steppe under snowy mountains, weather by hunt. Left thumb held creeps forward; lift it to freeze when the animal flicks its ears and looks up, or it sees you and bolts. Right thumb pulls back and lets go to throw a flint spear where the mark on the ground is: the chest brings it down, a haunch only wounds it, and far throws fall short. Sometimes a cave lion growls from one side: swipe toward it and tap the instant it leaps to brace your spear. Deer, horses, boar, bison, and a woolly mammoth every tenth hunt. A heartbeat that quickens as you close in.
14. **Police Chase**: A three-part police chase in 3D through a city at night. Pursuit: hold the left or right side of the screen to steer your patrol car (both to brake) through traffic, and ram the getaway car's back corner from the side to spin him out. On foot: chase him down a brick alley, swiping round crates, over fences and under pipes, and tap to tackle when you are right behind him. Arrest: when he twists one way, swipe the other way to hold him until the cuffs are on. Lights, siren, rain on the tarmac; weather changes by case.
15. **Pilot**: Land an airliner in 3D at a countryside airport, seen from just behind the plane, in weather that changes by flight (fog, rain, storm, sunset, night with the runway lights). Right thumb is the control column: left and right to bank, pull back to raise the nose; left thumb is the throttle.
